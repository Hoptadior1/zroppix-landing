Is Zroppix Safe?

What the extension collects, what it says it does not collect, and how you can check for yourself.

Checked against the privacy policy (last updated May 13, 2026) on October 18, 2026

Zroppix is a Chrome extension that reads the name, price and URL of the Amazon product pages you view. Its privacy policy says it does not collect your name, browsing history, purchase history, Amazon account information or financial information, and that it never sells your data.

What Zroppix collects

It collects

  • Product names from Amazon product pages you visit
  • Product prices from those pages
  • Product URLs from those pages
  • Your email, only if you set a price alert

It says it does not collect

  • Your name or personal information
  • Your browsing history
  • Your purchase history
  • Your Amazon account information
  • Any financial information
  • Anything from pages other than Amazon product pages

The policy says price data is used to build price history, generate price drop predictions and show trends, and that it is anonymous and not linked to any individual user. Your alert email is used only to send the alert you asked for.

Who handles the data

The policy lists three services used to operate Zroppix: Supabase for database storage, Vercel for website and API hosting, and Resend for sending price alert emails. It says Zroppix never sells data to third parties.

How long data is kept

Price data is kept to improve predictions. If you set a price alert, your email is kept until the alert is triggered or you unsubscribe. You can ask for an email address to be deleted at any time by writing to hello@zroppix.com.

How to check it yourself

  • Read the full privacy policy. It is short.
  • Open the extension's Chrome Web Store listing and read its privacy practices section.
  • Read the permissions Chrome shows in the install prompt before you accept.
  • Remove it any time: open chrome://extensions, find Zroppix and choose Remove.

Zroppix is a young extension and has only a small number of reviews on the Chrome Web Store so far. Do not rely on star ratings alone. Read the policy and decide for yourself. This page covers the extension and price alerts as described in the privacy policy.

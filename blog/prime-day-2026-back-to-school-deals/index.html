📚 Backpacks and Bags — Skip Prime Day for These

This is an important counter-point: not everything is worth buying at Prime Day. Backpacks are one category where Prime Day deals are often underwhelming. Name-brand backpacks (North Face, JanSport, Herschel) rarely see more than 15-20% Prime Day discounts, and the selection at the best prices tends to be limited colorways and older stock.

For backpacks, the better strategy is to shop in late July and August during traditional back to school sales at Target, REI, or direct from brand websites. REI's annual July sale typically includes 20-30% off North Face and other premium brands — comparable to or better than Prime Day pricing with better selection.

The Prime Day back to school categories worth buying are tech and consumable supplies. The categories not worth prioritizing at Prime Day are bags, clothing, and furniture — those have better deals elsewhere in the back to school season.

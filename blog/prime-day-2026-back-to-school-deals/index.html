🏠 Dorm Essentials and School Supplies

Amazon Basics dorm items (storage, small appliances, desk lighting, bedding) and name-brand school supplies (Sharpie, Paper Mate, Expo) are categories that historically see real discounts around major sale events, since these are commodity goods with transparent, trackable pricing. Rather than citing a specific blanket percentage we can't independently verify, the practical approach is the same as for tech: check the price history on specific items you need before assuming a "sale" badge reflects a genuine discount.

General strategy that holds regardless of the specific event: stock up on consumable, non-tech supplies (pens, notebooks, folders) whenever you find a confirmed real discount, and reserve your tech budget for the one or two devices you actually need — checked against real price history first.

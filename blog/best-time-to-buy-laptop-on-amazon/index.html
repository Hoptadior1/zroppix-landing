Why Amazon beats the Apple Store on MacBooks

Apple has strict Minimum Advertised Pricing rules. The Apple Store almost never discounts MacBooks. Amazon is an Apple Authorized Reseller that regularly offers $100-200 off MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models during Back to School and Black Friday events. For a MacBook purchase — Amazon during these windows is almost always your best option.

When to buy a gaming laptop

Gaming laptops from brands like ASUS ROG, Razer, MSI, and Alienware follow a different pattern. New GPU generations typically launch in the fall, which means September-October sees current generation gaming laptops drop in anticipation of new models. Black Friday then delivers the deepest discounts on both current and previous generation gaming laptops — typically 20-35% off.

The 3 Best Laptop Buying Windows in 2026

Window 1 — Back to School: July-August 2026

Back to School is the most underrated laptop buying season. Student discounts are genuine — retailers compete aggressively to capture the educational market. Lenovo, HP, and ASUS deliver 15-25% discounts. MacBook Air models see $100-150 price reductions that Apple never offers directly. If you need a laptop before Black Friday, Back to School in July is your best option.

Window 2 — After New Model Announcements: September-October

When Apple announces new MacBooks or when major brands release updated processors, the previous generation drops immediately. The M2 MacBook Air dropped by $200 within days of the M3 announcement. If you are happy with last year's model — buying immediately after an announcement can deliver all-time low pricing. Monitor Apple's September event and major laptop brand announcements.

Window 3 — Black Friday: November 2026

The single best time to buy a laptop on Amazon. Our 12-month analysis shows an average 22% discount across all brands during Black Friday — the deepest of any annual event. The catch: popular configurations sell out quickly. Have your Zroppix price alert set now so you are notified the moment any deal goes live, rather than discovering it after it has sold out.

Laptop Buying Mistakes That Cost Real Money

Mistake 1 — Buying in May or October

Our data shows laptops average 6-8% above the annual average price in both May and October. May is the worst month due to inventory transition. October spikes pre-holiday. Avoid both if possible.

Mistake 2 — Buying before checking if a new model is coming

Before buying any laptop, spend 60 seconds searching "[laptop name] 2026 release date." If a new version is due in 2-3 months — wait. You will either get the new model at launch price or the current model at a significantly reduced clearance price. Either outcome is better than buying the current model two months before it becomes last generation.

Mistake 3 — Trusting sale badges without price history

Amazon's sale badges on laptops are frequently misleading. A laptop showing "15% off" may be priced above its 90-day average. Always check with Zroppix before buying. If the verdict is WAIT despite a sale badge — the badge is calculated from an inflated reference price, not a genuine saving.

Best time to buy a laptop on Amazon — FAQ
What is the best time to buy a laptop on Amazon?+
The three best times are Back to School in July-August with 15-20% average discounts, Black Friday in November with 20-25% average discounts, and after a new model announcement when previous generation prices drop significantly. May pricing is historically 8% above average — wait if you can.
How much do laptops drop in price on Amazon?+
Budget laptops typically drop 10-20% during sale events. Mid-range laptops drop 15-25%. Premium laptops like MacBook Pro typically drop 10-15% due to manufacturer price controls. The average saving during Black Friday across all laptop categories is approximately $180.
Is Prime Day or Black Friday better for laptops?+
Black Friday is typically better — 22% average discounts vs 12-18% during Prime Day. The exception is if a specific model you want is featured as a Prime Day deal, in which case savings can match Black Friday levels.

Amazon often has better prices on MacBooks during sale events. Amazon is an Apple Authorized Reseller that regularly offers $100-200 off MacBook models during Back to School and Black Friday — discounts Apple almost never offers at its own store.

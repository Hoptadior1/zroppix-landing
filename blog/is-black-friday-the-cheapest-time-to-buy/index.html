What the studies found

Which?, the UK consumer group, tracked prices every day around Black Friday. These are its two studies we found:

  • Black Friday 2020, 201 deals: Which? (November 23, 2021) tracked each product daily for six months before and six months after at six UK retailers. It found 99.5% were the same price or cheaper at other times of the year, 92% were the same or cheaper in the six months before, and 40% were strictly cheaper at some other time. Only one of the 201 was at its cheapest on Black Friday alone.
  • An earlier Which? study, 83 products: Which? via LBC (November 26, 2019) said just 5% of Black Friday deals were cheaper during the sale than at other times.

How to read this: "the same price or cheaper" includes ties, so many of these products were simply not discounted on Black Friday. It does not mean Black Friday prices are fake, and it does not mean they never win. It also covers UK retailers in 2019 and 2020, not US stores in 2026.

A four-step buy-or-wait check

  • Find the history for your exact model. The free Zroppix extension shows 90 days on Amazon, or use a tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa.
  • Compare today's price with the lowest in that history. If it is at or near the low, waiting has less to offer.
  • Check the safety net. Some stores refund the difference if the price drops, as in our price adjustment guide.
  • Decide with an alert. If the price is well above the low, set an alert and buy when it falls. This is a rule of thumb, not a prediction.

When Black Friday can still make sense

We don't say Black Friday deals are fake or that prices always fall. Some products do hit their lowest price then, and the Which? study found one of 201 did. The point is that you can't assume it. Common mistakes

  • Trusting the "was" price. A crossed-out price is not a price history.
  • Waiting for a discount that doesn't come. The MacBook Neo example went up in June.
  • Ignoring earlier sales. Prime Day in June was lower than last year's Black Friday for the AirPods Pro 3.
  • Comparing different versions. Model, color and seller change the price.

Why colour and edition decide the price

A DualSense price is only meaningful for one colour. Best Buy's Black Friday-week listing on November 20, 2025 priced six colours (White, Midnight Black, Cosmic Red, Starlight Blue, Galactic Purple and Gray Camouflage) at $54.99, another six (Volcanic Red, Cobalt Blue, Sterling Silver, Chroma Pearl, Chroma Indigo and Chroma Teal) at $59.99, and limited editions such as Astro Bot Joyful, God of War 20th Anniversary and Ghost of Yōtei Gold at $64.99. A commenter confirmed the only difference was colour.

So before you compare, find the exact colour on the listing.

What the price did last Black Friday

Last year, three dated reports: Best Buy $54.99 against a $75 list (November 20, 2025, expired), Android Headlines at $54.99 and $59.99 against a $74.99 usual price (published November 21, 2025, which called the price "almost certainly" the lowest heading into Black Friday, an expectation rather than a record), and Slickdeals at $54 for Sterling Silver at Amazon against an $80 list (November 25, 2025, expired).

That suggests the low-to-mid $50s was where mainstream colours landed in late November 2025, but that is a single year and no promise for 2026. Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday is November 30. Amazon's October Prime Big Deal Days already ran October 6 and 7.

How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the PS5 DualSense controller on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

What to do now

  • You need it now: check the live price. If it's near last year's mid-$50s, buy.
  • You can wait: set an alert and decide in the Black Friday window. See our Black Friday plan.
  • You want a specific colour: check that colour only, since the cheapest colours move first.

Common mistakes when buying

  • Comparing different colours. Limited editions cost more.
  • Assuming last year's price repeats. One year is a data point, not a rule.
  • Trusting the $75 or $80 "was" price.
  • Buying the Edge when you meant the standard.

Complete DualSense Price History — Launch to 2026

The DualSense launched alongside the PS5 in November 2020 at $69.99. This price held for approximately 18 months before Sony raised the list price to $74.99 in April 2022 as part of a broader PlayStation hardware price adjustment in response to component cost increases. Since then the list price has remained at $74.99 with discounts appearing only during specific promotional windows.

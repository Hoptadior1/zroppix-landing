Why We Don't Trust the Banner Discount

Retailers routinely raise a product's price for a few weeks before a sale event, then "discount" it back down to where it normally sits — creating a bigger-looking percentage off than what you're actually saving. That's why every price above comes from tracked historical data, not the discount badge Amazon shows you at checkout. The Chromebook is a genuine best-of-year price. The iPad and AirPods are solid, real prices — just don't expect them to be dramatically below their normal range this particular week.

What Amazon says its price history does

Amazon’s page on checking price history was published May 1, 2026 and updated June 24, 2026. It says:

  • Price history is available through a link on product detail pages, placed next to the price, and through Alexa for Shopping, where you can ask about a product’s price history.
  • The views cover 30, 90 and 365 days. The 365-day view is described as rolling out in the U.S., UK and India, with full availability “in the coming weeks.”
  • It is available to all customers in the U.S., UK, Canada and India.
  • Amazon says more than 50 million customers have used it since its 2024 launch. That is Amazon’s own figure.

The page does not say how the prices are collected, whether third-party seller prices are included, or how complete the data is for new products. It also does not describe a recommendation: the tool shows history so you can judge whether a deal is lower than the usual price.

What a price history cannot tell you

  • Whether a lower price is coming. History shows the past. Pro includes drop estimates, but an estimate is not a promise.
  • The price at other stores. A good price on Amazon can still be above what another seller charges.
  • Which version you are looking at. A colour, size or bundle can have its own history.
  • Why a price moved. A fall can be a coupon, a sale or a clearance.

Which should you use?

If you want a quick look at one Amazon product, Amazon’s link is the simplest and costs nothing. If you shop across stores, want an alert, or want a plain call on whether today’s price is low, a tool like Zroppix saves steps. Many people will use both, since two sources can disagree and a disagreement is a reason to look closer. Our comparison of price trackers covers the other options, and our page on the discount lawsuits explains why checking a “was” price matters.

How to check a price in three steps

  1. Open the price history on the product page and choose 90 days.
  2. Find the lowest price in that range and the price just before the current sale.
  3. Compare the total with the same model at another store.

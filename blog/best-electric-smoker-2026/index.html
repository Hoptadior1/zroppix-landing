Why Electric Smokers Are a Popular Choice

Electric smokers have carved out a real, growing niche among outdoor cooking appliances that prioritize convenience: they require less active monitoring than charcoal or propane setups, use standard 120V household power instead of specialty fuel, and offer more precise, hands-off temperature control than traditional methods. For anyone who wants genuinely low-effort, consistent results without babysitting a fire, electric is the category to shop in.

Why the Masterbuilt 40-Inch Bluetooth Is the Default Recommendation

The Masterbuilt 40-inch Digital Electric Smoker (specifically the Bluetooth-enabled model) pairs a built-in meat probe with app-based Bluetooth monitoring, removing the guesswork of separately tracking internal temperature and letting you check progress without standing over the unit. How Much Electricity Does an Electric Smoker Actually Use

Electric smoker heating elements in this category typically run in the 800-1,650 watt range during initial heating, then cycle down to maintain temperature once the target is reached — genuinely efficient for the length of most smoking sessions, which can run several hours to over a day depending on the cut of meat. This lower, cycling power draw compares favorably to running a gas grill's burners continuously or restocking charcoal throughout an equally long cook.

ℹ️

Weather Considerations: Electric smokers handle cold weather reasonably well with additional insulation — a welding blanket or dedicated smoker insulation jacket helps maintain consistent temperature in winter conditions. Manufacturers consistently advise against use in rain or snow, since water intrusion can damage the heating element and electrical components.

Is an electric smoker better than a pellet smoker?+

Pellet smokers produce a stronger smoke flavor and can typically reach higher temperatures for searing, making them the better choice for maximum flavor and versatility.
How much electricity does an electric smoker use?+
Most electric smokers draw between 800 and 1,650 watts during the initial heating phase, then cycle down to maintain temperature once the target is reached, making them relatively energy-efficient for long cook sessions compared to running a gas or charcoal setup for the same duration.
What is the side wood chip loading feature on the Masterbuilt smoker?+

Can electric smokers be used in cold or rainy weather?+

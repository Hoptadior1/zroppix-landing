How Much Electricity Does an Electric Smoker Actually Use

Most electric smokers draw 800-1,500 watts during the initial heating phase, then cycle down to roughly 300-500 watts to maintain temperature once the target is reached — genuinely efficient for the length of most smoking sessions, which can run 6-14 hours depending on the cut of meat. This lower, steadier power draw compares favorably to running a gas grill's burners continuously or restocking charcoal throughout an equally long cook, both in terms of ongoing cost and hands-on effort required.

ℹ️

Weather Considerations: Electric smokers handle cold weather reasonably well with additional insulation — a welding blanket or dedicated smoker insulation jacket helps maintain consistent temperature in winter conditions. Manufacturers consistently advise against use in rain or snow, since water intrusion can damage the heating element and electrical components — worth planning cook sessions around clear weather specifically for electric models, more so than gas or charcoal alternatives.

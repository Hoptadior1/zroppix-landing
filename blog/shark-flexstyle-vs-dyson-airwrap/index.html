What changed since our last update

The page we replaced said the Dyson Airwrap "drops to $399 to $449 every Prime Day since 2022" and that the gap to the Shark was $200. The 2026 deal coverage we can open doesn't show a $399 price. 9to5Toys (May 22, 2026) reported the Airwrap i.d. at $499 against a $649 list, calling it the best price it had tracked in 2026 and the second-lowest overall. It said a one-time drop to $479 happened in December. On August 14 a special-edition set was $519.99, $21 above the year's lowest tracked price. Both deals are marked expired.

What independent reviewers say

The best side-by-side we could open is TechRadar's comparison (last updated November 23, 2022). It tested the original FlexStyle and Airwrap, so newer versions like the Airwrap i.d. and FlexStyle Air may differ. Its findings:

  • Overall: the Airwrap "does take the edge," with better design, attachments and finish. Its volumising brush held volume for hours, while the FlexStyle's "fell flat within half an hour."
  • Curly hair: the FlexStyle's diffuser gave the most defined curls.
  • Drying: roughly even, at 2 minutes 47 seconds against 2 minutes 46.
  • Versatility: the FlexStyle's rotating barrel works as a normal hair dryer without an extra attachment.
  • Value: the FlexStyle wins because it costs far less.

TechRadar also said both tools were frustrating for curling. We haven't tested either, so treat this as one review, not a verdict.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Shark FlexStyle as good as the Dyson Airwrap?

TechRadar said the Airwrap takes the edge on finish, volume and attachments, and the FlexStyle is the most worthy alternative it found. That review used older models.

How much is the Shark FlexStyle Air?

Gizmodo reported the copper version at $200 against a usual $314 on September 16, 2026, and other versions at $250 against $350. Check the live listing.

How much is the Dyson Airwrap?

9to5Toys reported the Airwrap i.d. at $499 against a $649 list on May 22, 2026, and $519.99 on August 14. Both are expired. We couldn't find a 2026 price of $399.

Which is better for curly hair?

TechRadar found the FlexStyle's diffuser gave the most defined curls.

Which is better for beginners?

TechRadar said both were frustrating for curling and that the Airwrap's mode switch is a little fiddly. Neither is effortless at first.

Will they be cheaper on Black Friday?

We can't predict that. Black Friday is November 27 and Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026. Set an alert and check the history first.

Where should I buy the Dyson?

9to5Toys' May deal was through Amazon's official Dyson storefront. Buy from Dyson or Amazon and check the seller line.

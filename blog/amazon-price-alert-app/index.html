Method 1 — Amazon Rufus (Built-In, No App Needed)

Amazon added a built-in price alert feature to its shopping app through its AI assistant Rufus. This is the simplest way to set alerts without installing anything extra — but it only works through the Amazon app, not the website.

How to set up Rufus price alerts on iPhone or Android

1

Turn on notifications first

Open the Amazon app → tap Account → Settings → Notifications. Make sure shopping and price notifications are enabled. Without this step Rufus tracks prices silently and you never see the alert.

2

Open any product page

Navigate to the Amazon product you want to track inside the Amazon shopping app on your iPhone or Android.

3

Tap the Rufus button

Look for the colorful Rufus chat bubble icon on the product page or near the bottom of the screen. Tap it to open the Rufus assistant.

4

Ask Rufus to track the price

Type or say "Track the price of this product" or "Alert me when this goes on sale." Rufus will confirm it is now monitoring the price for you.

5

Wait for the notification

Amazon will push a notification to your phone when the price drops significantly. Note: you cannot set a specific target price with Rufus — it decides when to alert you.

Limitation: Rufus does not let you set a custom target price. It alerts you when Amazon decides the drop is significant enough — which may not be the price you actually want to pay.

Method 3 — Keepa App (iPhone and Android)

Keepa has a dedicated mobile app available on both the App Store and Google Play. It brings the full power of Keepa price history charts to your phone and lets you set push notification alerts at a specific target price. As of 2026 the Keepa app has over 2.8 million downloads and a 4.3 out of 5 star rating.

How to set Keepa price alerts on mobile

  1. Download Keepa from the App Store or Google Play — free
  2. Create a free Keepa account or log in
  3. Search for a product by name or paste an Amazon product URL
  4. Tap Track and enter your target price
  5. Enable push notifications in your phone settings for the Keepa app
  6. Keepa monitors 24/7 and sends a push notification when the price drops to your target

Pro tip: You can import your entire Amazon wishlist into Keepa to track multiple products at once without entering them one by one.

Limitation: Advanced alert features require a paid Keepa subscription. The free tier has restrictions on how many products you can track simultaneously.

Method 4 — CamelCamelCamel (Email Alerts, Mobile Browser)

CamelCamelCamel does not have a dedicated mobile app but its website works well on mobile browsers. You can set email price alerts from your phone in under a minute and they will arrive as push notifications if your email app has notifications enabled.

  1. Open your mobile browser and go to camelcamelcamel.com
  2. Create a free account or log in
  3. Copy the Amazon product URL from the Amazon app or mobile browser
  4. Paste it into the CamelCamelCamel search bar
  5. Set your desired target price and click Create Alert
  6. CamelCamelCamel emails you when the price drops to your target

Completely free with no limits on how many products you can track. Some users report alert delays — not ideal if you need to act fast on a flash sale.

Method 5 — Amazon Wishlist (Basic, No Setup Required)

Adding a product to your Amazon Wishlist is the most basic way to get price drop notifications. Amazon occasionally emails you when a wishlist item drops in price — but you cannot set a specific target price and notifications are inconsistent.

  1. Open the Amazon app on your iPhone or Android
  2. Go to any product page and tap Add to List
  3. Go to Account → Your Lists → Manage List
  4. Enable price drop notifications in the list settings
  5. Amazon will email you if the price drops significantly

Limitation: Amazon controls when and whether alerts trigger. You cannot set a specific price threshold. For serious savings, use one of the methods above instead.

Tips To Get The Most From Mobile Price Alerts

Enable email notifications on your phone

If you use email-based alerts like Zroppix or CamelCamelCamel, make sure your email app has push notifications turned on. That way the alert pops up on your phone screen instantly — just like a text message.

Set your alert before sale events

Set price alerts a few weeks before Prime Day and Black Friday. Prices on tracked products often drop significantly during these events and you want your alert ready to fire the moment it happens.

Act fast when alerts arrive

Amazon flash sales can last just a few hours. When a price alert hits your phone, check it quickly. The price may return to normal before the end of the day.

Track multiple similar products

If you want wireless headphones for example, set alerts on three or four different models at your target price. Buy whichever one drops first. You get the product you want without waiting forever for one specific item to drop.

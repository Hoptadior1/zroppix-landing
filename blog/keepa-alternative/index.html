Keepa vs CamelCamelCamel vs Zroppix: Which Is Best in 2026?

📅 May 2026 ⏱ 6 min read ✍️ Zroppix Team

If you want to stop overpaying on Amazon you need a price tracker. The three names that come up most often are Keepa, CamelCamelCamel, and Zroppix. They all track Amazon prices — but they work very differently and serve very different types of shoppers.

Here is an honest breakdown of all three so you can pick the right one for how you actually shop.

💡 Bottom line up front: Keepa is best for Amazon sellers and power users. CamelCamelCamel is best for casual shoppers who want free and simple. Zroppix is best for everyday shoppers who want a clear AI answer — not just a chart to interpret.

The Core Problem All Three Tools Solve

Amazon changes prices on millions of products every single day. A product with a sale badge may actually be at its highest price in months. Without price history data you have no way of knowing whether a deal is real or manufactured.

All three tools solve this by showing you what a product has actually cost over time. Where they diverge is in what they do with that data — and how much they ask you to figure out yourself.

Keepa — The Power User's Tool

Best for Power Users and Sellers

Keepa

Keepa is the most widely used Amazon price tracker in the world with over 4 million Chrome users and a 4.7 out of 5 star rating. It tracks over 5 billion Amazon products across 11 international marketplaces and embeds detailed price history charts directly on every Amazon product page you visit.

The charts show the Amazon price, third-party new prices, third-party used prices, sales rank, Buy Box history, and inventory levels — all going back years. For Amazon resellers and serious deal hunters this depth of data is unmatched.

The free tier gives you basic charts. The premium subscription at around €19 per month unlocks full price alerts via email, push notifications, and Telegram, plus API access for large-scale data work.

  • Most data of any tool
  • 4M+ Chrome users
  • 11 Amazon marketplaces
  • Mobile app available
  • Barcode scanning
  • API access for sellers
✗ Cons
  • Charts are complex to read
  • No AI verdict or guidance
  • Full alerts need paid plan
  • Overkill for casual shoppers
  • Premium costs €19/month

Keepa Verdict

If you sell on Amazon or do online arbitrage, Keepa is essential. If you are a regular shopper who just wants to know whether to buy now or wait — the charts will feel like overkill.

CamelCamelCamel — The Free Classic

Best for Casual Shoppers

CamelCamelCamel

CamelCamelCamel has been tracking Amazon prices since 2008 — making it the original and most trusted free price history tool. Over 800,000 people use it. The browser extension known as The Camelizer overlays price history charts directly on Amazon product pages.

It shows the high, low, and average price over 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 1 year. You can set email price alerts for free with no account required. It covers Amazon in the US, UK, Canada, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Australia, and Japan.

The entire tool is completely free — funded by affiliate links. No premium tier, no subscription, no upsell. Everything it does is available to everyone at no cost.

✗ Cons

  • Less data than Keepa
  • No mobile app

CamelCamelCamel Verdict

Perfect if you want a completely free, zero-hassle price check before a purchase. The weak point is you still have to look at the chart and decide for yourself — and some users report their alerts arrive late.

Best for Everyday Shoppers

Zroppix

Zroppix takes a completely different approach to the other two tools. Rather than showing you a chart and leaving you to interpret it, Zroppix uses AI to analyze the price history and gives you a direct answer — BUY or WAIT.

Click the Zroppix icon on any Amazon product and you instantly see your overpay risk score from 0 to 100, what percentage of shoppers paid less than the current price, the lowest and average prices on record, your potential savings, and the BUY or WAIT verdict all in one clean popup.

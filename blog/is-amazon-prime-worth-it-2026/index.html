The Prime Video Question

Prime Video is the benefit that makes or breaks the value calculation for most members. At $8.99/month standalone, it represents $107.88/year — 77% of Prime's total cost. If you use it regularly, the rest of Prime is essentially free.

Prime Video strengths: The Boys, Rings of Power, Reacher, Fallout — genuinely high-quality originals with real audiences. Channel add-ons (Paramount+, MGM+) can replace multiple separate streaming subscriptions at a discount.

Prime Video limitations: The interface mixes Prime-included content with paid rentals confusingly. Ads were introduced in early 2024 — removing them costs $2.99/month extra. Total for ad-free Prime Video: $11.58 (Prime) + $2.99 = $14.57/month — more than Netflix's ad-free plan at $15.49.

If you value ad-free viewing and use Prime primarily for Prime Video — compare $14.57/month (Prime + ad-free) against $15.49/month Netflix. Netflix typically has a stronger content library for most tastes. Worth checking before auto-renewing.

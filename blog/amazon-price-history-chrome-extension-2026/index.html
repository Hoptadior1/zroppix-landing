Why Amazon Does Not Show Price History

Before comparing extensions it helps to understand why they exist. Amazon deliberately hides price history from shoppers. Their product pages show today's price and a crossed-out "was" price — but no chart, no history, no context.

This is not an oversight. It is a deliberate design decision. Amazon's algorithm knows that showing customers "this product was $40 cheaper three weeks ago" would dramatically reduce impulse purchases and create hesitation at checkout. Hiding price history is profitable for Amazon.

This created a market for third-party price history extensions — tools that track Amazon prices independently and show customers the history Amazon hides. The tools on this list all solve that problem, just in different ways for different audiences.

🚨 Amazon actively tries to limit price history tools by frequently changing its HTML structure. All extensions on this list have been tested and confirmed working in May 2026, but functionality can change.

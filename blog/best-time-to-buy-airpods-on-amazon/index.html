When to Expect the Next Real AirPods Sale

Outside of Prime Day, the two other windows worth watching are:

  • Amazon Big Deal Days (October) — Amazon's second major annual sale event, historically bringing another round of Apple discounts, though typically not always as deep as Prime Day or Black Friday.
  • Black Friday (November) — Historically Apple's and Amazon's biggest combined discount window of the year, and the event where AirPods Pro-tier models have most reliably hit their lowest tracked prices in past years.

If you're not in urgent need, waiting for one of these two windows and comparing the live price against the confirmed Prime Day 2026 figures above is a reasonable strategy. If you need AirPods now, the honest answer is to check the live price — if it's close to the Prime Day figures, that's a fair price; if it's noticeably higher, you're likely paying a post-sale-event premium.

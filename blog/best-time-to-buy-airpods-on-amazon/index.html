What the prices suggest

  • Black Friday wasn't the low for the Pro 3. 9to5Toys (November 28, 2025) called $220 an all-time low in November 2025. Later reports of $169 to $179 were lower.
  • Prime Day in June was the low for the Max 2. 9to5Toys (July 27, 2026) said the lowest price it tracked was around Prime Day, when most colors dropped $150. The $429 on October 5 was within $30 of that, per 9to5Toys (October 5, 2026).
  • Prices bounce. The Pro 3 went from $179 on October 6 to $199 on October 15, per 9to5Toys (October 15, 2026).
  • Black Friday 2026 is November 27. We can't say what AirPods will cost then, and one year of examples isn't a pattern. A UK study found most Black Friday deals matched or were beaten at other times, which we cover in Is Black Friday the cheapest time to buy?

A four-step check before you buy

  • Pick the exact model. The Pro 3, Max 2, AirPods 4 and AirPods 4 with ANC are priced very differently.
  • Compare today's price with the table above. Within about $20 of the lowest reported price is close to the best we have seen.
  • Check the seller. Amazon or Apple is safest.
  • If it isn't close, set an alert, and keep your receipt in case the price falls after you buy. See the price adjustment rules.

Which model, briefly

  • AirPods Pro 3 ($249 list): the flagship earbuds. See its price page.
  • AirPods Max 2 ($549 list): Macworld (March 17, 2026) said it adds an H2 chip, USB-C and noise cancellation rated 1.5 times better than the original, with the same size, weight and battery life. See its price page.
  • AirPods 4 ($129 list) and AirPods 4 with ANC ($179 list): the cheaper earbuds. See their price page.

We haven't tested any of them. Read independent reviews for sound and comfort, then compare the price.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming an old deal is still live. Every sale price above has expired.
  • Comparing different models. $120 for AirPods 4 with ANC is a different deal from $179 for the Pro 3.
  • Waiting for one date. The lowest prices came in different months for different models.
  • Ignoring the seller and color. Some sales apply to certain colors only.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to buy AirPods?

The dated prices we found say it varies by model. The lowest reported Pro 3 and Max 2 prices were around June Prime Day, and the Pro 3 was $220 on Black Friday 2025. Check the live price against the table above.

How much are the AirPods Pro 3?

Apple lists them at $249. 9to5Toys reported $179 at Amazon on October 6, 2026 and $199 on October 15.

How much are the AirPods Max 2?

Macworld listed $549. 9to5Toys reported $429 at Amazon on October 5, 2026, and said the lowest was around June Prime Day at $150 off.

Will AirPods be cheaper on Black Friday 2026?

We can't say. Black Friday is November 27, 2026. The Pro 3 was $220 on Black Friday 2025, and lower prices have been reported since.

Are the AirPods 4 on sale?

We found $119.99 for AirPods 4 with ANC on March 11, 2026 and $89.99 for the standard model on October 5, 2025. We found no report for October 2026.

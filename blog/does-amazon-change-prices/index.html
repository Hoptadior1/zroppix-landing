Method 2 — CamelCamelCamel

CamelCamelCamel is one of the oldest Amazon price alert services. Here is how to use it:

  1. Go to camelcamelcamel.com
  2. Create a free account
  3. Copy the Amazon product URL
  4. Paste it into CamelCamelCamel
  5. Set your desired price
  6. Click create alert

CamelCamelCamel will email you when the price drops. The downside is you need to create an account and manually copy and paste URLs for every product you want to track.

Method 3 — Amazon Watch This Deal

Amazon has a built in feature on some deals pages called Watch this deal. When available you can click it to get notified when a deal price becomes available.

The limitation is this only works on specific deals — not on regular products at their standard price. You cannot set a custom target price.

How To Choose Your Target Price

Setting the right target price is important. Too low and the alert never triggers. Too high and you miss out on savings.

Here is a simple framework:

  • Check the average price — Zroppix shows you this automatically. Set your alert 10-15% below average for a realistic target.
  • Check the lowest price — If the all time low was $199 and current price is $279 set alert at $210-220 for a realistic target.
  • Consider urgency — If you need it soon set a higher target. If you can wait set it lower for maximum savings.

Tips To Get The Most From Price Alerts

Set alerts on multiple products

If you are shopping for a category — like wireless headphones — set alerts on 3-4 different models. Buy whichever drops to your target first.

Act fast when alerts trigger

Flash sales on Amazon can last just hours. When you get a price alert check it quickly. The price may not stay low for long.

Use the BUY verdict as a signal

When Zroppix shows a BUY verdict on a product you have been tracking that is a strong signal to buy now even if your exact target price has not been hit yet.

Check before major sale events

Before Black Friday and Prime Day check your tracked products. Prices often drop significantly during these events — sometimes below your alert threshold.

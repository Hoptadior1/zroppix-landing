Does Walmart Price Match in 2026? What Walmart Says

People often search “does Walmart price match Amazon?” The short version from Walmart’s own “Ask Walmart” page, which we read on November 1, 2026, is that it matches itself. This page sets out what it says, how it compares with Best Buy, and what to do about it.

⚡ Quick Answer

Per Walmart’s own page, Walmart U.S. stores will match the price of the identical item advertised on Walmart.com. Walmart.com does not price match: it excludes competitor prices, Marketplace sellers, and items you bought on Walmart.com that later dropped in price.

In a Walmart store

The page says: for items purchased in a Walmart U.S. store, Walmart will match the price of the identical item advertised on Walmart.com. Restrictions apply, and the store manager on duty makes the final decision. In practice, that means you can show the Walmart.com listing on your phone at the register if it is lower than the shelf price.

A hypothetical, for illustration only: if the shelf tag says $100 and the same item is listed on Walmart.com at $90 and sold by Walmart, the page says the store will match the $90 price, subject to the manager’s decision. The same rule would not apply if the $90 listing were from a Marketplace seller.

On Walmart.com

The page says Walmart.com does not price match. It strives to offer its best price online every day, and the policy excludes:

  • Prices offered by competitors
  • Items previously bought on Walmart.com that later dropped in price
  • Marketplace or third-party seller prices
  • Items sold in Walmart stores or Neighborhood Markets

The page does not mention a price adjustment after purchase. If an item you bought on Walmart.com falls in price, the page says it is not covered.

What to do instead

  1. Check the Walmart.com price before you walk in. If the store shelf is higher, ask for the match.
  2. Look at who is selling. A Walmart.com listing can be a Marketplace seller. Those prices are excluded from the match.
  3. If you want a rival’s price, buy from the rival. Walmart’s page does not offer to match Amazon or Best Buy.
  4. Compare totals, not sticker prices. Shipping and the seller change the real cost. A dated, sourced comparison across stores is what Zroppix Find is for.
  5. Screenshot the Walmart.com page with the date, and show it at the register.

You bought in a store and the Walmart.com price dropped afterwardsThe page does not say. Ask the store and keep your receipt.

The last row is the gap. The page describes matching at the time of purchase in a store. It does not describe a refund of the difference after the fact, so do not assume one exists.

What this means for Black Friday

Walmart’s page says nothing specific about Black Friday. Because the store only matches Walmart.com, a rival’s Black Friday price is not something the page offers to match. If a Walmart.com price drops after you buy there, the page says that is not covered, so the safest habit is to compare before you pay. Black Friday is November 27, 2026.

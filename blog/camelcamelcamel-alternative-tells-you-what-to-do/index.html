The Problem With Price History Graphs

CamelCamelCamel and Keepa show you price history charts. Those charts are valuable. But they create a problem: interpretation requires effort and expertise that most shoppers do not have.

When you look at a price history chart you have to mentally calculate: what is the average? Is today's price above or below it? How volatile is the price? Is there a seasonal pattern? When was the last major drop? How long until the next one?

Most people make a rough guess based on what the line looks like and move on. That guess is often wrong — and it costs real money.

When you open any Amazon product page and click the Zroppix icon in your Chrome toolbar, the extension:

Price match vs price adjustment

They sound alike, but they happen at different times. Consumer Reports via KRGV (January 7, 2026) explained it this way:

  • Price match happens before you buy. A store agrees to match a lower advertised price.
  • Price adjustment happens after you buy. If the item drops in price, the store refunds the difference.

Most Black Friday regret comes from the second one: you buy in early November and the price falls on the 27th.

What this means around Black Friday 2026

Black Friday is November 27, 2026. These are the windows as reported, and the arithmetic is ours.

  • Target (14 days): a purchase made around November 13 or later would still be inside the window on Black Friday, per ConsumerAffairs (October 9, 2026).
  • Best Buy (15 days, 30 for some members): Priceva (updated May 14, 2026) also said purchases from October 31 to December 31 can be matched through January 15. Verify that on BestBuy.com.
  • Costco (30 days): a purchase from about October 28 would be inside the window on Black Friday, per Consumer Reports via KRGV (January 7, 2026).
  • Amazon: DealNews (updated July 27, 2026) said there is no standard adjustment. For pre-orders it charges the lowest price in the window. Within the return window you can return an item and buy it again at the lower price, if returns are free for that item.
  • Best Buy and special events: Priceva (updated May 14, 2026) said Black Friday and Cyber Monday pricing is excluded from competitor matching.

How to claim an adjustment: five steps

  • Keep the receipt or order number. You will need it.
  • Check the lower price live. ConsumerAffairs (October 9, 2026) said Target does not accept screenshots, and employees must be able to see the price themselves.
  • Match the exact item: the model number, color and seller, per Consumer Reports via KRGV (January 7, 2026).
  • Ask inside the window, in store, by chat or by phone, depending on the store.
  • If refused, check your credit card's price protection, which Consumer Reports via KRGV (January 7, 2026) said some cards offer, or return and rebuy where the store allows it.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming Amazon will refund the difference. It has no standard price adjustment, per DealNews.
  • Waiting too long. Target's window is 14 days and Best Buy's is 15.
  • Showing a screenshot. Target does not accept them.
  • Expecting a match on a marketplace seller. Best Buy excludes them, per Priceva.
  • Skipping the exclusions. Clearance, refurbished and special-event prices are often excluded.

Frequently asked questions

Does Amazon do price adjustments?

DealNews said Amazon has no standard price adjustment refund or formal price-matching policy. It charges the lowest price in the window for pre-orders, and agents may offer a credit at their discretion.

How long does Target give you?

ConsumerAffairs said 14 days, on Target's own price. It said Target no longer matches Amazon or Walmart.

How long does Best Buy give you?

Priceva said 15 days, or 30 for My Best Buy Plus and Total members, and that purchases from October 31 to December 31 can be matched through January 15. Verify that on BestBuy.com.

Does Walmart price match?

Consumer Reports said Walmart matches its own Walmart.com price in store, and doesn't match competitors.

What if the price drops on Black Friday after I buy?

Check the store's window. Target, Best Buy and Costco have windows of 14, 15 and 30 days in the sources above. Amazon has none, but you may be able to return and rebuy.

Are these rules the same all year?

Not always. Priceva said Best Buy excludes Black Friday and Cyber Monday pricing from competitor matching, and holiday policies change.

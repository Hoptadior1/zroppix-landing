The Core Problem With Keepa for Regular Shoppers

Open any Amazon product with Keepa installed and you see a chart overlaid on the product page. That chart shows:

  • Amazon price history (blue line)
  • Third-party new price history (green line)
  • Used price history (orange line)
  • Sales rank history (red line)
  • Buy Box winner percentage
  • Lightning deal markers
  • Coupon indicators

For an Amazon reseller this data is gold. They need to understand inventory dynamics, Buy Box competition, and seasonal sales rank patterns to make buying and selling decisions.

For someone who wants to know if they should buy an air fryer today or wait — this chart tells them everything except the one thing they actually need: is the current price good or not?

🚨 Keepa shows you all the data and makes you do the analysis yourself. Most regular shoppers look at the chart, make a rough guess about whether the current price looks high or low on the graph, and move on. That rough guess is often wrong — and it costs real money.

Poppi vs OLIPOP — Which Is Actually Better?

Both Poppi and OLIPOP are prebiotic sodas with gut health claims, low sugar, and strong TikTok communities. They are different products for different taste preferences.

OLIPOP — The One That Tastes Like Real Soda

OLIPOP is designed to taste as close to traditional soda as possible while delivering prebiotic fiber benefits. Its flavors like Vintage Cola, Classic Root Beer, and Classic Grape genuinely evoke their full-sugar counterparts. The sweetness comes from cane sugar and stevia. At 35-45 calories per can and 9g of dietary fiber, it is the prebiotic soda most likely to satisfy a traditional soda craving.

OLIPOP works best as a direct Coca-Cola or Sprite replacement for someone reducing sugar intake but not ready to drink plain water all day. The TikTok content that made OLIPOP viral is almost all "I replaced Coke with OLIPOP and here's what happened" style — that is the product's strongest use case.

Poppi — The Wellness-Forward Choice

Poppi uses apple cider vinegar as its prebiotic source, which gives it a distinctive tart, slightly vinegary flavor that is immediately noticeable. At 25 calories per can it is lower in calories than OLIPOP. Its best flavors — Strawberry Lemon, Raspberry Rose, Cherry Limeade — lean fruity and refreshing rather than soda-classic.

Poppi works best for someone who already likes kombucha or apple cider vinegar drinks and wants a sparkling, sweeter version. If the idea of apple cider vinegar in a beverage sounds unpleasant to you, try OLIPOP instead — Poppi's ACV flavor is present in all varieties.

The Health Claims — What Prebiotics Actually Do

Both Poppi and OLIPOP are marketed primarily as gut health beverages. Understanding what prebiotics actually do — and what the science says — helps you evaluate whether these products are worth buying beyond the taste and price angle.

Prebiotics are non-digestible fibers that feed beneficial bacteria (probiotics) in your gut microbiome. Unlike probiotics themselves (live bacteria), prebiotics are the food source that helps existing gut bacteria thrive. The clinical evidence for prebiotics is generally positive: regular prebiotic intake is associated with improved digestion, better regularity, and immune support. However, the research specifically on prebiotic sodas as a delivery mechanism is limited compared to traditional fiber sources like vegetables, legumes, and whole grains.

OLIPOP's prebiotic blend includes cassava root fiber, chicory root inulin, Jerusalem artichoke inulin, nopal cactus, and calendula. At 9g of dietary fiber per can, OLIPOP delivers a meaningful prebiotic dose — approximately 32% of the recommended daily fiber intake in a single can.

Poppi's prebiotic source is apple cider vinegar (ACV), which provides acetic acid and trace amounts of pectin. The clinical evidence for ACV specifically is mixed — some studies suggest modest blood sugar regulation benefits; others show no significant effect. Poppi's 4g of fiber per can is lower than OLIPOP's 9g.

The honest assessment: both are better for you than traditional soda and provide real prebiotic benefit. OLIPOP's higher fiber content and more established prebiotic blend gives it a slight health edge on paper. Neither replaces a diet rich in whole food fiber sources, but as a soda substitute they are a genuine net positive.

The average Amazon shopper overpays by 15-25% on most purchases. Not because they are careless — but because Amazon's pricing system is designed to make you think today's price is the best price.

It usually is not.

Here are 7 proven strategies to always get the best price on Amazon — starting today.

Always Check Price History Before Buying

This is the single most important habit you can build as an Amazon shopper. Before you click buy on anything — check the price history.

Is today's price near the all time low? Buy now. Is it near the all time high? Wait. Is it average? Depends on your urgency.

2

Use Price Alerts Instead of Impulse Buying

Found something you want but the price is too high? Do not buy it and do not forget about it.

Set a price alert. Enter the price you want to pay and let the tool notify you when it gets there. This turns impulse buying into strategic buying.

Most products on Amazon will hit a lower price within 30-90 days if you are patient. The alert ensures you do not miss it.

Potential saving: 15-40% per purchase
3

Shop on Tuesday and Wednesday

Amazon's pricing algorithms adjust most aggressively at the start of the week. Products that saw low weekend demand often get price cuts on Tuesday and Wednesday to stimulate sales.

Weekend prices are consistently higher across most categories. If you can wait until mid-week you will often find better prices on the exact same products.

Potential saving: 3-8% per purchase
4

Buy Electronics When New Models Launch

This is one of the most reliable price drop triggers on Amazon. When a new iPhone launches the previous model drops significantly. When a new laptop generation releases last year's model gets heavily discounted.

For electronics always check if a new model is about to launch. If it is — wait for the launch and buy the previous model at the post-launch discount. You get nearly the same product for significantly less money.

Potential saving: 20-50% per purchase
5

Verify Black Friday Deals with Price History

Black Friday is not always what it seems on Amazon. Research consistently shows that many Black Friday deals involve prices that were artificially raised in the weeks before — then discounted back to normal price with a flashy percentage off tag.

Before buying any Black Friday deal check the price history for the previous 90 days. If the price was lower in October the Black Friday deal is not as good as it looks.

Potential saving: 10-25% per purchase
6

Buy Seasonal Items Off Season

This is one of the most consistent money saving strategies on Amazon. Prices for seasonal products drop dramatically when demand drops.

Buy summer garden furniture in September or October — often 40-60% cheaper than June prices. Buy winter coats in February or March. Buy Christmas decorations in January. Buy fans and air conditioners in autumn.

The products are identical. Only the timing changes.

Potential saving: 30-60% per purchase

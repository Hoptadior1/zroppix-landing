Black Friday 2026 Date and Cyber Monday 2026 Date

What is the lowest total you would pay today?

We search stores, coupons, cashback and open-box offers, and show only prices with a date on the page.

The dates are simple to work out, and the weeks around them are where most shoppers lose money. Here are the dates, how they are set, and what to do now.

⚡ Quick Answer

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27. It falls the day after U.S. Thanksgiving, which is the fourth Thursday of November (November 26, 2026). Cyber Monday 2026 is Monday, November 30. Retailers often start their sales earlier than these dates.

The dates

DayDate in 2026How it is set
Thanksgiving (U.S.)Thursday, November 26Fourth Thursday of November
Black FridayFriday, November 27The day after Thanksgiving
Cyber MondayMonday, November 30The Monday after Thanksgiving weekend

The dates are calendar arithmetic: November 1, 2026 is a Sunday, so the Thursdays in November are the 5th, 12th, 19th and 26th.

Do sales start earlier?

Often, yes. Many retailers run “early” Black Friday sales for days or weeks. We do not publish a start date for a specific store unless the store has announced one, and we date every offer we show. Treat any “starts in 3 hours” timer with caution. See our post on fake countdown timers.

What to do before November 27

  1. Write down the products you actually want. A short list beats browsing 500 deals.
  2. Record today’s price for each, with the date. A discount means little without a price from before the sale. Our price histories list dated, sourced prices.
  3. Set a target price for each product. A price you would happily pay is the only deal that matters.
  4. Read the return and price match rules of the store before you buy. See our store-by-store guide. Best Buy’s own page, for instance, excludes Black Friday pricing from matching.

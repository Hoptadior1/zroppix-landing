⚡ Quick Answer

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27. It falls the day after U.S. Thanksgiving, which is the fourth Thursday of November (November 26, 2026). Cyber Monday 2026 is Monday, November 30. Retailers often start their sales earlier than these dates.

The dates

DayDate in 2026How it is set
Thanksgiving (U.S.)Thursday, November 26Fourth Thursday of November
Black FridayFriday, November 27The day after Thanksgiving
Cyber MondayMonday, November 30The Monday after Thanksgiving weekend

The dates are calendar arithmetic. November 1, 2026 is a Sunday, so the Thursdays in November are the 5th, 12th, 19th and 26th, and the fourth is Thanksgiving. From November 1 there are 26 days to Black Friday.

Do sales start earlier?

Often, yes. Many retailers run “early” Black Friday sales for days or weeks, and several of the prices in the table above were published well before November 27. We don’t publish a start date for a specific store unless the store has announced one. Treat any “starts in 3 hours” timer with caution. See our post on fake countdown timers.

Is Black Friday really the lowest price?

Not always. For some products an earlier sale was lower. We looked at it in Is Black Friday the cheapest time to buy? Look at each product’s dated history instead of assuming.

Four mistakes that cost money

  1. Comparing the wrong model. A “Pro”, “Ultra” or larger size can be a different product with a different price.
  2. Trusting a “was” price. It may never have been charged. See how to tell if a deal is real.
  3. Ignoring the rules after you buy. Best Buy’s own page, for example, excludes Black Friday pricing from matching.
  4. Buying a refurbished unit as if it were new. See what the condition words mean.

What to do before November 27

  1. Write down the products you actually want. A short list beats browsing 500 deals.
  2. Record today’s price for each, with the date. Our price histories list dated, sourced prices.
  3. Set a target price for each product. A price you would happily pay is the only deal that matters.
  4. Read the return and price match rules of the store before you buy.

When is Black Friday in the next two years?

Black Friday follows Thanksgiving, the fourth Thursday of November. By the same arithmetic:

Why it matters: the number of shopping days between Black Friday and Christmas changes from year to year, which can change how retailers time their sales. We make no prediction from that. We only note that the dates move.

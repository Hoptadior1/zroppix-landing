Best Buy Price Match Guarantee 2026: What It Covers and What It Excludes

Every November, shoppers ask the same thing: if I buy at Best Buy and a rival is cheaper, or Best Buy’s own price drops, will they match it? We read two pages on Best Buy’s own site on November 1, 2026, the Price Match Guarantee page (effective October 30, 2026) and the return and exchange policy page, instead of relying on summaries. Policies change, so check both again before you rely on any detail here.

⚡ Quick Answer

Best Buy’s page says it will match "key online and local competitors" on new, immediately available items sold by Best Buy, and that most products sold and shipped between Oct. 30, 2026 and Dec. 31, 2026 can be matched through Jan. 15, 2027. Black Friday pricing, open-box, refurbished, clearance and Marketplace items are excluded.

What Best Buy says it will match

  • Competitors: “key online and local competitors” listed by Best Buy as Qualified Competitors. The list is on a separate page. If a competitor’s web price needs a membership login, Best Buy says matching is available only in store.
  • Online and in-store: online and app prices are matched on in-store purchases, and in-store prices are matched on online and app purchases.
  • If Best Buy lowers its own price: if it lowers an in-store, online or app price during the return and exchange period, it will match the lower price on request.
  • Only new, immediately available products sold by Best Buy.
  • Limit: one match per identical item, per customer, at the time of purchase, on the pre-tax price.

Two clocks: the price match window and the return window

The price match page says most products sold and shipped between October 30, 2026 and December 31, 2026 can be matched through January 15, 2027. The return page uses the same dates: purchases made October 30 through December 31 have an extended return period through January 15, 2027. Outside those dates the standard return window applies.

The same page says the policy applies to new, clearance, open-box, refurbished and pre-owned products. The price match page, however, excludes open-box, refurbished, clearance and pre-owned items from matching. They can be returned on the standard terms but not price matched.

What is excluded from price matching

  • Open-box, refurbished, clearance and pre-owned items
  • Marketplace products sold by third-party sellers, on BestBuy.com or on a competitor’s site
  • Black Friday pricing and special daily or hourly sales
  • Bundles, free gifts, coupons, gift card offers and financing
  • Pre-orders, contract cell phones, trade-in offers and service prices
  • Limited-quantity or out-of-stock items, and pricing errors
  • Membership Program pricing, and Best Buy Business and Education prices
  • Sales tax holidays: no match is given if the pre-tax price during the event is the same or higher than the original pre-tax purchase price.

Black Friday pricing is on the excluded list. A rival’s Black Friday price is not something you can count on Best Buy to match.

Worked examples with real dates

These use the dates on Best Buy’s pages. They are our date arithmetic, so confirm the details for your purchase with Best Buy.

  1. A TV bought on November 20, 2026. It falls inside the October 30 to December 31 purchase window, so the price match window runs to January 15, 2027. That is 56 days after purchase. If Best Buy lowers its own price during that period, the page says it will match the lower price on request.
  2. A phone bought on November 20, 2026. Activatable devices are excluded from the holiday extension and have a 14-day return window, so the clock is two weeks from the day you receive it. Check the price match page for whether a contract phone is eligible. It lists contract cell phones as excluded.
  3. A My Best Buy Plus member buying on December 10, 2026. That is on or after December 4, so the return window is 60 days from receipt, which is around February 8, 2027 if you received it that day. The January 15 date is the price match deadline on the other page, and the two are separate.
  4. An open-box item bought on November 25, 2026. It has the standard return terms but is excluded from price matching.

What this means before Black Friday (November 27, 2026)

Two separate things are easy to mix up. Best Buy’s page lists Black Friday pricing as excluded when you ask it to match another store’s price. Separately, the page says that if Best Buy lowers its own price during your return and exchange period, it will match the lower price on request. The page does not say how it treats its own limited-time event prices, so ask in store before you buy and keep the answer.

How to ask for a match

  1. Is the item new and sold by Best Buy itself, not a Marketplace seller?
  2. Is the rival on the Qualified Competitors list, and is its price for the identical item, in stock?
  3. Is the lower price a coupon, bundle, member price or a special-event price? If so, it is probably excluded.
  4. Screenshot the page with the date and time, and keep your receipt and order number.
  5. Ask in store or through Best Buy’s support, and note the name of the person who approves it.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming a rival’s Black Friday price will be matched. The page excludes it.
  • Waiting too long. The window runs from your purchase date, not from the sale date.
  • Buying an open-box or refurbished unit and expecting a match.
  • Confusing the price match deadline (January 15, 2027) with a member’s return deadline.

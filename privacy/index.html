Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

🛡️ Short version: We only collect product prices. Never your personal data. Never sold to anyone. Ever.

Who we are

Zroppix is a Chrome extension that helps users track Amazon prices and know exactly when to buy. Our website is zroppix.com and you can contact us at hello@zroppix.com.

What data we collect

When you use the Zroppix Chrome extension we collect:

  • Product names from Amazon pages you visit
  • Product prices from Amazon pages you visit
  • Product URLs from Amazon pages you visit

We do NOT collect:

  • Your name or personal information
  • Your browsing history
  • Your purchase history
  • Your Amazon account information
  • Any financial information
  • Any information from pages other than Amazon product pages

How we use this data

Price data is used solely to:

  • Build price history for products
  • Generate price drop predictions
  • Show you price trends and analysis

All price data is anonymous and is never linked to any individual user.

Price alerts and email

If you choose to set a price alert we collect your email address. We use this email solely to send you the price alert you requested. We never use your email for marketing, promotions or any other purpose without your explicit consent.

Data sharing and third parties

We never sell your data to third parties. Ever. We use the following services to operate Zroppix:

  • Supabase — secure database storage
  • Vercel — website and API hosting
  • Resend — sending price alert emails

These services are used solely to operate Zroppix and are bound by their own privacy policies and data protection agreements.

Data retention

Price data is retained indefinitely to improve prediction accuracy. If you set a price alert your email is retained until the alert is triggered or you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of any email address we hold
  • Unsubscribe from price alerts at any time
  • Contact us with any privacy questions or concerns

Cookies

Zroppix does not use cookies on its website or in its extension.

Children

Zroppix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at: hello@zroppix.com

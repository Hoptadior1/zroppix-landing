The Core Question: Is Switch 2 Worth $150 More?

Nintendo Switch 2 launched in 2025 at $449.99. The Switch OLED, which launched in 2021, is currently $299.99 — down from its original $349.99 launch price. The $150 price difference is real and meaningful, but whether it is justified depends entirely on what you plan to play and how you plan to play it.

The Switch 2's genuine advantages over the OLED are: significantly better graphics performance in docked mode, a larger 7.9-inch screen vs the OLED's 7-inch screen, and the ability to play Switch 2 exclusive titles that require the new hardware. The OLED's advantages are: it costs $150 less, it plays the entire existing Switch library of hundreds of games, and it is a refined, polished product that has been on the market for four years.

Who Should Buy the Switch OLED Right Now

Buy the Switch OLED if you primarily want to play the existing Nintendo back catalog — Mario Kart 8, Zelda: Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom, Animal Crossing, Pokémon, and hundreds of other titles. All of these play on the OLED. At $299.99 it is a complete gaming system with a massive library and zero compromises for anyone whose wishlist doesn't include Switch 2 exclusives.

The Switch OLED also makes sense as a gift or as a second console for a household. At $150 less than Switch 2, a parent buying a console for a child is getting genuinely equivalent gameplay value for most titles at a significantly lower price.

Who Should Buy Switch 2 Instead

Switch 2 is worth the premium if you specifically want to play Switch 2 exclusive titles, if you prioritize the improved docked performance for TV gaming, or if you are a serious Nintendo fan who wants the latest hardware. The Switch 2 exclusives library is still growing — if you care about being on the latest platform as new titles release over the next 4-5 years, the Switch 2 is the right investment.

Will the Switch OLED Get Even Cheaper?

The Switch OLED has been available since 2021 and has only seen significant discounts during major sale events — Black Friday and occasional promotional periods. With Switch 2 now out, there is pressure on Nintendo to discount the OLED more aggressively, but Nintendo is historically reluctant to cut hardware prices. $299.99 is already $50 below its launch price, which is meaningful for Nintendo hardware. Prime Day on June 15th could bring it to $279-289 but that is not guaranteed. The current $299.99 price is already at the lower end of its historical range.

Why 5 Million People Use This Thing

MEATER's core pitch is simple: 100% wireless, dual-sensor accuracy. One sensor in the probe tracks internal meat temperature up to 212°F while a second sensor simultaneously monitors ambient cooking temperature up to 527°F — meaning it works identically well in a smoker, oven, grill, air fryer, or rotisserie without needing a different tool for each cooking method. Over 5 million users and 80 million logged cooks back up that this isn't a gimmick product.

💡 The 165ft Range Is the Actual Selling PointMEATER Plus's charging base includes a built-in Bluetooth repeater that extends wireless range up to 165 feet — genuinely far enough to monitor a smoker in the backyard from inside the house, or step away from a long cook without losing connection. This is the specific feature that separates the Plus model from the base MEATER, and it's the reason most buyers upgrade to it.

The Guided Cook System Takes the Guesswork Out

Rather than just displaying raw numbers, the MEATER app's Guided Cook System walks you through the entire process — select your protein and desired doneness, and the app's Advanced Estimator Algorithm calculates how long to cook and rest your food, then sends real-time alerts as you approach your target temperature. For anyone who's pulled a roast too early or let a steak go past medium by accident, this structured guidance is the actual value, not just the raw temperature reading.

MEATER Plus vs. the Rest of the Lineup

The base MEATER model has standard Bluetooth range without the repeater boost — fine for cooking right next to the grill, less useful if you want to walk away. The MEATER Pro and Pro Duo step up to 1000°F heat resistance (enough for open-flame grilling) and dual probes for cooking multiple cuts simultaneously, at a meaningfully higher price. For the vast majority of home grilling and roasting, the Plus model's 527°F ambient ceiling and single probe cover the job.

✅ Who Should Buy ThisThis is close to essential for anyone who grills or roasts regularly and has a track record of over- or under-cooking meat — the dual-sensor accuracy and guided app genuinely eliminate the guesswork. If you only cook meat a handful of times a year, a basic $15 instant-read thermometer covers the essentials for less.

Care and Maintenance

The probe is dishwasher safe, which matters more than it sounds — most competing wireless thermometers require careful hand-washing around exposed electronics. Keep the charging base's Bluetooth repeater positioned with a clear line of sight to where you're cooking for the most reliable 165ft range, since walls and large obstacles can reduce effective distance.

Why Amazon Is Discounting a 3-Month-Old MacBook Air by $200

When Amazon discounts a current-generation Apple product by $200 within 3 months of launch, it signals one of two things — either heavy inventory or an aggressive Prime Day strategy to drive MacBook buyers to Amazon rather than the Apple Store. In this case it appears to be both. Amazon has explicitly positioned MacBook Air as a Prime Day hero product for 2026, which means the $899 current price is not the floor.

The MacBook Air M3 trajectory provides the clearest precedent: launched at $1,099, discounted to $999 within months, then hit $849 at Prime Day 2025. The M5 is already at $899 — which is where the M3 was four months before it hit $849. If the pattern holds, Prime Day June 23 brings the M5 to $849-879.

What the M5 adds over M4 that makes it worth choosing: Wi-Fi 7 support provides theoretical 2.4x faster wireless speeds on compatible routers. Bluetooth 6 delivers lower latency for audio peripherals. The M5 Neural Engine is measurably faster for AI-assisted tasks in apps like Final Cut Pro, Logic, and the growing suite of Apple Intelligence features. None of these are dramatic day-to-day differences — but for a product you will use for 5-7 years, the newer chip provides a longer runway.

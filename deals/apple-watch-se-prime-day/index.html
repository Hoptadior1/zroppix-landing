Which Apple Watch SE are you looking at

This is the first thing to check, because the SE range has changed. Apple introduced the Apple Watch SE 3 in September 2025 alongside the Series 11. According to MacRumors' roundup, it has the S10 chip, an always-on display (new to the SE), sleep apnea alerts and wrist temperature sensing, 64GB of storage, fast charging, and launched from $249 for the 40mm and $279 for the 44mm. It does not have ECG, blood oxygen or hypertension alerts (MacRumors).

The previous model, the SE 2, had the S8 chip and 32GB of storage. Listings for it may still appear, and the two look almost the same, so read the title and the model details carefully before comparing prices. These are launch figures and may have changed, so check Apple's current page.

SE or Series: which fits

  • The SE suits people who want an Apple Watch for notifications, workouts, sleep tracking and safety features at a lower price.
  • The Series 11 adds the health sensors the SE lacks, per Apple's specs. If those matter to you, check the current price gap.
  • The older SE 2 can make sense only if the saving against the SE 3 is large enough to give up the newer chip and always-on display.
  • An Android phone? An Apple Watch needs an iPhone. If you don't have one, look at Fitbit or Garmin instead.

The watch is not a medical device and we are not giving health advice. If you need a particular health sensor for a medical reason, ask your doctor which device is right for you.

  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.

  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

Compare the whole offer

Apple Watch listings differ by size, case material, GPS or cellular, and band. A cheaper listing may be a different size or the older model, and cellular models need a carrier plan to be useful. Check who sells it (Amazon, Apple or a third party), whether it is new or refurbished, and what the return window and warranty are. Apple also runs its own refurbished store, so compare it with that.

Common mistakes when buying an Apple Watch

  • Buying the wrong generation. The SE 2 and SE 3 look alike.
  • Comparing different sizes or cellular options. Match the exact variant.
  • Trusting a "was" price. Compare against recorded price history.
  • Forgetting the iPhone requirement. It works with an iPhone only.
  • Ignoring the seller. Check the warranty and returns.

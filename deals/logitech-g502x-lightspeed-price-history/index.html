Why the G502 Lineage Is the Most Popular Gaming Mouse Ever Made

The original Logitech G502 launched in 2014 and became the bestselling gaming mouse in Amazon history, accumulating over 100,000 reviews. Twelve years later, the G502 X Lightspeed is the current wireless flagship evolution of that same shape — keeping the ergonomic right-handed form factor that made the original famous but adding LIGHTSPEED wireless technology, LIGHTFORCE hybrid optical-mechanical switches, and the HERO 25K sensor with tracking precision to the sub-micron level.

The fact that this specific design has sold at volume for over a decade tells you something important: it works. The right-handed sculpted grip, the 13 programmable buttons, the adjustable weight system, and the thumb buttons are all positioned where experienced gamers expect them. There is very little learning curve if you are upgrading from any previous G502 model.

G502 X Lightspeed vs G502 X Plus — Which to Buy

Logitech sells two wireless G502 X models. The G502 X Lightspeed at $109.99 has no RGB lighting and a 130-hour battery life. The G502 X Plus adds RGB lighting and POWERPLAY wireless charging compatibility but costs $159.99 and has shorter battery life. For most players the Lightspeed is the better choice — the 130-hour battery means you almost never think about charging, and the lack of RGB is irrelevant if the mouse is on a mousepad rather than on display.

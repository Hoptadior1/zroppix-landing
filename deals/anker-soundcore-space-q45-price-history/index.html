📊 Consistently Priced Below the $150 List

Independent tracking shows the Space Q45 selling around $139.99 on Amazon as recently as June 2026, positioning it in the bottom 11% of headphones by price relative to its feature set according to comparative research. This isn't a rare flash discount — it's close to the everyday street price.

Real ANC, Not Marketing ANC

Independent reviewers testing the Space Q45 on public transit found the noise cancellation did a genuinely adequate job blocking train and traffic noise — the caveat being fit matters, since gaps between the ear padding and skull noticeably reduce effectiveness. Where it competes well is call quality: two microphones with an AI algorithm specifically detect and amplify voice, making it a solid pick for video calls or voice chat in a noisy dorm.

💡 Where the Q45 Doesn't Match Premium HeadphonesReviewers are consistent that sound quality is the Q45's weakest point — described as "ok" rather than exceptional, with sub-bass slightly exaggerated and higher frequencies occasionally sounding hissy compared to more expensive reference headphones. For casual listening and calls this is a non-issue; for critical, detail-focused music listening, the gap to $300+ headphones is real.

50 Hours Means You Basically Never Charge It Mid-Semester

With ANC active, the Space Q45 delivers up to 50 hours of playtime — enough for a full around-the-world flight, or realistically, multiple weeks of daily studying and commuting between charges. A 5-minute quick charge adds roughly 4 hours back, useful for a between-classes top-up if the battery does run low.

Comfort Over Long Sessions

The metal-reinforced headband with adjustable give and swiveling, foldable ear cups make the Q45 genuinely comfortable for multi-hour library sessions, according to hands-on testing — though the pleather ear cups run less breathable than premium fabric alternatives, worth factoring in for hot climates or long wear without breaks.

✅ Who Should Buy ThisThis is a strong fit for students who need real noise blocking for studying, commuting, or flights without spending flagship money — the ANC and call quality genuinely compete above their price point. If pristine audio fidelity for critical music listening is the priority, budget for a pricier reference-quality pair instead.

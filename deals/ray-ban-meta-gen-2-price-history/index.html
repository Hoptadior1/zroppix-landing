Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 Key Features — What You Actually Get

The Gen 2 camera shoots 3K Ultra HD still photos and 60fps video — a significant upgrade over the Gen 1's 1080p. The 12MP ultra-wide camera captures a broader field of view than a standard phone camera, which is useful for travel photography and content creation where you want context around your subject. Photos and videos save to your phone via the Meta View app, where they can be shared directly to Instagram, Facebook, or any other platform. The hands-free nature of the camera — no need to pull out a phone, no obvious lens pointing at a subject — produces more natural photographs and video than any smartphone can.

The five-microphone array with beamforming delivers clear call quality even in windy or noisy environments. Callers consistently report better audio quality compared to AirPods in outdoor settings — the microphones are positioned closer to the face and use directional filtering to isolate your voice from background noise. For anyone who makes calls while commuting, walking, or working outdoors, the call quality improvement over earbuds is meaningful.

Meta AI's Live translation feature in Gen 2 covers English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, and additional languages added in the 2026 software updates. The translation works by hearing the conversation in real time and speaking the translation into your ear with a delay of 1–3 seconds — fast enough for conversational use, not fast enough for rapid exchanges. It is the most practical real-time translation system available in any consumer device in 2026 and works without phone connectivity once the language pack is downloaded.

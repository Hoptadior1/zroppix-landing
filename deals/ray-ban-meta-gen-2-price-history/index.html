How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

Match the style, the lenses and the list price

  • Style: the Wayfarer lists at $379 and the Skyler at $409 in these reports.
  • Lenses: clear, tinted, blue-light and Transitions lenses are priced differently.
  • Generation: the Gen 1 is a cheaper, older model. Check the title says Gen 2.
  • Seller: check that Amazon or Meta sells it, and that it's new.

We haven't tested the glasses. Read independent reviews for camera quality and battery life.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Comparing different styles. The Wayfarer and Skyler have different list prices.
  • Mixing up Gen 1 and Gen 2.
  • Using an expired price. Both the May and November prices ended.
  • Picking lenses after the price. Lens type changes the price.

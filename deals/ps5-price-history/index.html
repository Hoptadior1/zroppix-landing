PS5 price timeline: every change we could source

Sony raised prices in August 2025 and again on April 2, 2026. One later cut is reported but not confirmed.

  • August 21, 2025: PlayStation Blog (August 20, 2025) set the disc PS5 at $549.99, the Digital Edition at $499.99 and the PS5 Pro at $749.99.
  • April 2, 2026: PlayStation Blog (March 27, 2026) set the disc PS5 at $649.99, the Digital Edition at $599.99, the PS5 Pro at $899.99 and the PlayStation Portal at $249.99. CNBC (March 27, 2026) described it as a rise of up to $150.
  • August 21, 2026 (not confirmed): tech-insider.org says a US price cut back to $549.99 disc and $499.99 Digital took effect. The article gives no link to a Sony post and contradicts itself on scope, and we could not find Sony's own announcement. Treat it as unverified.
  • October 2025 offer (expired): Notebookcheck (October 23, 2025) reported a PlayStation Direct code that took the disc PS5 to $424.99 through October 31, 2025.
  • We found no source for today's Amazon price.

How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the PlayStation 5 on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

Work out what you'd really pay

  • Disc or Digital: the Digital Edition is $50 cheaper at Sony's list price but has no disc drive.
  • Bundles: a console bundle can beat buying the game separately. Add up both.
  • Pro: see our PS5 Pro price page if you want the $899.99 model.
  • Seller and date: check Amazon is the seller and that the listing price is not an old one.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Quoting 2025 prices. The $549.99 figure was Sony's list price until April 2, 2026.
  • Trusting an unconfirmed cut. Check the live listing.
  • Paying a third-party markup.
  • Missing the Digital Edition. It is $50 less if you don't need discs.

What Is the Kindle Colorsoft and Why Does It Cost $279.99?

The Kindle Colorsoft is Amazon's first e-reader with a colour display — launched in late 2025 after years of Kindle being exclusively black and white. The colour display uses Paperwhite colour technology that Amazon developed to deliver a reading experience closer to printed books while still maintaining the eye-friendly properties that make e-ink superior to LCD for long reading sessions. The 7-inch display supports 16 levels of colour with adjustable warm light and is readable in direct sunlight — something colour LCD tablets cannot match.

The $279.99 price reflects this being a genuinely new technology. For comparison, the standard Kindle Paperwhite is $159.99 and the Kindle Scribe is $339.99. The Colorsoft sits in the middle as the premium reading-focused device for buyers who want colour illustrations in comics, cookbooks, magazines, and children's books without moving to a full tablet.

Kindle Colorsoft vs Kindle Paperwhite — Which Should You Buy?

If colour is not important to your reading — novels, non-fiction, most text-heavy content — the Kindle Paperwhite at $134.99 is currently at one of its best prices of the year and is a significantly better value than the Colorsoft at $279.99. The Paperwhite has slightly better battery life, a lighter weight, and the same excellent e-ink reading experience for black and white content.

Buy the Colorsoft if you specifically read comics, manga, illustrated cookbooks, children's books, or magazines where colour adds genuine value to the reading experience. For those use cases the Colorsoft is the best e-reader ever made. For everything else, the Paperwhite saves you $145.

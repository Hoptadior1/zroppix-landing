PS5 Pro Price History — Why Prime Day Is the First Opportunity to Save

No holiday promotion. No bundle discount. $699.99 has been the price from day one. This makes Prime Day June 23-26 the first realistic opportunity for a meaningful discount. Sony has confirmed Prime Day participation and the PS5 base model dropped to $349.99 at Prime Day 2025 — establishing that Sony does discount aggressively at Prime Day once a console has been in market for a year.

The PS5 Pro features the same CPU as the base PS5 but with a GPU that is 45% faster, enabling PS5 Pro Enhanced mode on supported titles — a native 4K 60fps experience that was previously only possible with quality mode sacrifices. Ray tracing performance is 2-3x faster than the standard PS5. For anyone with a 4K TV who plays graphically demanding titles, the Pro delivers a genuinely different experience. Is the PS5 Pro Worth $599-649 at Prime Day?

Frequently Asked Questions
Has the PS5 Pro ever been on sale?+
No — the PS5 Pro has never been discounted at any retailer since its November 2024 launch. $699.99 is the only price it has ever been. Prime Day June 23-26 2026 is expected to deliver the first ever discount, estimated at $599-649.
What is PS5 Pro Enhanced mode?+
PS5 Pro Enhanced is a certification for games that have been updated to take advantage of the PS5 Pro's faster GPU. Enhanced titles run at higher resolutions (typically native 4K), higher frame rates (60fps in modes that previously ran at 30fps), and with better ray tracing quality. Over 50 titles are PS5 Pro Enhanced at launch.
PS5 Pro vs PS5 — is the upgrade worth it?+
The PS5 Pro upgrade is worth it if you have a 4K 120Hz TV and play PS5 Pro Enhanced titles like Spider-Man 2, Gran Turismo 7, or Ratchet & Clank. If you have a 1080p TV or primarily play indie games the performance difference is not visible and the base PS5 at $449.99 is the better choice.
Does the PS5 Pro play all PS5 games?+
Yes — the PS5 Pro is fully backward compatible with all PS5 and PS4 games. PS5 Pro Enhanced titles run with improved performance on the Pro. All other titles run identically to the standard PS5.

PS5 Pro Price History — What Actually Happened

The PlayStation 5 Pro launched on November 7, 2024 at $699.99. Contrary to a common assumption that it has "never been discounted," it received a genuine first-ever price cut during PlayStation's Days of Play sale in May 2025 — dropping to $649.99, confirmed by Tom's Guide's live coverage of the event.

The picture changed in 2026. Sony officially announced a price increase across the PS5 lineup, effective April 2, 2026, via PlayStation.Blog, citing global economic pressures. Multiple independent outlets confirm this hike happened, though they report different resulting PS5 Pro prices depending on publication date — some cite figures around $749.99, others around $899.99, suggesting either regional variation, a second increase later in 2026, or reporting at different points in a volatile pricing period. We're not going to assert a single confident number here — check the live price before buying.

What is clear: at the most recent Days of Play sale (May 27–June 10, 2026), Sony explicitly did not discount PS5 consoles, confirmed by GameSpot's coverage — a notable reversal from the 2025 event that gave the PS5 Pro its first-ever cut.

⚠️

Don't assume the PS5 Pro is at a fixed, unchanging price, and don't assume Prime Day will automatically deliver a discount — Sony's most recent major sale event (Days of Play 2026) explicitly skipped console discounts. Check the live price and treat any specific historical figure here as a dated reference point, not a current offer.

PS5 Pro vs Xbox Series X — 2026 Pricing Context

Console pricing has been unusually volatile industry-wide in 2026. Microsoft confirmed via Xbox Wire (June 25, 2026) that Xbox Series X pricing rises to $799.99 (disc) / $749.99 (digital) effective August 1, 2026, citing similar component cost pressures — memory and storage prices affecting the whole industry. Both companies have cited a global memory/DRAM shortage as a factor. This means both PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X pricing may look different depending on exactly when you're reading this — check current listings for both before deciding.

Is the PS5 Pro Worth It?

The PS5 Pro's GPU advantage is real and visible in PS5 Pro Enhanced titles, delivering better resolution, ray tracing, or frame rates than the standard PS5 in supported games. Whether that's worth the price premium depends on your display (a 4K TV is necessary to see the benefit clearly) and how much you value the specific titles that support Enhanced mode. Given how much console pricing has moved in 2026, the honest advice is to compare the live PS5 Pro price against the standard PS5 and Xbox Series X before deciding — the value equation between them has shifted more than once this year.

💡

The PS5 Pro does not include a disc drive — it's a separate accessory purchase. If you buy physical games, factor that cost in when comparing total price against the Xbox Series X, which includes a disc drive in the box.

Frequently Asked Questions
Has the PS5 Pro ever been on sale?+
Yes. The PS5 Pro got its first-ever discount during PlayStation's Days of Play sale in May 2025, dropping from $699.99 to $649.99, confirmed by Tom's Guide. Since then, Sony raised PS5 Pro pricing multiple times in 2026 due to component cost pressures, and at the most recent Days of Play event (May 2026), Sony explicitly did not discount PS5 consoles.
What is the current price of the PS5 Pro?+
PS5 Pro pricing has changed multiple times in 2026 following Sony's April 2026 global price increase, with different sources reporting figures ranging from roughly $749.99 to $899.99 depending on when they were published. Check the live price on Amazon rather than assume a specific figure, since pricing has been unusually volatile this year.
Why did Sony raise PS5 and PS5 Pro prices in 2026?+
Sony cited global economic pressures and component costs, widely reported as tied to tariffs and a memory/DRAM shortage affecting the broader electronics industry. Sony announced the price changes officially via PlayStation.Blog, effective April 2, 2026. Microsoft followed with its own Xbox Series X/S price increases effective August 1, 2026, citing similar component cost pressures.
What is PS5 Pro Enhanced mode?+
PS5 Pro Enhanced is Sony's certification for games updated to take advantage of the PS5 Pro's more powerful GPU, typically delivering higher resolution, better ray tracing, and/or higher frame rates than the same game running on a standard PS5.
PS5 Pro vs PS5 standard — is the upgrade worth it?+
The PS5 Pro is worth considering if you have a 4K TV and play PS5 Pro Enhanced titles where the extra GPU power is visible. If you have a 1080p TV or mostly play less graphically demanding games, the price difference over a standard PS5 is harder to justify.
Does the PS5 Pro include a disc drive?+
No. The PS5 Pro is digital-only out of the box; the Ultra HD Blu-ray disc drive is sold separately as an add-on. Check the current price of the disc drive separately, as accessory pricing has also been affected by the same 2026 cost increases affecting the consoles themselves.

Club ball vs Pro ball: the tiers explained

adidas sells each tournament ball in a ladder of tiers. Names change slightly from season to season, but the pattern is consistent:

  • Pro is the replica of the ball used in the actual matches, built with the premium construction the tournament uses. It costs noticeably more and is aimed at match play and collectors.
  • Competition / League sit in the middle: closer to the match feel, still built for regular play.
  • Club is the everyday version. It carries the same Champions League graphics but is built to take a beating in training and casual games.
  • Mini and Training versions are for skills practice, young kids and display.

For most buyers the Club ball is the sensible pick: you get the look and a ball that holds up to regular use, without paying for match-level construction you will not notice at the park. If you play competitive fixtures, check your league's rules first, because some require specific approved balls.

Which size should you buy?

Ball size is set by age. Buying the wrong one is the single most common mistake with kids' gifts, so it is worth a minute.

Size 5About 13 and up, adultsThe standard full-size match ball. The Laws of the Game set it at roughly 68–70 cm around and about 410–450 g.
Size 4About 8 to 12Common for youth leagues. Many leagues specify it, so check yours.

If a child is a strong or tall player near an age boundary, ask their coach which size the team uses. Do not "buy up" a size for them to grow into: a ball that is too heavy hurts their technique and can sting their feet.

What to check on the Amazon listing

This is where most disappointments happen. Amazon often puts several sizes, seasons and sellers on one page. Before you pay, check:

  1. The size selected. The page can default to a different size from the one you meant.
  2. The season or design. The Champions League ball design changes every season, and older designs linger in listings. The title or images should show the one you want.
  3. Who sells and ships it. "Ships from and sold by Amazon" or the official adidas store is safest. Third-party sellers are fine if they have strong feedback, but they are where fakes show up.
  4. Whether it is inflated. Many balls arrive flat or part-inflated. You will need a pump (see "Complete the kit" below).
  5. The return window for that exact listing, in case the size or design is not what you expected.

How to spot a fake ball

Counterfeit tournament balls are common because the graphics are so recognisable. Warning signs:

  • A price far below every other listing for the same ball.
  • Blurry, smudged or off-centre printing, and logos that look slightly wrong.
  • Uneven stitching or panels that do not sit flush.
  • No clear seller identity, or a seller with very little history.
  • A ball that feels hard and plasticky or loses air very quickly.

Compare the product photos with the ball on adidas's own site and keep your order confirmation. If it arrives looking wrong, return it inside the window rather than keeping it.

Inflation and care: making it last

  • Check the pressure printed near the valve. Many balls state a recommended range (commonly around 8 to 12 PSI). Do not guess or overinflate.
  • Wet the needle with a little water before pushing it into the valve. A dry needle can damage the valve, which is the part that lets air out for good.
  • Clean it with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid solvents.
  • Keep it out of heat. A ball left in a hot car or by a radiator can warp and lose shape.
  • Match the surface to the ball. Grass and good 3G/4G turf are kind to it. Concrete and gravel eat any ball's outer layer in weeks.
  • For storage, let a little air out if it will sit unused for weeks, and keep it somewhere dry.

Buying it as a gift

A Champions League ball is an easy gift for a fan because the design is instantly recognisable. Pick the size for the player, not the fan: a size 5 for a teenager who plays, a smaller size for a younger child. Pair it with a pump and needles so it is usable straight out of the box, and consider a ball bag. If you are buying from far away, choose a listing with free returns in case it arrives in the wrong size.

Frequently asked questions

Is the adidas Champions League Club ball the official match ball?

No. The official match-quality version is the Pro tier. The Club ball uses the same graphics but is built for training and everyday games.

What size football do adults use?

Size 5 is the standard full-size ball for teenagers and adults. Size 4 is typical for ages 8 to 12 and size 3 for younger children.

Can I use the Club ball in league matches?

It depends on your league. Some require a specific size or an approved ball, so check the rules before you buy.

How do I know the ball is real?

Buy a listing sold and shipped by Amazon or adidas, compare the print and stitching with adidas's own site, and be wary of prices far below every other listing.

Why did my ball arrive flat?

Many balls ship deflated or part-inflated to save space. Inflate it with a pump and a wetted needle to the pressure printed by the valve.

Does the design change every year?

Yes. The Champions League ball gets a new design each season, so confirm the season on the listing if you want a specific one.

How long does a football last?

With regular play on grass or turf, a good ball lasts a long time. On concrete or gravel it can wear out within weeks.

Get the right size, the right ball

The Club ball is the best value route to the Champions League look. Check the size and seller on the listing, add a pump, and you are set for the weekend.

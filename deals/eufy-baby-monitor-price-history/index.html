Setting Up the Eufy Baby Monitor — What New Parents Need to Know

Setup takes under 5 minutes out of the box. Power on the camera and parent unit — they pair automatically. Position the camera on a dresser or shelf angled toward the crib, or use the included mount to attach it to a wall or crib rail at the recommended safe distance of at least 3 feet from the baby. The pan and tilt range of 330° horizontal and 110° vertical covers any standard nursery from a corner position without needing to reposition the camera.

For WiFi connectivity and remote monitoring via the eufy Security app, connect the camera to your 2.4GHz home WiFi during initial setup. The app enables live viewing when you are away from home — at work, running errands, or in another part of the house out of parent unit range. The app also receives push notifications for motion and sound detection. The local direct-signal monitoring via the parent unit continues to function regardless of internet connectivity — if your WiFi goes down at 2am, the parent unit still shows a live feed.

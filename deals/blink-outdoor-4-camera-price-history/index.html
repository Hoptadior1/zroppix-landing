Blink Outdoor 4 vs Ring Spotlight Cam — Which Security Camera Should You Buy?

The Blink Outdoor 4 at $39-49 at Prime Day and the Ring Spotlight Cam at $59-79 serve different use cases. The Blink Outdoor 4 runs on AA batteries (2-year life), connects to the Blink app, and requires a Sync Module 2 for local storage. It is ideal for easy installation anywhere without running wires. The Ring Spotlight Cam has built-in spotlight, siren, and integrates deeply with Ring's security ecosystem.

For most users who want outdoor security cameras without wiring complexity, the Blink Outdoor 4 at Prime Day pricing is the best value available. At $39-49 for a weatherproof, 2-year battery camera with motion alerts it is difficult to beat.

Does the Blink Outdoor 4 Require a Subscription?

The Blink Outdoor 4 works without a subscription — motion alerts and live view are free. The optional Blink Subscription Plan at $3/month per device or $10/month for unlimited devices adds cloud video storage, so you can review footage from the last 60 days. Without a subscription you can still view live footage and receive motion alerts, but clips are not saved automatically.

For most users the free tier is sufficient. Install the camera, set up motion zones, and you receive instant phone alerts when motion is detected. Add the subscription only if you need to review recorded footage of past events.

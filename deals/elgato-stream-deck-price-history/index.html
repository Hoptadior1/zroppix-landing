Which Stream Deck is right for you

Elgato's line is easy to confuse. Compare these before you pay:

  • Stream Deck Mini: 6 keys. Fine for a few scenes and a mute button.
  • Stream Deck MK.2: 15 keys. The usual choice for streaming, editing and shortcuts.
  • Stream Deck +: 8 keys, 4 dials and a touch strip. Aimed at audio and video work.

Each key shows its own icon, so you can trigger scene changes, mute your mic, launch apps or run multi-step macros with one tap. The Stream Deck app runs on Windows and macOS, so check Elgato's system requirements for your version. You don't need to stream to use one: editors and developers use them for shortcuts.

How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the Bambu Lab A1 on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

Compare the printer-only price with the combo

  • Printer or combo: the combo adds the AMS lite for multi-colour printing. Compare like with like.
  • Where it's sold: BambuHub tracks Bambu's own store. Compare with the Amazon listing and its seller.
  • Size: the A1 is the larger printer. The A1 mini is smaller and cheaper.
  • Filament: a printer needs it. Check what's included.

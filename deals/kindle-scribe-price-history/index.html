Which Kindle Scribe is this

The Scribe line has changed, which matters because older price histories (including ours before this rewrite) mix generations. According to Android Authority's launch coverage, Amazon's refreshed line has three models with 11-inch screens: the Scribe with a front light (reported at $499.99), a cheaper Scribe without a front light ($429.99) and the Scribe Colorsoft ($629.99). The earlier Scribe had a 10.2-inch screen. Those are launch figures and may have changed, so confirm the exact model on the listing before you compare prices.

Is a Scribe for you

  • Yes, if you want to write on your e-reader: annotating books and documents, taking handwritten notes and reading PDFs on a larger screen.
  • No, if you only read. A Paperwhite costs far less.
  • Consider other options if you want a note-taking tablet with apps. A Scribe is deliberately limited.

Check Amazon's current page for what pen is included and what the note-taking features are, since they have changed between generations.

How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the Kindle Scribe on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

Compare the whole cost

With a Scribe, compare the exact model, the storage size and what comes in the box. A premium pen or a folio cover can add noticeably to the cost, so price the setup you actually want. Amazon runs promotions at various times, and we can't predict when. If a past low is quoted, check which generation it applies to.

Common mistakes when buying a Scribe

  • Using an old price history. Generations differ.
  • Buying the wrong model. Check the front light and the colour option.
  • Forgetting accessories. Check the pen and cover.
  • Buying one just to read. A Paperwhite is cheaper.
  • Trusting a "was" price. Compare against recorded history.

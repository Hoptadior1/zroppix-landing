What's in the box

According to AOL, the charger comes with the magnetic mount, an air vent clip, a dashboard anchor and a 3.3-foot USB-C to USB-C cable. It does not include a car charging adapter, and AOL said you need to supply one with at least 20W output. AOL also described it as Qi2 certified, which is what delivers the 15W speed on supported phones. Check that your phone supports Qi2 or MagSafe charging, and that your case allows the magnet to connect.

Budget for the adapter when you compare prices: a $20 charger plus a separate adapter can cost more than the headline price.

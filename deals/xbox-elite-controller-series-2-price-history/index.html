Elite Series 2 vs Elite Series 2 Core — Pricing Correction

It's a common assumption that the "Core" variant of the Elite Series 2 is meaningfully cheaper than the full version, similar to how "Core" versions of other Xbox controllers work. That's not clearly the case here: Microsoft's own store lists the full Elite Wireless Controller Series 2 at $199.99, and a March 2026 Kotaku deal report on the Core variant cites its MSRP as $200 as well — with a 20%-off sale bringing it to $160. If you're choosing between the two specifically for the price difference, check current listings closely, since the gap may be smaller than expected.

The functional difference between the two is primarily which components ship in the box — the full Series 2 typically includes the complete set of interchangeable paddles, thumbsticks, and the charging case, while Core variants ship with a reduced component set. If component completeness matters to you, that's the real differentiator, not necessarily price.

💡

The Elite Series 2's main upgrades over a standard Xbox controller: adjustable-tension thumbsticks, interchangeable paddles and D-pads, shorter hair-trigger locks, and up to 40 hours of battery life via USB-C rechargeable battery — versus AA batteries or a smaller built-in battery on the standard controller.

Is the Elite Controller Series 2 Worth It?

For competitive players or anyone who plays for extended sessions, the adjustable components and extended battery life are genuine upgrades that show up in daily use. For casual players, the standard Xbox Wireless Controller at a much lower price covers most needs without the premium.

What is the price of the Xbox Elite Controller Series 2?+

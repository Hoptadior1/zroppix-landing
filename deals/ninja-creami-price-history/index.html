What Does the Ninja CREAMi Deluxe Actually Do?

The Ninja CREAMi Deluxe uses Creamify Technology — a process where you freeze a liquid base solid in the 24oz XL tub, then the dual-drive motors drive a precision paddle downward through the frozen block, shaving ice particles into a smooth, creamy texture. The result is ice cream, gelato, sorbet, or frozen yogurt that is genuinely scoopable and creamy — not icy — because the shaving process creates far smaller ice crystals than a traditional freezer.

The Deluxe NC501 has 11 one-touch programs: Ice Cream, Sorbet, Gelato, Frozen Yogurt, Milkshake, Smoothie Bowl, Italian Ice, Slushi, Creamiccino, Frozen Drink, and Mix-In. The Mix-In program lets you fold in chocolate chips, nuts, fruit pieces, or candy after processing. The Re-Spin function runs a second cycle if the first pass is too firm. Every part except the motor base is dishwasher safe.

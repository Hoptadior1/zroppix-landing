What changed since our last update

The page we replaced said the Apple TV 4K was "$129 right now," with the 128GB at $149, "stable" and rarely discounted. That is no longer true. MacRumors (October 13, 2026) reported that Apple's new 4th-generation Apple TV 4K has a single configuration, 128GB with Wi-Fi 7 and Ethernet, at $299 in the US. It said the previous model's two configurations were most recently $199 and $249 after an earlier price increase, and that the starting price had gone from $129 to $299 this year. Apple's store page lists the new model for pre-order, available from November 13.

Why check the history before buying an Apple TV 4K now

Apple changed the price twice this year and is about to replace the model, so an old price tells you little.

  • The starting price went from $129 to $299 this year, per MacRumors: $170 more, or about 132% (our arithmetic).
  • The previous model's most recent prices were $199 and $249, so the new model is $50 to $100 above them (our arithmetic).
  • The new model is on pre-order and is due on November 13, two weeks before Black Friday on November 27.

How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the Apple TV 4K on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

What this means if you're buying

  • You want the newest model: Apple lists it at $299 for pre-order. Retailers may price it differently, so compare before you decide.
  • You want to spend less: a cheaper streamer, like a Fire TV Stick 4K Max, covers many of the same apps. We haven't compared them.
  • You're looking at the previous model: check that the listing says which model it is, who sells it and what the price is. Our sources don't tell us how much of it is left in stock.

We don't predict prices, and we haven't found a report of the new model being discounted. Black Friday is November 27 and Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026.

Common mistakes when buying an Apple TV

  • Using an old "$129" price. Apple's pricing changed this year.
  • Mixing up the new and previous models. Check the title and the generation.
  • Paying for features you won't use. Many smart TVs already include the streaming apps, so check what your TV does first.
  • Expecting a discount on a new model. We can't predict one.

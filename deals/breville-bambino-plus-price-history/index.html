Why the Bambino Plus Is Worth the Wait

Breville's ThermoJet heating system reaches optimal extraction temperature in about 3 seconds — a genuinely different experience from machines that need several minutes to warm up before your first shot. Combined with a 54mm portafilter designed for an 18-gram dose, the Bambino Plus delivers what Breville calls its "4 keys formula": fresh grounds, precise temperature, optimal pressure, and true microfoam milk, the same formula used across its pricier machines.

💡 The Automatic Steam Wand Is the Real Upgrade Over the Base BambinoWhere the standard Bambino uses a manual, ball-jointed steam wand, the Bambino Plus adds an automatic, hands-free steam wand with vertical-only movement that textures milk to a consistent microfoam roughly 12 seconds faster per drink. For anyone making lattes or cappuccinos regularly, that hands-free consistency is the single biggest reason to pay the premium over the base model.

Maintenance Is a Bit More Involved

The Bambino Plus includes a 3-way solenoid valve, which the base Bambino lacks — this means the Plus requires a separate backflush cleaning cycle in addition to regular descaling, where the base model skips backflushing entirely. It's a modest amount of extra maintenance in exchange for drier pucks and reduced portafilter "sneeze" when removing it after a shot.

Compact Enough for Any Kitchen

At just 12 inches tall and roughly 7.7 inches wide, the Bambino Plus is genuinely small for what it does — Breville designed it specifically to compete with countertop-hogging machines while still delivering "third wave" specialty coffee quality at home. The size difference from the base Bambino is minor (about 1.4 inches wider), mostly accommodating a larger water tank and the automatic steam wand's temperature sensor.

✅ Who Should Wait vs. Buy NowIf you're not in urgent need of an espresso machine, wait — this specific model has proven itself a reliable candidate for a $359.99-$399.95 price point multiple times over the past year, and there's no indication that pattern has stopped. If you need one immediately for a specific reason (a gift, a move-in, a broken machine), $493.99 still buys a genuinely well-reviewed machine; you're just not getting the best available price.

Care Tips

Run the backflush cycle weekly with regular use, and don't skip the descale cycle when the machine prompts you — mineral buildup is the most common cause of reduced steam pressure and inconsistent shots over time. Use the official or a compatible 54mm portafilter; third-party options exist but fit and finish vary.

GTA 6 — Everything Confirmed as of June 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI has been officially confirmed for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. Preorders open June 25. Cover art was revealed June 18. Every other detail — final pricing, edition contents, preorder bonuses, collector's packages, multiplayer structure — will be confirmed when preorders open. This guide covers only what is confirmed and what is credibly expected based on Rockstar's history and Take-Two's official statements.

The November 19 date is significant for two reasons. First, it is a Thursday launch — unusual for major games, which typically launch Friday or Tuesday. Second, it positions GTA 6 squarely in the 2026 holiday season, competing with itself rather than other games. No major publisher wants to compete with GTA 6 in November. The migration of Wolverine and Townfall to September demonstrates how much the rest of the industry is adjusting around Rockstar's release window.

Rockstar has twice delayed GTA 6 — first from Fall 2025 to May 2026, then from May to November. Both delays were accompanied by official statements citing polish and quality. The November date has been publicly reaffirmed by Zelnick as final. A third delay would be catastrophic for Take-Two's stock and Rockstar's relationship with publishers, retailers, and the player base — the current consensus is that November 19 will hold.

GTA 6 Price — What to Expect on June 25

No official GTA 6 price has been announced — that will come June 25 when preorders go live. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick's reference to "$70 or $80" as the premium game price point is the closest thing to an official signal. The standard edition is almost certainly $69.99 or $79.99. A Special Edition at $99.99 and a Collector's Edition above $150 are expected based on GTA V's 2013 model (Standard $59.99, Special $79.99, Collector's $149.99).

The viral rumor of a $200 GTA 6 price originated from speculation and has been directly contradicted by Zelnick's comments. A $100 standard edition is possible but considered unlikely — Zelnick's awareness that triple-digit standard pricing "generates negative headlines" suggests Take-Two will stay at $79.99 or below for the base game.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does GTA 6 release?+
GTA 6 releases November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Rockstar confirmed this date after two delays — originally targeting Fall 2025, then May 26, 2026. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick confirmed in February 2026 that the November 19 date is locked and a third delay is off the table. No PC release date has been announced.
How much will GTA 6 cost?+
GTA 6's official price will be confirmed when preorders open June 25, 2026. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick indicated a $70–80 range for the standard edition in a CNBC interview. Most analysts expect $69.99–$79.99 standard and $99.99+ for special editions.
Can you preorder GTA 6 now?+
GTA 6 preorders open June 25, 2026 — confirmed by Rockstar on June 18, 2026 alongside the cover art reveal. Preorders will be available on PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, and Walmart. Trailer 3 is expected on June 25 when preorders go live. Do not buy from any site claiming to take preorders before June 25.
What platforms is GTA 6 on?+
GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 exclusively on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. No PC version has been announced for 2026. Based on Rockstar's history — GTA V took 18 months to reach PC after console launch — a GTA 6 PC version is expected in late 2027 or early 2028. No last-gen (PS4/Xbox One) version exists or is planned.
What is GTA 6 about?+
GTA 6 follows dual protagonists Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval in the fictional state of Leonida — a reimagined version of Florida including Vice City, the Leonida Keys, and vast rural and suburban areas. Lucia is the first female protagonist in the mainline GTA series. The game is set in modern day and is the largest open world Rockstar has ever built, with NPC density, draw distances, and world reactivity far beyond GTA V.
Will GTA 6 come to Game Pass?+
GTA 6 is not confirmed for Xbox Game Pass at launch. Rockstar and Take-Two have historically avoided Day 1 Game Pass releases for major titles — GTA V was only added to Game Pass years after launch. A Day 1 Game Pass launch for GTA 6 would be unprecedented and has not been indicated by any official source. Expect to pay full price at launch on Xbox.
GTA 6 price history — how did GTA 5 price over time?+
GTA V launched at $59.99 in 2013, dropped to $29.99 at Black Friday 2013, hit $19.99 within 18 months, and eventually became free on Epic Games Store in 2021. For GTA 6, the pattern will likely be slower — the game will hold $69.99–79.99 longer given its scale and cultural impact. The first significant discount is unlikely before Black Friday 2027, approximately 12 months post-launch. Buying at launch is the only way to play in 2026.

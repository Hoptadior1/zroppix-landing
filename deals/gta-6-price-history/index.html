What's confirmed about GTA 6

  • Release date: November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
  • Price: the Standard Edition was priced at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99, according to Reuters-sourced coverage (June 24, 2026).
  • Physical copies: reported as a box containing a download code, not a disc, with pre-loading starting November 12 (Engadget).
  • Early-order bonus: Engadget reports a Vintage Vice City Pack for anyone who orders or buys before November 20. Check Rockstar's page for exactly which editions get it.
  • PC: no PC release was named in that coverage.

These details come from launch-announcement coverage and can change, so confirm them on Rockstar's page and your store before you pay. We haven't verified current stock or any later price changes.

Standard or Ultimate

The Ultimate Edition costs $20 more and, according to the coverage above, adds digital story-related items such as vehicles, weapons and clothing. If you care about those extras, the extra $20 is easy to price: compare what each edition lists on Rockstar's page and decide whether you'd pay for them separately. If you only want the game, the Standard Edition is the plain choice. Either way, compare the same edition across sellers so you're comparing like with like.

Where to buy, and what to avoid

  • Official stores and big retailers. The PlayStation Store, Xbox Store and major retailers such as Amazon are the safe places. Compare the final price, including tax, and read the cancellation or return terms.
  • Physical is a code in a box. That affects resale and sharing, so decide whether a box is worth it over digital. Pre-loading starts before launch for both.
  • Avoid key resellers and too-good deals. A GTA 6 listing far below the official price is a warning sign. Stay with known sellers.
  • Check the platform. A PS5 code won't work on Xbox, and the reverse.

  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

After launch: tracking the price

The launch price is set, so there's little to track until later. After launch, retailers and stores run promotions at various times, and we can't predict when a given game will be discounted.

How to check a electronics price in 5 steps

  1. Confirm the exact model. Check the model year, storage and colour in the listing title.
  2. Check what's included. Chargers, cases and bundles change the real price.
  3. Compare sellers. Many of the reports we cite were at other retailers, so open the Amazon listing.
  4. Open the price history for that exact version, and compare today's price with the lowest in the window.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet.

How to read the prices on this page

Cards with a price show a report from a named source on a specific date. That is different from a live price, and it's why the source and date sit under each one. Cards without a price link to Amazon, where you can see today's price, and some also to a price page.

  • Reported means a deal site or publication posted that price on that date. Many such deals have expired.
  • List or regular price is the seller's reference price. A badge on Amazon compares against a reference price too, so check what it's compared with.
  • We are rebuilding the remaining price pages in batches to the same standard, so more cards will gain a dated price over time.

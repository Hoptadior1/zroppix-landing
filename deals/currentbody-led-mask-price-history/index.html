Why Professional-Grade Wavelengths Matter Here

The mask delivers clinically recognized red (633nm) and near-infrared (830nm) wavelengths — the same two frequencies used in professional in-office LED treatments, not a marketing-invented spectrum unique to the device. Red light stimulates collagen production for reduced fine lines, while near-infrared penetrates deeper to support cell repair, working together rather than as separate treatments.

💡 The Math Against Clinic Visits Is the Real Value CaseA single professional LED light therapy session at a med-spa or dermatology office typically runs $75-150. At $379.99, this device pays for itself in roughly 3-5 sessions' worth of clinic pricing — and then keeps delivering unlimited sessions afterward with no per-visit cost, at home, on your own schedule.

Series 2's Real Upgrades Over Series 1

CurrentBody's Series 2 adds extra LED bulbs in an improved layout, a chin strap for genuine lower-face coverage, and new deep near-infrared light — this deeper wavelength specifically targets more pronounced signs of aging by penetrating up to 10mm into the hypodermis, well beyond what surface-level red light alone reaches. The Best-Fit flexible silicone also contours to more face shapes than the original design.

What Actual Results Look Like

CurrentBody states visible results within 4 weeks of consistent use, with full effects building over 8 weeks — using the mask 3 to 5 times weekly for roughly 10-minute sessions, with rest days built in rather than daily use. This isn't an instant-transformation device; it's a cumulative treatment closer to how professional skin treatments actually work.

✅ Who Should Buy ThisThis makes real financial sense for anyone already spending money on professional LED facials or considering starting — the device pays for itself quickly against clinic pricing and then keeps delivering value indefinitely. If you've never tried red light therapy and aren't sure you'll stick with a consistent routine, a single professional session first is a lower-commitment way to test the waters before this investment.

Safety Considerations

The device is FDA cleared and safe for all skin tones and types, but CurrentBody explicitly states it has not been tested on pregnant or breastfeeding individuals, and should not be used during pregnancy or while trying to conceive. Anyone with photosensitivity conditions or on medications that increase light sensitivity should consult a doctor before starting LED treatment.

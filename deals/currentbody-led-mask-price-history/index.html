Series 2 or Series 3?

The first thing to know is that CurrentBody sells more than one mask. Its best-sellers page lists the Series 2 red light mask at $469.99, the Series 3 at $569.99 and a Series 2 blue light mask at $469.99 with a struck-through $379.99. For the red masks the "was" price matched the current price, so no sale was shown.

If you saw $379.99 on an older page, that was a different (blue light) product, not today's Series 2 red mask.

What the Series 3 page says

CurrentBody's Series 3 page lists six wavelengths (red 633nm, near-infrared 830nm, deep near-infrared 1072nm, blue 415nm, green 532nm, yellow 590nm), 750 LEDs, and 10-minute sessions 3 to 5 times a week. The page headline says the mask is FDA-cleared, without a clearance number. We could not confirm Series 2 specs from CurrentBody's pages, so check the listing for the version you buy.

The page lists contraindications, including pregnancy or breastfeeding, photosensitive conditions and photosensitising medications, and use on moles or open sores. It also describes a 24-hour patch test, with a 14-day return if skin reacts. Read these and check with a doctor if unsure.

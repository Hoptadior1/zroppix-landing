How to read the prices on this page

Cards with a price show a report from a named source on a specific date. That is different from a live price, and it's why the source and date sit under each one. Cards without a price link to Amazon, where you can see today's price, and some also to a price page.

  • Reported means a deal site or publication posted that price on that date. Many such deals have expired.
  • List or regular price is the seller's reference price. A badge on Amazon compares against a reference price too, so check what it's compared with.

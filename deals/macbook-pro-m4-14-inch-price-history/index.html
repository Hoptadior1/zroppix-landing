MacBook Pro M4 vs MacBook Air M5 — The Key Question

You already have MacBook Air M4 and M5 pages on Zroppix. The MacBook Pro M4 targets a completely different buyer — professionals who need the fan, the Liquid Retina XDR display, and the expanded port selection. The Air is passive cooling (no fan) which means it throttles under sustained heavy loads like 4K video export or large code compilation. The Pro never throttles under any sustained workload because the fan keeps the chip cool.

The Pro's 14.2-inch Liquid Retina XDR display with 120Hz ProMotion is in a different class from the Air's display — 1,000 nits sustained brightness versus the Air's 500 nits, plus ProMotion that dynamically adjusts from 24Hz to 120Hz based on content. For photographers, video editors, and designers this difference is meaningful in daily use.

Port selection is the third differentiator. The MacBook Pro M4 has MagSafe 3, two Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, an SD card reader, and a headphone jack. The MacBook Air M5 has MagSafe and two Thunderbolt ports — no HDMI, no SD card reader. For professionals who connect cameras, external displays, and storage regularly the Pro's port selection eliminates the need for a dock.

MacBook Pro M4 vs MacBook Air M5 — which should I buy?+
The MacBook Air M5 is the better choice for everyday use — lighter, fanless, and starts at $899-999 at Prime Day pricing. The MacBook Pro M4 is for professionals who need sustained performance for video editing, coding, and heavy creative work — it has a fan for sustained loads, a Liquid Retina XDR display with ProMotion, and better port selection including SD card reader and HDMI.
Does the MacBook Pro M4 come with a charger?+
Yes — the MacBook Pro M4 includes a 70W USB-C power adapter in the box. It also supports MagSafe 3 charging up to 96W. Unlike the MacBook Air, which charges via USB-C only, the Pro has a dedicated MagSafe port plus two Thunderbolt 4 ports for simultaneous charging and peripherals.
How long does the MacBook Pro M4 battery last?+
Apple rates the MacBook Pro M4 14-inch at up to 24 hours battery life. In real-world testing Tom's Guide measured over 16 hours of continuous use. This is significantly longer than most Windows laptops at the same price point and makes it a strong choice for professionals who work away from power outlets.
Is the MacBook Pro M4 good for gaming?+
The MacBook Pro M4 handles gaming better than any previous MacBook thanks to the M4 chip's GPU improvements and Apple's Metal API. AAA titles available on Mac including Resident Evil Village, No Man's Sky, and Control run well. However, the Mac gaming library is still smaller than Windows — if gaming is the primary use case a Windows gaming laptop is the better choice.
MacBook Pro M4 vs M4 Pro — what is the difference?+
The base M4 MacBook Pro has a 10-core CPU and 10-core GPU with 16GB RAM starting at $1,599. The M4 Pro model has a 12-14 core CPU and 20-core GPU with 24GB RAM starting at $1,999. The M4 Pro is worth the premium for video editors, developers compiling large codebases, and 3D artists. For most other professional workloads the base M4 is excellent at a lower price.

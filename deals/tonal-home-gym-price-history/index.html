What Tonal is

Tonal is a wall-mounted smart strength-training machine. It uses electromagnetic resistance instead of weight plates, a touchscreen for guided workouts, and a subscription for the coaching and workout library. Tonal publishes the specifications, accessories and warranty on its own site, so check the current model and what comes in the box before you buy, since these change.

It's a major purchase, which is why the hardware price is only part of the question.

The real cost: hardware plus membership

Smart fitness machines usually need an ongoing subscription to unlock the coaching and workouts. Before comparing prices, check these on Tonal's own site:

  • The monthly membership price and the minimum commitment, if any. These have changed over time for fitness subscriptions, so check the current figure.
  • What works without a membership. Find out what the hardware does without one.
  • Installation. Tonal mounts to a wall, so check the wall requirements, the installation terms and who does it.
  • What's included. Compare bundles by what's in the box, not by the headline price.
  • Financing. Financing offers and the terms vary, so read the full terms.

Add the hardware, the membership over the period you'll use it, and any accessories to get a fair total cost, then compare that with a gym membership or a conventional home gym.

Where to compare

Tonal is sold directly by Tonal and through some retailers. Promotions, bundles, financing and installation terms can differ between sellers, so compare the full offer: price, what's included, installation, return policy and warranty. For an item this expensive, buying from the manufacturer or an authorised seller is the safest route, and check the return terms before you pay.

Common mistakes when buying a smart home gym

  • Looking only at the hardware price. Include the membership and accessories.
  • Not checking your wall. Confirm the mounting requirements first.
  • Trusting a "was" price. Compare against recorded price history.
  • Buying an unauthorised third-party listing. Check the seller, warranty and installation eligibility.
  • Not checking the return window. A machine this size is hard to judge until you've used it.

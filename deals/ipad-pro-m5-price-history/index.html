iPad Pro M5 — What Actually Happened With Pricing

The iPad Pro M5 launched in October 2025 with a Tandem OLED Ultra Retina XDR display — the first OLED panel Apple has put in an iPad. The 11-inch 256GB Wi-Fi model launched at $999. It saw its first real discount at Black Friday 2025, about a month after launch, and its lowest independently confirmed price came at Prime Day 2026: $899.99, reported by AppleInsider. A price briefly dipped under $800 at another point, but Macworld's own reporting flagged that as "something of an anomaly" rather than a price worth expecting again.

What makes this product's price history unusual is what happened next: in early July 2026, Apple raised list prices on multiple M5 iPad Pro configurations. 9to5Toys confirmed the 1TB 11-inch model went from $1,599 to $1,799 directly at Apple, with Amazon updating to reflect the new regular price shortly after. We don't have confirmed post-hike pricing specifically for the base 256GB 11-inch model, so treat the original $999 figure as historical, not current, until you've checked the live price yourself.

iPad Pro M5 vs iPad Air M4

The Pro's Tandem OLED display, M5 chip, Face ID, Thunderbolt port, and ProRes camera system justify a real price premium over the iPad Air M4's LCD screen and USB-C port. For casual use — browsing, video, note-taking, light productivity — the Air remains the better value. The Pro earns its premium for creative professionals working with Apple Pencil Pro or video editing where the OLED contrast and Thunderbolt speeds genuinely matter.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the lowest confirmed price the iPad Pro M5 has been?+
The confirmed Prime Day 2026 price for the 11-inch 256GB Wi-Fi (Standard Glass) was $899.99 — $100 off the $999 list price, reported by AppleInsider. A brief sub-$800 price also appeared at another point but was described as an anomaly rather than a reliable target.
Did Apple raise iPad Pro M5 prices?+
Yes. In early July 2026, Apple raised list prices on multiple iPad Pro M5 configurations. For example, the 1TB 11-inch model rose from $1,599 to $1,799 at Apple directly, with Amazon reflecting the new regular price shortly after. Always check the current live price rather than assuming the original launch price still applies.
What is the difference between iPad Pro M5 and iPad Air M4?+
The iPad Pro M5 has a Tandem OLED Ultra Retina XDR display, the M5 chip, Face ID, a Thunderbolt port, and a ProRes camera system with Apple Pencil Pro support. The iPad Air M4 uses an LCD display and USB-C. The Air is better value for most users; the Pro suits creative professionals who want the best display.
iPad Pro M5 11-inch vs 13-inch — which should I buy?+
The 11-inch is more portable and is the most purchased configuration. The 13-inch suits digital artists and video editors who want a larger canvas and a closer laptop-replacement experience, at a real price premium.

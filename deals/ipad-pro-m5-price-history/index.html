iPad Pro M5 vs iPad Air M4 — Honest Comparison

iPad Pro M5 — Why the OLED Display Changes Everything

The single most impactful upgrade in the iPad Pro M5 over every iPad that came before it is the Tandem OLED display. Standard OLED panels dim at high brightness to protect longevity — this is why many OLED phones look washed out in direct sunlight. Tandem OLED stacks two panels and powers them at half brightness each, achieving 1,000 nits sustained and 1,600 nits peak without the lifespan trade-off. The result is an iPad that looks spectacular in direct sunlight and in dark rooms simultaneously — something no LCD iPad has ever achieved.

For photographers using Lightroom on iPad, the color accuracy difference between the M5 Pro OLED and the iPad Air M4 LCD is immediately visible. The OLED delivers true blacks that make dark shadows in images read correctly, while the LCD's backlight bleed creates a grey haze that masks shadow detail. For video editors reviewing footage, the same contrast advantage applies — color grading decisions made on the M5 Pro display translate more accurately to finished output than those made on LCD screens.

The M5 chip also meaningfully improves the iPad Pro's AI capabilities compared to M4. Apple's on-device AI features in iPadOS — Image Playground, Writing Tools, and Priority Notifications — run faster and handle larger context windows on M5. For users who use the iPad Pro as their primary device for document work, research, and creative output, the M5's AI acceleration makes day-to-day tasks measurably faster. At Prime Day pricing of $849-937, the M5 iPad Pro is the most capable tablet available at that price anywhere in the market.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the lowest price the iPad Pro M5 has been?+
The iPad Pro M5 11-inch lowest confirmed price is $937 at early Prime Day 2026 — $62 off the $999 list price. Prime Day June 23-26 is expected to push it to $849-937. The M4 iPad Pro 11-inch hit $928 at early Prime Day 2024, setting the precedent.
What is the difference between iPad Pro M5 and iPad Air M4?+
The iPad Pro M5 has a Tandem OLED Ultra Retina XDR display — the only OLED iPad Apple makes — with significantly better contrast and black levels than the iPad Air M4's LCD. The Pro also has the M5 chip (vs M4 in the Air), Face ID, a Thunderbolt port (vs USB-C), a ProRes camera system, and supports the Apple Pencil Pro with haptic feedback. The Air is the better value for most users. The Pro is for creative professionals and those who want the best display.
Does the iPad Pro M5 support Apple Intelligence?+
Yes — the iPad Pro M5 fully supports Apple Intelligence including Writing Tools, Image Playground, Genmoji, and on-device AI features. It runs iPadOS 18 and will receive iPadOS updates through at least 2029 per Apple's software support policy.
iPad Pro M5 11-inch vs 13-inch — which should I buy?+
The 11-inch at $849-937 Prime Day pricing is the right choice for portability — it fits in bags without a dedicated laptop compartment and is light enough for one-handed reading. The 13-inch at $1,099-1,199 Prime Day pricing is better for digital artists using Apple Pencil Pro, video editors using Final Cut Pro, and users who want a laptop replacement experience. The 11-inch is the most purchased iPad Pro config.
What accessories does the iPad Pro M5 support?+
The iPad Pro M5 supports the Apple Pencil Pro (haptic feedback, barrel roll detection), the Magic Keyboard with built-in trackpad, and the Smart Folio case. The Thunderbolt port supports external displays up to 6K, external SSDs at full USB 3 speeds, and Thunderbolt hubs. All M5 iPad Pro accessories are fully compatible.
Should I buy the iPad Pro M5 with Standard Glass or Nano-Texture?+
Standard Glass is the right choice for most buyers. The Nano-Texture Glass ($100-200 more) reduces glare significantly and is worth it for digital artists and users who work in bright outdoor environments. If you primarily use your iPad indoors or in controlled lighting, Standard Glass delivers the same OLED display quality at a lower price.

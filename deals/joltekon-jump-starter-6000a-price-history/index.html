How to Use a Jump Starter Safely

Regardless of brand, jump starters share the same basic safety sequence. Connect the red clamp to the positive terminal first, then the black clamp to a grounded, unpainted metal surface on the engine block or chassis — not directly to the negative terminal, which reduces spark risk near the battery. Start the vehicle, then remove the clamps in reverse order once it's running: black first, then red.

A few general precautions apply across all jump starter models: keep the unit away from open flames or sparks given the battery's flammable-liquid warning labels, avoid using it in heavy rain unless it's specifically rated for outdoor exposure, and recharge it every 1-3 months even if unused, since lithium battery packs slowly self-discharge and can degrade if left fully depleted for extended periods.

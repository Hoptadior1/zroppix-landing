Check what you're buying

Jump starters are sold on a headline number, like 6000A, that is a peak rating and not a guarantee for your engine. Before you pay, check the listing for the engine sizes it supports, the battery capacity, the safety features such as reverse-polarity protection, and the seller. Joltekon is a lesser-known brand, so read recent reviews and the warranty terms. Always follow the manual's instructions for using a jump starter and keep it charged.

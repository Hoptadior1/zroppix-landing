What changed since our last update

The page we replaced said the A7 IV had a $2,498 list price and that B&H had a $1,998 sale in July 2026 "reported by two outlets." We can't source that, so it's removed. Here's what we can source.

TechRadar (December 2, 2025) reported the A7 IV body at $1,998 at Adorama against $2,699, the day Sony announced the A7 V at $2,899. It also reported £1,549 at Amazon UK, which it called a record low there. Technobezz (August 1, 2026) reported the kit with the 28-70mm lens at $2,088.10 on Amazon against $2,699.99, 23% off, and called it the lowest price on record for that kit.

Why check the history on a $2,000 camera

A reported $600 to $700 swing on one camera is worth a real check, and the body and the kit are different products.

  • $1,998 body at Adorama (Dec 2, 2025) against $2,699: $701 off, about 26% (our arithmetic).
  • $2,088.10 kit at Amazon (Aug 1, 2026) against $2,699.99: $611.89 off, about 23% (Technobezz's figure).
  • The A7 V launched at $2,899 (Dec 2, 2025), so the older body's price moves with the newer model.

How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the Sony A7 IV on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

Body, kit or the newer A7 V

  • Body only: the better pick if you already own lenses that fit.
  • Kit with the 28-70mm: check whether the kit price beats the body plus the lens bought separately.
  • A7 V: TechRadar reported the launch price. Compare it against the current A7 IV price rather than the list price.

We haven't tested these cameras. Read independent reviews for image quality and video features.

Common mistakes when buying a camera

  • Comparing a kit price with a body price.
  • Trusting "lowest price on record" without a source. Ask who tracked it and over what period.
  • Ignoring the seller. A price at Adorama or B&H is not an Amazon price.
  • Forgetting cards and batteries. They add to the real cost.

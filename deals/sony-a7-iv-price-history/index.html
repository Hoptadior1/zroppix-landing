Sony A7 IV Price History

The Sony A7 IV launched in October 2021 at $2,499 body only ($2,699 with 28-70mm kit lens), per Sony's official specs — the list price has held at essentially that level since launch. Real, dated discounts do happen through authorized retailers: most recently, B&H Photo cut the body-only price to $1,998 — a confirmed $500 off — as part of a July 2026 sale, independently reported by both Digital Camera World and Imaging Resource on July 13, 2026.

We don't have sourced evidence of a single guaranteed annual discount event (like a specific Prime Day date) for this camera — discounts through B&H, Adorama, and Amazon appear periodically rather than on a fixed calendar. The honest takeaway: check the live price before buying, and treat anything at or below $1,998 as a genuinely good, historically-confirmed price point.

What Is the Sony A7 IV

The Sony A7 IV is a 33MP full-frame BSI-CMOS mirrorless camera positioned as Sony's mainstream hybrid — capable as both a stills camera and a video tool. Its Real-time Tracking with AI subject recognition autofocus tracks eyes, faces, bodies, animals, vehicles, and aircraft, technology derived from Sony's professional sports-oriented bodies.

Sony A7 IV vs A7 V — Real Prices

The Sony A7 V launched in December 2025 at $2,898 body only (confirmed via Sony's own specs, Wikipedia, and Imaging Resource) — about $400 more than the A7 IV's list price, and roughly $900 more than the A7 IV's confirmed July 2026 sale price. The A7 V's meaningful upgrade is a partially-stacked sensor enabling 30fps blackout-free electronic shutter shooting (versus the A7 IV's 10fps), an improved AI processing unit, and up to 16 stops of dynamic range.

Sony E-Mount Lens Ecosystem

The Sony A7 IV uses the Sony E-mount, one of the most mature full-frame mirrorless lens ecosystems available, with native Sony FE lenses plus third-party options from Sigma, Tamron, Zeiss, and others. It also accepts Sony A-mount lenses via the LA-EA5 adapter.

Sony A7 IV Battery Life and Memory Cards

The Sony A7 IV uses the NP-FZ100 battery, rated for roughly 580 shots per charge (CIPA, viewfinder mode). Real-world mixed shooting typically delivers less than the CIPA rating. The camera has dual SD card slots, with buffer performance improved when using a CFexpress Type A card in Slot 1 for extended burst sequences.

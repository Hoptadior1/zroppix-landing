What the Echo Show 11 is

The Echo Show 11 is Amazon's 2025 smart display. TechRadar's review (November 26, 2025) lists a 11.95-inch HD touchscreen at 1920 x 1200, a 13 MP camera with auto framing, the AZ3 Pro processor, and one 2.8-inch woofer plus two full-range drivers. It works as a Zigbee, Matter and Thread border router and supports Wi-Fi 6E. TechRadar gives the US launch price as $219.99.

Check the listing title: Amazon has sold several Echo Show 11 and 10-inch-class displays over the years, and the model year decides what you get.

What the reviewer liked and didn't

TechRadar scored Design 4.5/5, Performance 4/5 and Value 3.5/5. It liked the bright screen, floating design, audio and smart-home support. It noted no adjustable display (the Echo Show 10 rotated), no physical camera shutter, and that Alexa+ is US-only for now. Its verdict was that screen size alone doesn't justify it, because it largely matches the cheaper Echo Show 8, so it suits owners of older Echo Shows or heavy smart-home users.

Who should look at it

  • You have lots of smart-home gear: the Thread, Matter and Zigbee hub is the main draw.
  • You want a bigger screen than the Echo Show 8: compare, as TechRadar found them similar.
  • You care about privacy: there is no camera shutter, so check the alternatives.
  • You want a rotating display: look at the Echo Show 10 instead.

Common mistakes when buying

  • Trusting the "list" price. Sources disagree ($219.99 vs $249.99). Use recorded history.
  • Buying the wrong generation. Read the model year.
  • Waiting for Big Deal Days. It already happened (Oct 6 to 7, 2026).
  • Expecting Alexa+ outside the US. It is US-only per TechRadar.

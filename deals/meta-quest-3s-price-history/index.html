Meta Quest 3S vs Meta Quest 3 — Which Is Worth Buying?

The Quest 3S at $249-299 and the Quest 3 at $499.99 offer the same core VR experience with one key difference: the Quest 3 has a higher resolution display and wider field of view in mixed reality mode. For gaming — which is what most people use a Quest headset for — the difference is minimal. For mixed reality productivity, the Quest 3's superior display quality is more noticeable.

For the vast majority of buyers, the Quest 3S at $249 is the right choice. The $250 you save goes a long way toward games, accessories, and the accessories that actually expand what you can do in VR. Buy the Quest 3 only if you specifically need the highest resolution mixed reality experience for productivity work.

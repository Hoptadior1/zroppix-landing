What changed since our last update

The page we replaced said the Quest 3S hit an "all-time low of $249 at Prime Day 2025." We can't source that, and Slickdeals (March 9, 2026) shows $249 was also posted in March 2026, so it wasn't unique. We removed the claim. Prices also changed in April, so older prices aren't comparable with newer ones.

Road to VR (June 24, 2026) reported that Amazon's Prime Day 2026 sale was the first Quest 3S discount since the April price hike: the 128GB with Gorilla Tag at $300 (regular $350) and the 256GB at $380 (regular $450), with Gorilla Tag or Batman: Arkham Shadow. The article said the hike raised prices by $50 to $100.

How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the Meta Quest 3S on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

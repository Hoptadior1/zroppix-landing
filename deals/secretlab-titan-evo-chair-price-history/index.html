What the Titan Evo is

The Secretlab Titan Evo is a gaming chair from Secretlab, a company that sells mainly through its own website and through some retailers. It's built for long sitting sessions and has adjustable lumbar support, a magnetic headrest, recline and 4D armrests, in a choice of fabric and leatherette. It comes in Small, Regular and XL sizes, each with a stated height and weight range.

It's a long-established product, and Secretlab runs its own promotions throughout the year. That's the first thing to understand about its pricing: the best offer may be on Secretlab's own site, not on Amazon, and the two can differ.

Choosing the right size

Size is the main thing that causes returns on a chair like this. Secretlab publishes a size chart with height and weight ranges for each size, so check it on Secretlab's own page and measure yourself. If you sit between sizes, read the size guidance and recent buyer reviews, and check the return terms before you buy.

  • Small, Regular, XL: match your height and weight to Secretlab's chart.
  • Fabric or leatherette: fabric tends to run cooler, and leatherette is easier to wipe clean, but check the current descriptions.
  • Warranty: Secretlab advertises a warranty period, which can depend on the product and where you buy. Check the terms for your region and seller.

Amazon or Secretlab's own site?

Secretlab runs promotions on its own site, and these don't automatically apply to Amazon listings. Before you buy, compare the full price on each: the item price, any discount code, delivery, taxes and the return policy. The warranty and returns may also differ depending on the seller. A deal that exists only on one site isn't a deal on the other.

Whichever you choose, open the live listing, confirm the size and cover, and check the seller.

Common mistakes when buying a premium chair

  • Ordering the wrong size. Check the chart, not just the name.
  • Assuming a promotion applies everywhere. A site-wide promotion may not apply to Amazon.
  • Trusting a discount badge. Compare against recorded price history.
  • Skipping the return terms. A chair is hard to judge until you've sat in it for a few days.
  • Ignoring a pure office chair. If you mostly work, compare ergonomic office chairs.

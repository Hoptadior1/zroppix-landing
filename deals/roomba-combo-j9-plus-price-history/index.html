What the Roomba Combo j9+ is

The Roomba Combo j9+ is iRobot's robot vacuum with a built-in mop. BGR's June 7, 2024 review lists a $1,399 launch price and calls it the most impressive Roomba iRobot had made at the time. The mop sits on top of the robot and is lowered by arms while it mops; BGR says the robot lifts the mop pads when it detects carpet, which keeps water off rugs.

The dock is larger than other Roomba docks. BGR describes room for debris bags (up to 60 days of debris), clean and dirty water tanks good for up to 30 days of regular mopping, and automatic emptying and refilling of water after each mopping job. The same review notes there is no automatic mop-pad washing or drying, so you will still handle the pads yourself.

What the reviews say, and the price trade-off

BGR's verdict was that the mop extends the time between manual mopping sessions rather than replacing them. Its listed cons were regular rollers instead of the wider ones on the s9+, no dual spinning mops, and the price. It also named strong suction, obstacle avoidance and the auto-empty and refill dock as pluses.

That review is from 2024. iRobot has launched newer models since, so read recent reviews before you buy, especially if you are comparing the j9+ with something newer at a similar price.

Who should look at it

  • You want one robot to vacuum and light-mop: this is what the Combo is for. Expect to wash the pads yourself.
  • You mostly vacuum: compare the vacuum-only j9+, which BGR said costs less.
  • You have lots of carpet: the mop lifts over carpet per BGR, but check the room layout you have.
  • You want the cheapest self-emptying robot: look at the Shark and Roborock options on our other pages.

Common mistakes when buying

  • Assuming it washes and dries its own mop pads. It doesn't, per BGR.
  • Comparing against the launch price. $1,399 was a list price few paid for long.
  • Mixing up the Combo j9+ with the vacuum-only j9+. They are different models.
  • Ignoring running costs. Bags, filters and pads wear out.

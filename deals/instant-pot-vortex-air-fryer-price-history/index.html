What the Instant Pot Vortex 6QT Actually Is

The standard Instant Pot Vortex 6QT is a 4-function air fryer — air fry, bake, roast, and reheat — built around Instant Pot's EvenCrisp technology, which circulates hot air for consistent crisping with little to no preheating time. It runs on 1700 watts, has an adjustable temperature range of 120–400°F, and includes safety features like overheat protection and auto-shutoff. The companion app ships with 100+ recipes.

It's worth being precise here, because Instant Pot sells several closely-named Vortex variants that get conflated in reviews and deal posts: the standard Vortex covers 4 functions, while the separate Vortex Plus model adds broil and dehydrate for 6 functions total, and has a different price and listing. If a source describes "6 cooking modes including broil and dehydrate," they're likely describing the Vortex Plus, not the standard Vortex covered on this page — worth double-checking which exact model you're comparing prices or specs against.

Is the Instant Pot Vortex 6QT Worth Buying?

For straightforward air frying, baking, roasting, and reheating, the standard Vortex 6QT covers the core use case well — the 6-quart basket handles a family-sized batch of fries, a whole chicken, or a full pizza, and EvenCrisp genuinely reduces the need to shake or flip food partway through cooking, which is a common complaint with cheaper air fryers. NBC Select's independent, hands-on endorsement (from someone who uses it personally, not just reviewed it once) plus a large five-star rating count are reasonable signals this holds up under everyday use.

If you specifically want broiling or dehydrating built in, look at the Vortex Plus instead rather than assuming the standard Vortex covers those functions — that's the single most common point of confusion in air fryer shopping within this product line. Whichever model you choose, check the live Amazon price before buying, since appliance pricing in this category moves meaningfully around seasonal sales.

What The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution is

The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution is an exfoliating toner from The Ordinary, a skincare brand owned by Deciem. It's sold in a 240ml bottle and applied with a cotton pad. Glycolic acid is an alpha-hydroxy acid (AHA) used in exfoliating products. Check the label and The Ordinary's own product page for the exact ingredients, size and directions, since formulas and packaging can change.

It's one of the lower-priced acid toners in a crowded category, which is why people compare it with other exfoliants. The question for a buyer is whether it suits your skin and whether today's price is fair for the size you're getting.

Before you buy: is an acid toner right for you?

This is general information, not medical or dermatological advice.

  • Acid exfoliants can irritate. People with sensitive skin, a damaged skin barrier, or skin conditions should ask a pharmacist or dermatologist before starting.
  • Patch test first. Brands commonly recommend testing a small area before regular use.
  • Follow the label's directions. Use the frequency the brand recommends and build up slowly.
  • Use sunscreen. Exfoliating acids can make skin more sun-sensitive, so sunscreen is usually advised the next day.
  • Be careful combining actives. Check the brand's guidance before using it with retinoids or other acids.
  • Not for everyone. Check the label for warnings, including for pregnancy or use on broken skin.

Common mistakes when buying skincare online

  • Comparing different sizes. Work out the price per ml.
  • Trusting a "was" price. Compare against recorded price history.

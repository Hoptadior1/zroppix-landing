What Is Snail Mucin and Why Does It Work — The Science Behind the Hype

Snail secretion filtrate — the active ingredient at 96% concentration in the COSRX essence — is collected humanely from Cryptomphalus aspersa garden snails by placing them on a mesh surface and allowing them to crawl, depositing their natural secretion which is then filtered and processed. The reason this secretion is so effective in skincare is its biological composition: it is a complex mixture of glycoproteins, proteoglycans, hyaluronic acid, allantoin, glycolic acid, zinc, and copper. This is not a single active ingredient — it is an ecosystem of compounds that address multiple skin concerns simultaneously in a way that synthetic formulas typically cannot replicate without combining many separate ingredients.

The glycoproteins support skin barrier repair and hydration retention. The hyaluronic acid delivers deep moisture. The allantoin soothes and heals irritated skin. The naturally occurring glycolic acid provides mild, gentle exfoliation at a concentration far below what would cause sensitivity. The zinc and copper are antimicrobial and support wound healing and collagen synthesis. Combined at 96% concentration in a lightweight gel-watery base, the result is an essence that simultaneously hydrates, repairs the barrier, gently brightens, and helps fade scarring — a multi-functional performance no single synthetic active achieves at this price point.

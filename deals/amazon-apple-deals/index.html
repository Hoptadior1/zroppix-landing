How Apple deals on Amazon actually work

Apple rarely discounts its own products on its own site, so Amazon is usually where price drops show up first. But "Apple deal" can mean very different things, and not all of them are good:

  • New-model discounts. A recent product with a smaller price cut. This is the most common type for AirPods and iPads.
  • Previous-generation clearance. When a new model arrives, older stock is often marked down. This can be the best value if the older model does what you need.
  • Colour and configuration discounts. A price drop that applies only to certain colours, storage or sizes, so the headline price may not be the one you want.
  • Coupon-based savings. An on-page coupon that you have to clip. Check that it's applied at checkout.

It's also worth knowing that Apple has run its own shopping events around Black Friday in past years, which are separate from Amazon's prices. Amazon and other retailers may or may not match them, so compare before you buy.

The 4-step check before you buy any Apple product

  1. Confirm the exact model, chip, storage and colour. Apple's product names are close to each other and the cheap-looking listing may be a different configuration.
  2. Check who sells and ships it. Prefer Amazon or Apple-authorised sellers. Be cautious of third-party sellers on high-value items.
  3. Open the price history. Compare today's price with the lowest of the past 90 days or longer. The Zroppix extension shows this on the Amazon page.
  4. Check the return window and condition. New should mean new. If the listing says Renewed or Used, read our guide to Warehouse, Outlet and Renewed first.

Should you wait for a sale?

It depends on the product and on how much a discount would save. A big-ticket Mac or iPad is worth waiting on when the price isn't already near its past low, and Amazon's own Black Friday event runs from late November (see our Black Friday 2026 plan). But for something you need now, a small difference isn't worth weeks of waiting. Check the price history: if today's price is at or near the lowest it has been, buy. If not, set an alert. We never predict future prices, because nobody can.

Frequently asked questions

When are Apple products cheapest on Amazon?

Prices vary through the year, and sale events such as Black Friday are a common time for discounts. Check each product's price history to see its past low rather than relying on timing.

Are Apple discounts on Amazon real?

Often, but not always. Check the price history to see whether the 'deal' price is actually lower than it has been recently.

Should I buy the previous-generation model?

If it does everything you need, it can be a better value, especially when stock is being cleared. Compare the features and the price history of both.

Is it safe to buy Apple products from third-party sellers on Amazon?

Prefer items sold and shipped by Amazon or an authorised seller. For expensive items, check the seller rating and return policy.

Does Apple price-match Amazon?

Apple's policies change and vary. Check Apple's current terms before relying on a price match.

Are Renewed Apple products worth it?

They can be, but read the grade and return terms carefully. See our guide to Warehouse, Outlet and Renewed.

How do I know if an iPhone is unlocked?

The listing should say 'unlocked'. Carrier-locked listings often have a low price tied to a plan.

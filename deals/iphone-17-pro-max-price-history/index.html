What happened to the iPhone 17 Pro Max

On September 9 to 10, 2026, Apple removed the iPhone 17 Pro Max and 17 Pro from its US online store after launching the iPhone 18 Pro line, according to Tech-ish and Business Today. WhistleOut says some carriers still sell them. The 18 Pro Max starts at $1,299, $100 above the 17 Pro Max's $1,199 launch price (Tech-ish). Tech-ish adds that discontinued doesn't mean the phone stops working or loses support, and that third-party retailers keep selling existing stock.

So the 17 Pro Max is now an old-stock purchase, and price and condition vary by seller.

What you get, from Apple's page

Apple's specs page lists a 6.9-inch OLED at 2868 by 1320 with ProMotion up to 120Hz, the A19 Pro chip, 256GB to 2TB, a 48MP Fusion main and ultra wide, a 4x telephoto and digital zoom to 40x, and up to 39 hours of video playback (35 streamed). It has an aluminum unibody with Ceramic Shield 2 on the front. It is the largest-screen, longest-battery model Apple listed in that generation.

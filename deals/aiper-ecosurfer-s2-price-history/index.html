What reviewers say

PCWorld's review (May 4, 2026) called it "a skimmer on the slow side" that makes up for it with long battery life. Its notes:

  • Battery and solar: a 5200mAh battery with a claimed 30+ hours per charge, a 24-watt solar panel and an AC adapter (about 6 hours for a full charge). In its test it ran 24 hours and lost about 25% of the battery.
  • Cleaning: synthetic leaves were cleared in about 3.5 hours, with roughly 80% collected.
  • Drawbacks: a slightly underpowered motor, slow speed, no cameras (so pickup is semi-random), a filter basket that is hard to remove, and an app that doesn't report when the basket is full.
  • Weight and connectivity: 13 lbs, Bluetooth and 2.4GHz Wi-Fi.

We haven't tested it ourselves, and these are one reviewer's findings.

Common mistakes when buying a pool skimmer

  • Comparing prices across stores. The same product was $120 apart across Amazon, Aiper and eBay in PCWorld's listing.
  • Expecting it to clear heavy debris. PCWorld said heavier debris would likely sink before the robot reaches it.
  • Trusting a "lowest price" headline. Gizmodo's article gave no price history.
  • Skipping the filter basket. It needs regular cleaning.

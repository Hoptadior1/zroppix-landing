What the EcoSurfer S2 Actually Does

This is a solar-powered floating skimmer, not a full bottom-and-wall pool cleaner — it patrols the surface of your pool collecting leaves, bugs, pollen, and floating debris automatically, without you dropping a net in every day. BobVila's month-long test in an open-air Florida pool found it noticeably reduced surface debris day to day, especially when left running continuously rather than switched on and off.

💡 SolarSeeker Tech Means You Basically Never Charge ItWhen the EcoSurfer S2's battery runs low, it automatically navigates itself toward sunlight for a quick top-up rather than requiring you to fish it out and plug it in. On a full charge it can run over 30 hours continuously, so most owners never touch the DC adapter after the initial setup charge — even through stretches of overcast weather.

The One Real Limitation: Intake Ports

Independent reviewers, including BobVila's testers, noted the robot can occasionally get caught near a pool's wall skimmer intake, since its propulsion doesn't always avoid suction points as cleanly as it avoids solid walls. Aiper addresses this directly by including a foam bumper in the box — essentially a small pool-noodle-style piece — that partially blocks the port and stops the robot from getting stuck there. Not every pool needs it, but it's a smart inclusion for anyone with stronger-than-average suction.

✅ Who This Is Actually ForThis is close to essential for open-air, in-ground pools that constantly collect leaves, bugs, or pollen — the kind of pool where you'd otherwise be out there with a net multiple times a week. If your pool is enclosed, covered most of the time, or rarely collects surface debris, the EcoSurfer S2 is a nice-to-have rather than a necessity.

App Control and Chlorine Dispensing

The Aiper app handles start/stop control, scheduling, live battery monitoring, and a one-tap "return to me" retrieval feature — reviewers found pairing takes under a minute and the remote-steering feature is responsive if you want to manually target a specific patch of debris. The skimmer can also hold standard 3-inch chlorine tablets with adjustable flow control, which helps maintain water quality as a supplement to your pool's main sanitation system, not a replacement for it.

What to Do Right Now

At $339.99, this sits at the low end of its typical real-world price range, and independent testing backs up both the durability claims and the hands-off solar charging. If you have an open-air pool that constantly collects debris, there's little reason to wait for a deeper discount that historically hasn't materialized much below this level.

Maintenance Tips

Pull the EcoSurfer S2 out for a quick rinse periodically, even on days you don't strictly need to — chlorine and mineral buildup on the housing can affect the twin motors' efficiency over time. Empty the debris basket before it reaches capacity, since testers found performance bogs down once it's full, and give the foam bumper a check every few weeks if your pool has strong wall intakes.

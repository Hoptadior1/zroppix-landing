Why $54 Is a Genuinely Exceptional Price

The PS5 DualSense launched at $69.99 in 2020 and has since increased in price to $74.99 as of Sony's April 2026 price adjustment — which affected nearly the entire PlayStation hardware lineup. With the PS5 Pro jumping to $899.99 and the standard PS5 going to $649.99, the DualSense at $74.99 became the new baseline.

That makes today's $54 Days of Play price genuinely exceptional. It is $21 below the current list price and just $5 above the absolute all-time low of $49, which appeared briefly during Black Friday 2022 when the controller had just launched. For the current generation controller in 2026, $54 is a historic buying opportunity.

The Days of Play Sale — What It Is and Why It Matters

Sony's Days of Play is an annual promotional event that typically runs for two weeks in late May and early June. It is one of only two moments per year — along with Black Friday — when Sony authorizes retailers to discount first-party hardware significantly below list price. The DualSense dropping to $54 is not a random Amazon deal. It is a sanctioned Sony promotion with a hard end date of June 10th.

Once June 10th passes, the controller returns to $74.99. There is no Prime Day discount on DualSense controllers historically — Sony does not participate in Amazon's Prime Day promotions with the same depth as Days of Play. The next likely discount opportunity after June 10th is Black Friday in November, five months away.

⚡ Why You Cannot Wait for Prime Day

Prime Day is June 15th — five days after this sale ends. But DualSense controllers have never dropped to $54 during Prime Day. Sony reserves its deepest discounts for its own promotional events. If you are waiting for Prime Day to buy a controller, the price history suggests you will be disappointed. Days of Play is your window. It closes June 10th.

Which Color to Buy

All standard DualSense color options — Midnight Black, Cosmic Red, Starlight Blue, Nova Pink, Galactic Purple, and White — are at $54 during Days of Play. The limited edition controllers including the God of War 20th Anniversary Edition and Marathon Edition are discounted to $64, down from their usual $84.99. If you have been eyeing a limited edition, $64 is likely the lowest those will ever go given they are runs with no restock.

The standard controllers at $54 are the better value for most buyers. The haptic feedback and adaptive triggers are identical across all colors — you are paying for aesthetics on the limited editions, not performance.

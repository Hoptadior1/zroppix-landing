Check which Nano 45W you're looking at

Anker sells several 45W chargers, and the July deals were for the Nano 45W Max GaN Smart Display model (listed as A121D on Slickdeals). The plain Nano and a newer Neo model are separate products with separate prices. Check the title and the photos for a built-in display, and compare against Anker's own site, which has run a coupon before.

A few buying tips that apply to any USB-C charger:

  • Match the wattage to your devices. A 45W charger will charge most phones and tablets at full speed and can run many thin laptops. Heavier laptops want 65W or more. Check the rating printed on your laptop's original adapter.
  • Use a good cable. The cable limits the power, so check its rating. Check whether one is in the box.
  • Check the plug and the seller. Buy from Anker, Amazon or a retailer you know.

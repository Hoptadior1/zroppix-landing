Getting the Most Out of Your Air Fryer

A few habits meaningfully improve results with any air fryer, including the AF101. Avoiding overcrowding the basket is the single biggest factor in achieving genuinely crispy results — cooking in smaller batches with room for air to circulate around each piece of food produces noticeably better texture than packing the basket full. Shaking or flipping food halfway through cooking, particularly for smaller items like fries or wings, ensures even browning on all sides rather than one side crisping while the other stays soft.

Common Uses Beyond Frozen Foods

While frozen fries and chicken nuggets are the obvious starting point, the AF101's roast and dehydrate functions open up genuinely different use cases — roasting vegetables with a fraction of the oil a conventional oven method requires, reheating leftover pizza or fried food with far better texture than a microwave produces, and dehydrating fruit or jerky for anyone interested in home food preservation. This versatility, combined with the compact footprint, is a meaningful part of why the AF101 has remained Amazon's top-selling air fryer despite newer competing models entering the market regularly.

Cleaning and Maintenance

The ceramic-coated basket's dishwasher-safe design is a genuine time-saver compared to older air fryer models requiring hand-washing, but hand-washing with a soft sponge still extends the ceramic coating's non-stick lifespan longer than repeated dishwasher cycles, which can gradually wear coatings over years of heavy use. Wiping down the exterior and heating element area with a damp cloth after each use prevents grease buildup that can affect performance and produce smoke on subsequent uses if left to accumulate over time.

LG Gram 17 in 2026 — Who Should Buy It and Who Should Not

The LG Gram 17 is not for everyone, and being clear about that makes it a better recommendation for the right buyer. It is not a gaming laptop — the Intel Arc integrated graphics handle light games and older titles, but demanding AAA games at high settings require a dedicated GPU that the Gram 17 does not have. It is not a workstation replacement — the Core Ultra processor is powerful for productivity but cannot match the M4 Pro MacBook or Lenovo ThinkPad P-series for intensive rendering or simulation workloads. What it is, is the best portable 17-inch productivity laptop on the market, and it is not close.

The ideal Gram 17 buyer is someone who carries their laptop every day, needs a large screen for spreadsheets, documents, presentations, or code review, and cannot justify the weight penalty of a standard 17-inch laptop. Lawyers reviewing case documents, financial analysts working across multiple spreadsheets, developers who prefer screen real estate over portability, academics who attend lectures and need a large display for notes and reference material — these are the users who consistently rate the Gram 17 as the best laptop purchase they have made.

At Prime Day pricing of $999-1,099, the LG Gram 17 competes directly with the MacBook Pro 14-inch in price. The decision between them is straightforward: if you are in the Apple ecosystem and do creative work, the MacBook Pro 14-inch wins. If you need Windows, need a 17-inch screen, or want a touchscreen, the Gram 17 wins — and it weighs nearly a pound less than the MacBook Pro 16-inch despite having a larger display.

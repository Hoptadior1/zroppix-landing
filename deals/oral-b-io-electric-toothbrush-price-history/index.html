Oral-B iO vs Philips Sonicare — The Technology Difference

The two dominant electric toothbrush brands use fundamentally different technologies. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right product for your dental situation rather than buying based on brand familiarity alone.

Oral-B iO uses oscillating-rotating-pulsating technology with micro-vibrations. The round brush head moves in small circular motions — mimicking the technique dental hygienists recommend for thorough plaque removal. The round head cups each tooth individually and cleans along the gumline more precisely than a side-to-side motion. Oral-B is the #1 dentist-recommended electric toothbrush brand globally, and the round head design is particularly effective for users with braces, implants, crowns, and bridges.

Philips Sonicare uses sonic technology — the brush vibrates at 31,000 strokes per minute in a side-to-side motion that also drives fluid between teeth. The sensation is gentler than Oral-B's oscillating motion, which some users prefer. Sonicare also includes AirFloss technology in some models for interdental cleaning. For users with sensitive teeth or gums who find Oral-B's motion too aggressive, Sonicare is the better choice.

At Prime Day both brands discount equally aggressively. If you have specific dental work — braces, implants, crowns — choose Oral-B iO. If you have sensitivity concerns or prefer a gentler brushing experience, choose Sonicare. If you have no strong preference, the Oral-B iO Series 7 at $49-59 Prime Day is the better absolute value due to the deeper percentage discount from retail.

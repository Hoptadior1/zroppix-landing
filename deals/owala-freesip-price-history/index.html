Check the exact version, size and colour

Owala sells several lids and sizes, and the Slickdeals post used the name "FreeSip Twist" in its title. Before you pay, confirm:

  • The version. The FreeSip and the FreeSip Twist are separate listings with separate prices.
  • The size. Owala lists sizes from about 19 oz to 40 oz. The 24 oz is the one the deals above refer to.
  • The colour. Deal posts usually name the colours that carry the price. Yours may cost more.
  • The seller. Amazon or Owala is safest. Compare the Owala site, which runs its own promotions.

Owala's marketing says its insulation keeps drinks cold for up to 24 hours. That is a maker's claim and we haven't tested it. Check Owala's care instructions before you put the lid or body in a dishwasher.

How to tell whether today's price is fair

We don't publish a price chart or a predicted price for the Owala FreeSip 24 oz on this page, because we can't back one up with recorded data here. What we can give you is a method that works for any product, and the free Zroppix extension that applies it for you on the Amazon page.

  1. Confirm the exact version. Model year, size, colour and bundle can each change the price. Read the title and the variant selector, not just the headline.
  2. Open the price history. Use the Zroppix extension (90-day history on Amazon pages) or another tracker such as CamelCamelCamel or Keepa. Where Amazon shows its own price history link, use that too.
  3. Compare today's price with the lowest in the window. At or near the low is a fair price. A big "percent off" badge on its own proves little, as our guide to sale badges explains.
  4. Check who sells it. Amazon or the brand is safest. Compare against the manufacturer's own site, which may run separate promotions.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet, and decide on your own timeline. Nobody can tell you what the price will do next.

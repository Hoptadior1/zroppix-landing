Xbox Series X Price History — Three Hikes Since May 2025

This is Microsoft's third Xbox console price increase since May 2025. Microsoft's official statement, published via Xbox Wire on June 25, 2026, cites console storage and memory prices rising more than 2.5x since the previous increase, with another doubling expected by fall 2027 — attributed to surging demand for memory chips driven by AI infrastructure buildout. Sony and Nintendo have cited similar pressures and made comparable 2026 price increases to their own hardware.

There is no sourced evidence the Xbox Series X has ever discounted to the $299-349 range during its current generation — that price band is typical of older, end-of-life console hardware, not a current-generation machine mid-lifecycle. If you're planning to buy a Series X, the confirmed August 1 price increase is the single most important date to know.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the Xbox Series X price going up?+
Yes. Microsoft confirmed via Xbox Wire (June 25, 2026) that Xbox Series X pricing rises August 1, 2026: the 1TB disc model goes from $649.99 to $799.99, and the digital model from $599.99 to $749.99, both a $150 increase. Microsoft cited memory and storage component costs rising more than 2.5x, with another doubling expected by fall 2027.
Has the Xbox Series X price ever dropped to $299-349?+
No sourced evidence supports this. Current-generation consoles have not been discounted to that level during their normal lifecycle; that price range is more typical of older, end-of-life hardware like the PS4. As of 2026, Xbox Series X pricing has only gone up, not down, following three price increases since May 2025.
Why did Microsoft raise Xbox console prices in 2026?+
Microsoft cited a global memory and storage component shortage, stating costs had risen more than 2.5x since the prior increase, with a further doubling expected by fall 2027 — attributed to surging demand for memory chips from AI infrastructure. This is Microsoft's third Xbox price increase since May 2025.
Xbox Series X vs PS5 — which console should I buy in 2026?+
After all 2026 price changes, a disc-drive Xbox Series X at $799.99 costs $150 more than a disc PS5 at $649.99. Both companies have raised prices due to component costs. The right choice depends on which exclusive titles and ecosystem (Game Pass vs PlayStation exclusives) matter more to you — check current prices for both before deciding.
Does the Xbox Series X play Xbox One games?+
Yes — the Xbox Series X is backward compatible with thousands of Xbox One, Xbox 360, and original Xbox games, with many titles running at improved frame rates and resolutions via the Xbox Velocity Architecture.

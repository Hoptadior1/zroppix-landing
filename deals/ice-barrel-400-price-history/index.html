Is the Ice Barrel 400 still sold?

The page we replaced told you to buy the 400 at $1,199 as a "confirmed current price." We couldn't confirm that, and several signs point the other way. When we opened icebarrel.com on October 17, 2026, its homepage showed the Ice Barrel 300, the 500 and a chiller, and no 400. Best Buy's listing for the 400 says it is "no longer available in new condition." A Canadian retailer, Northern Fitness, lists the 400 as out of stock.

None of these says the 400 is discontinued, and we can't say it is. What we can say is that you should confirm it is sold new, from a seller you trust, before you plan around it.

What reviewers say about the 400

Men's Fitness UK (June 2024) reported a price of $1,199 on the company website, a capacity of 397 litres (about 105 gallons), a height of 42 inches and a diameter of 31 inches, and a design limit of 6 ft 6 in and 300 lb. It praised the rock-solid build and the lifetime warranty, and said the expense can be hard to justify. Garage Gym Reviews says you need at least 40 lb of ice to reach cold plunge temperatures, that refills of 40 to 100 lb are needed every one to three days, and lists the cost, getting in and out, and the ice refills as cons.

The real cost is the ice

The 400 is an ice-only barrel, so the purchase price isn't the whole cost. Use your own numbers:

  • Monthly ice (lb) = lb per refill × refills per month. At Garage Gym Reviews' 40 lb every two days, that is about 600 lb a month.
  • Monthly cost = that weight ÷ the bag size × your local price per bag.
  • Compare it with a barrel that takes a chiller, whose running cost is electricity.

We don't print a dollar figure because ice prices vary by store. If you plan to plunge most days, run this math before you pay.

Ice Barrel lineup, as listed on its homepage

  • Barrel 300: 61 lb empty and 700 lb full, 35.5 in wide by 30.5 in high, fits most people up to 6 ft.
  • Barrel 500: 106 lb empty and 900 lb full, 57.6 by 30.7 by 42.1 in, fits most people up to 6 ft 9 in.
  • 500 with chiller: listed at $5,749.98 and marked FSA/HSA eligible.

Both list built-in chiller ports, and Ice Barrel says its products are backed by a limited lifetime warranty. Capacity isn't listed on the homepage.

Who should look at it

  • You want an ice-only barrel and have somewhere to put it: Men's Fitness says it is not meant for indoor use.
  • You plunge most days: price a chiller option too, since ice adds up.
  • You are over 6 ft: check the height limit for each size.
  • You want the 400 specifically: confirm it is still sold new first.

Common mistakes when buying a cold plunge

  • Buying a model that may be gone. Check the maker's site and the seller.
  • Ignoring ice costs. Add them to the price before you compare.
  • Skipping the height check. Fit differs between the 300 and 500.
  • Trusting a "confirmed" price without a date and source.

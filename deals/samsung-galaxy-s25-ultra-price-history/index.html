Galaxy S25 Ultra Price History — The Former Flagship Clearing Out Fast

The price has continued falling since then as the S26 Ultra's launch pushed Samsung to clear S25 Ultra inventory.

PhoneArena tracked a 31% discount on the S25 FE at the same time, signaling Samsung is actively running aggressive clearance across the entire S25 lineup ahead of Prime Day. The 256GB model at $749-899 Prime Day pricing represents a 43-58% discount from launch — extraordinary for a phone less than 18 months old.

The S25 Ultra's specs remain completely relevant in 2026. The Snapdragon 8 Elite chip is still one of the fastest mobile processors available. The built-in S Pen remains unique to the Ultra line and the only built-in stylus in flagship Android.

Is the Galaxy S25 Ultra Still Worth Buying in 2026?

At Prime Day clearance pricing of $749-899, the S25 Ultra is one of the most compelling smartphone purchases available. You are getting a phone that cost $1,299 at launch — with camera performance that still leads the Android market — for under $900. The software situation is solid: Samsung committed to 4 major Android OS updates, meaning the S25 Ultra receives Android 16, 17, 18, and 19 — updates through at least 2028.

The only meaningful advantage the S26 Ultra offers is 65W charging versus the S25 Ultra's 45W, the Privacy Display, and an additional year on Samsung's OS update cycle. For buyers who charge overnight and are not impacted by the slower charging speed, those advantages do not justify the $200-300 premium at Prime Day pricing. The S25 Ultra is the smarter value buy for most users.

Galaxy S25 Ultra Camera System — Still Leading Android in 2026

The S25 Ultra's quad camera system remains one of the most capable on any smartphone in 2026. The 200MP main sensor captures extraordinary detail in daylight conditions — at full resolution, you can crop dramatically and still have a publishable image. In real-world use at 12MP or 50MP modes, the main sensor delivers excellent dynamic range, accurate color science, and low noise in moderate lighting. Samsung's AI-powered night photography on One UI 7 has also improved significantly over earlier Galaxy Ultra models.

The dual telephoto system is the S25 Ultra's unique competitive advantage. The 10MP 3x telephoto and 50MP 5x telephoto deliver versatile zoom coverage that no iPhone or Pixel can match with built-in hardware. The 3x lens is ideal for portraits and medium-distance subjects. The 5x at 50MP allows for significant digital zoom to 10x and beyond while retaining usable image quality — something that was previously only possible with a dedicated telephoto lens. For buyers who shoot events, wildlife, or sports, this flexibility justifies the S25 Ultra over competing flagships at the same clearance price.

Samsung DeX support on the S25 Ultra is a final differentiator worth noting for productivity-focused buyers. Connecting the S25 Ultra to a monitor via USB-C delivers a full desktop experience — resizable windows, keyboard and mouse support, and access to full desktop-class apps. For buyers who want to reduce their device count, the S25 Ultra at $749-899 Prime Day pricing effectively replaces both a smartphone and a light productivity computer when paired with a monitor and keyboard. At clearance pricing, that value proposition is difficult to match anywhere in the mobile market.

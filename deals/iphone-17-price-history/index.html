⚠️ Why This Isn't a Normal "Wait for a Deal" Situation

Apple CEO Tim Cook told the Wall Street Journal that Apple can no longer absorb rising memory and storage chip costs, calling the shortage a "hundred-year flood" — the worst he's seen in 40 years. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports the hikes are "imminent" and not tied to Apple's usual fall refresh cycle, meaning they could hit current models like the iPhone 17 at any time, not just at a future launch.

What's New on the iPhone 17

The iPhone 17 gains a 120Hz ProMotion display for the first time on a base model — previously a Pro-only feature. It also adds a new 18MP Center Stage front camera with a square sensor for wider group selfies, a 48MP ultra-wide rear camera, and Ceramic Shield 2 offering 3x better scratch resistance than the previous generation. The second-generation Camera Control button returns with a refined mechanical click and capacitive touch surface.

How to Actually Save Money on an iPhone 17

Since Apple itself rarely discounts new iPhones, the real savings come through carrier trade-in and plan promotions. Verizon and T-Mobile have both offered the iPhone 17 free with an eligible trade-in on a qualifying unlimited plan. AT&T offers up to $700 off with trade-in and plan. Apple's own trade-in program offers $35-$695 credit toward a new iPhone 17 depending on your current device's condition.

💡 Should You Wait for the iPhone 18?If your current iPhone is from 2022 or newer and meets your daily needs, there's a reasonable case for skipping this upgrade — iOS 19/26 delivers similar Apple Intelligence features to older Pro models via server-assisted processing. But if you're planning to upgrade regardless, buying now avoids both the price hike risk and any supply constraints that historically accompany a new iPhone's first weeks.

What to Do Right Now

Given a sitting Apple CEO has publicly confirmed price increases are coming — not rumored, confirmed — waiting here carries real, unusual risk compared to most tech purchases. If you need or want an iPhone 17, today's $799 price is a reasonable one to lock in rather than gamble on.

Colors and Storage Options

The iPhone 17 ships in a refreshed matte-infused pastel color palette, including a new Sage green option alongside the standard lineup — a shift from the more saturated tones of previous generations. Storage starts at 256GB as the base configuration, a genuine upgrade from prior years where 128GB was still the entry point, giving most users comfortable headroom for photos, apps, and video without immediately needing to consider the 512GB option.

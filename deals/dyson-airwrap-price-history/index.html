Dyson Airwrap Complete vs Complete Long vs Special Edition — Which to Buy

Dyson sells the Airwrap in several configurations. Understanding the differences ensures you buy the right version at Prime Day pricing rather than discovering post-purchase you bought the wrong set.

Includes all attachments for long hair including 1.2-inch and 1.6-inch barrels, the Coanda smoothing dryer, round volumizing brush, firm smoothing brush, and wide-tooth comb. Designed for hair at or below shoulder length. This is the version most buyers want and the one that hits $399-449 at Prime Day. ASIN B0B2479GQN.

Airwrap Complete — same attachment count but with shorter barrel sizes optimized for hair above shoulder length. Special Edition colors — periodically Dyson releases limited-edition colorways (Vinca Blue, Strawberry Bronze, Topaz) at the same price as standard colors. Dyson Airwrap vs Shark FlexStyle — The Honest $250 Question

The Shark FlexStyle at $149-179 at Prime Day versus the Dyson Airwrap at $399-449 is the most common comparison buyers make before purchasing. Both use air-based styling — neither requires extreme heat to curl. But they are not equivalent products at a different price.

The Dyson Airwrap uses the Coanda effect — a phenomenon where high-speed air wraps around a cylindrical barrel and attracts hair to it. Hair coils around the barrel automatically with minimal manual wrapping required. The result is consistent curls or waves in significantly less time than with a traditional curling iron. The heat stays below 302°F throughout styling. The motor and airflow engineering in the Airwrap is proprietary Dyson technology that genuinely sets it apart.

The Shark FlexStyle uses a similar principle at a lower price point. It performs excellently for the price and is the right choice for buyers with a firm $150 budget. However, the curl-attachment performance, styling speed, and overall finish quality are noticeably below the Airwrap. If your budget allows the Airwrap at Prime Day pricing, the Airwrap is the better product. If $149-179 is your ceiling, the FlexStyle is the best option in that range.

Galaxy S26 Ultra Price History — The Brand New Flagship With Zero Competition

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra launched in 2026 as Samsung's definitive flagship — and it landed in a strange market position. That creates a genuinely interesting buying decision: pay $1,299 for the latest hardware, or grab the S25 Ultra at $749-902 and save $400.

What makes the S26 Ultra's price history notable right now is the timing. Samsung's own $200 promotion brings the S26 Ultra to $1,099 ahead of Prime Day — but that promotion expires June 21. Two days later, Prime Day kicks off, and based on the S25 Ultra's Prime Day 2025 performance ($365 off at Amazon), the S26 Ultra is expected to match or exceed $200 off, potentially hitting $999-1,049 for the first time.

Galaxy S26 Ultra Specs — What You Are Actually Buying

The S26 Ultra ships with a 6.9-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED display at 120Hz with a peak brightness of 2,600 nits — the brightest Samsung has ever shipped on an Ultra model. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 2 processor delivers performance improvements over the S25 Ultra's Snapdragon 8 Elite, particularly in AI processing tasks. The 12GB of RAM across all storage tiers is unchanged from the S25 Ultra.

The quad camera system leads with a 200MP main sensor, a 50MP ultrawide, a 50MP 5x telephoto, and a 10MP 3x telephoto. The S Pen is built in — the only flagship Android phone to include a built-in stylus in 2026. Battery capacity is 5,000mAh, matching the S25 Ultra, with the key upgrade being the 65W wired charging speed that can fill the phone in under 45 minutes.

Digital Trends noted after four months of testing that the S26 Ultra is still the one to beat for Android power users, while flagging the absent native magnetic charging as a notable omission at this price tier. PhoneArena gave it an 8.1/10, praising the charging upgrade and Privacy Display while noting the camera system is evolutionary rather than revolutionary versus the S25 Ultra.

What the Ninja AF101 is

The Ninja AF101 is a 4-quart air fryer. A Slickdeals post from January 7, 2026 describes a family-sized 4-quart ceramic-coated basket and lists roast, bake, broil and dehydrate alongside air frying, with a list price of $120. It is a single-basket model, so you cook one food at a time.

Ninja sells many air fryers, so read the model number off the listing. The AF101 is not the DualZone line.

What the price has looked like

Dated reports: TechRadar (December 24, 2023) reported $74.99 at Amazon against a $129.99 list price, calling it the lowest in three years and noting the model had held near $80 for three years. Slickdeals (January 7, 2026) posted $62.99 with free shipping against a $120 list price, since expired. So the prices we found sit in the low $60s to mid $70s, but list prices on the listing are not a reliable guide.

Who should look at it

  • You cook for one to three people: 4 quarts is small but fine.
  • You want a low-cost first air fryer: the AF101's deal history is in the low $60s to $70s.
  • You cook for a family: compare larger baskets or a dual-basket model.
  • You have little counter space: a 4-quart unit is easier to store.

Common mistakes when buying

  • Trusting the "was" price. $120 and $129.99 were list prices, and the deals sat far below.
  • Buying the wrong size. Check capacity against how many you feed.
  • Confusing it with the DualZone. That is a different model.
  • Assuming a deal is the lowest. We report only prices we found.

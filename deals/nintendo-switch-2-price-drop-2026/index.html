Nintendo Switch 2 Price Hike — Why You Need to Buy Before September 1

Nintendo of America officially announced that the Switch 2 MSRP will increase from $449.99 to $499.99 beginning September 1, 2026. The announcement cited "various changes in market conditions which are expected to extend over the medium to long term" as the reason. This is not a temporary promotional price — $449.99 is the final opportunity to buy the Switch 2 at its launch price. After September 1, every retailer including Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and Target will charge $499.99.

The Switch 2 launched on June 5, 2025 at $449.99 and has sold 19.86 million units as of March 2026 — making it one of the fastest-selling consoles in Nintendo history. The price hike signals that Nintendo sees sustained demand and has no need to discount. If you have been waiting for a deal, the window is closing. Should You Wait for Prime Day June 23 or Buy Now?

Prime Day June 23-26 is 12 days away. Nintendo first-party hardware almost never discounts at Prime Day — Nintendo controls its own pricing and rarely participates in Amazon sale events. The Switch 2 has not been discounted since launch. The $449.99 price you see today is the same price it launched at in June 2025.

The decision is simple: the only price event that matters is the September 1 hike. Whether you buy today or at Prime Day makes no difference to the price — $449.99 is $449.99. But buying before September 1 saves you $50 vs waiting. If you need a Switch 2, buy it before September.

Nintendo Switch 2 — What You Get for $449.99

The Switch 2 features a custom Nvidia Tegra T239 processor, 12GB RAM, a 7.9-inch 1080p 120Hz LCD display with HDR10, 256GB built-in storage, Wi-Fi 6, and backward compatibility with all Nintendo Switch games. Docked, it outputs up to 4K at 60Hz via HDMI 2.1. The new Joy-Con 2 controllers add a mouse mode for desktop-style gaming and improved haptics. Battery life is 2-6.5 hours depending on game.

At $449.99 the Switch 2 is the best value gaming console for households that want both home console and portable gaming in one device. Mario Kart World has sold 14.70 million copies — the fastest-selling game in Nintendo history — reflecting the install base momentum this console has.

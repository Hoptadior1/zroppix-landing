Cosori Air Fryer Cleaning and Maintenance

The Cosori 5.8QT basket and crisper plate are dishwasher safe, which is one of its most practical advantages over budget air fryers whose non-stick coatings degrade rapidly in the dishwasher. The non-stick coating on the Cosori Pro LE is PFOA-free and rated for hundreds of dishwasher cycles before showing wear. For best results, Cosori recommends removing food debris with a soft sponge before placing in the dishwasher, which extends coating life significantly.

The exterior of the unit wipes clean with a damp cloth. The heating element and interior cavity should be cleaned monthly with a non-abrasive brush — Cosori includes a cleaning brush in the box for this purpose. Avoid submerging the main unit in water or placing it in the dishwasher. With basic care, the Cosori 5.8QT lasts 5–7 years of regular use.

Cosori Air Fryer Power and Energy Efficiency

At 1700W, the Cosori 5.8QT uses significantly less energy than a conventional oven (2,000–5,000W) and preheats in under 3 minutes versus 10–15 minutes for a full oven. For everyday cooking tasks — reheating leftovers, cooking frozen foods, making chicken wings or vegetables — the air fryer replaces the oven almost entirely, reducing kitchen energy consumption meaningfully over a full year of use.

For context: running a conventional oven for 30 minutes at 375°F costs roughly $0.06 in electricity at the US average rate. The Cosori air fryer for the same 30 minutes at full power costs roughly $0.04. Across 300 cooking sessions per year, that is a $6 annual savings — modest but real, on top of the time savings from faster preheating and cooking cycles. The real energy win is replacing the oven entirely for single-dish meals, which eliminates the standby energy waste of heating a large oven cavity for a single portion.

What Makes the Arctis Nova Pro Worth Buying at $299.99

The SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro Wireless is a flagship gaming headset with dual wireless connectivity — it connects simultaneously to both a 2.4GHz gaming device and a Bluetooth device, so you can take a phone call without removing the headset. It also features active noise cancellation, a hot-swap battery system so you never have to pause to charge, and Hi-Fi audio tuning that The Verge specifically called the best-sounding wireless gaming headset they'd used at the time.

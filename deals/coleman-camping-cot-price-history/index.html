Coleman Cot Lineup — Which Model Is Right for You?

Coleman offers several cot models at different price points. The Trailhead II at ~$49.99 is the entry-level option — 75 inches long, 25 inches wide, supports up to 300 lbs, fits adults to 6'2". The Pack-Away Big 'n Tall at ~$79.99 adds extra width and height capacity for taller campers up to 6'6". The ComfortSmart Deluxe at ~$89.99 includes a built-in foam mattress pad for comfort that bridges the gap between a cot and a camping mattress.

For most car campers the Trailhead II at Prime Day pricing of $34-39 is exceptional value. The elevated sleeping surface alone — off cold, damp ground — improves sleep quality more than any other camping upgrade. See our guide on Amazon price history for more on seasonal pricing patterns.

Coleman Cot vs Air Mattress — Which Is Better for Camping?

A Coleman cot elevates you 7-8 inches off the ground — keeping you warmer by avoiding cold ground contact, improving airflow in hot weather, and making it easier to get in and out. An air mattress is softer but can puncture and deflate overnight. For car camping where weight is not a constraint, a cot consistently outperforms air mattresses for sleep quality and long-term durability.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the best time to buy a Coleman camping cot?+
The best time to buy a Coleman camping cot is Prime Day (June) or Labor Day (September) when prices drop to their annual lows. Avoid buying in late May or early June during peak camping season when prices are elevated. Prime Day June 23-26 typically delivers the deepest summer discount.
How much weight can a Coleman camping cot hold?+
The Coleman Trailhead II supports up to 300 lbs and fits adults up to 6 feet 2 inches tall. The Coleman Pack-Away Big 'n Tall version supports up to 300 lbs and fits up to 6 feet 6 inches. The ComfortSmart Deluxe also supports 300 lbs with an added foam pad for extra comfort.
Coleman camping cot vs air mattress — which is better for camping?+
A Coleman camping cot elevates you off the cold ground, improves airflow, and is more durable long-term. Air mattresses are softer but can puncture and lose air overnight. For car camping where weight is not an issue, a cot is generally the superior sleep option for adults.
Does the Coleman camping cot fit in a tent?+
The Coleman Trailhead II measures 75 inches long by 25 inches wide — fitting comfortably in a 4-person or larger tent. Two cots can fit side by side in a 6-person tent. The ComfortSmart is wider at 31 inches and requires at least a 4-person tent for single use.

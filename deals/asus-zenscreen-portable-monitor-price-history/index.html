Single-Cable Setup Is the Real Selling Point

ZenScreen's hybrid-signal technology carries both video and power over one USB-C cable, so most laptop setups need nothing more than a single cable connection — no separate power brick hunting for an outlet in a shared dorm room. That single-cable simplicity is the practical difference between a portable monitor people actually use daily and one that ends up back in a drawer.

💡 Landscape and Portrait, Not Just One Fixed OrientationThe included foldable smart case doubles as a stand, supporting both landscape and portrait orientation without a separate tripod or mount. On Windows, ASUS's DisplayWidget software auto-rotates the display when the case is repositioned — genuinely useful for reading long documents or code in portrait mode, then flipping to landscape for video or spreadsheets.

Works With More Than Just Laptops

Beyond MacBooks, Windows ultrabooks, and Chromebooks, the ZenScreen also connects to USB-C phones and gaming handhelds like the Steam Deck — a flexible second screen that isn't locked to one device type, useful for a dorm room where a phone, laptop, and handheld console might all want screen real estate at different times.

✅ Who Should Buy ThisThis makes sense for anyone working or studying at a small dorm or apartment desk who wants real dual-monitor productivity without a permanent second display taking up desk space — it folds flat and slides into a backpack when not in use. If you need a large, stationary monitor for a fixed desk setup, a traditional desktop monitor delivers more screen for less money per square inch.

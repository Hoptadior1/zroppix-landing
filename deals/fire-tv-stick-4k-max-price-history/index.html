What changed since the July sale

The $34.99 promo that ran through July 31, 2026 has ended. Amazon has since raised list prices on many of its own devices, which PCWorld attributed to a global shortage of memory and storage in an August 27, 2026 report. On October 6, 2026, AFTVnews reported the 4K Max at $44.99, its best price since the regular price rose to $84.99. It also reported the 4K Max with a Luna Controller at $79.98, $5 less than buying the two separately, and the 4K Plus at $37.99 (Amazon will discontinue it once supply runs out).

That was during Amazon's October event, which has ended, so we can't tell you whether $44.99 still applies. The 4K Max has 16GB of storage, 2GB of RAM and Wi-Fi 6E, according to AFTVnews. The next big date is Black Friday on November 27, 2026, but we can't say what will be discounted.

Which Fire TV Stick should you buy?

  • You have a 4K TV and stream a lot: the 4K Max. The extra storage and faster Wi-Fi help most if your router supports Wi-Fi 6E.
  • You have a 4K TV and want to spend less: compare the 4K Select, and the 4K Plus if you can still find it new.
  • Your TV is 1080p: the Fire TV Stick HD is enough, and a 4K stick adds nothing.
  • You want a box with more hardware: look at the Fire TV Cube, but check the price gap first.

Whichever you choose, you need a free HDMI port, and a 4K stick only shows 4K on a 4K TV.

Want your home sports and shows while abroad? Check VPN support before you buy

A VPN is optional, but it is the usual way people watch their home-country sports and streaming apps while travelling or living abroad: it sends your traffic through a server in another country. Two honest caveats. No VPN can promise access to a given service, because services change their blocking and some of their terms restrict VPN use. And on a Fire TV Stick the operating system matters.

  • Vega OS sticks: Amazon says starting with the Fire TV Stick 4K Select, all future Fire TV Sticks run Vega, with no Android apps and no sideloading (AFTVnews, Apr 17, 2026). A VPN app may not be available on those, so check before you buy.
  • PureVPN on Fire TV: PureVPN's own setup guide says you install its app from the Fire TV app store, search for PureVPN, and sign in by scanning a QR code (PureVPN guide, Feb 13, 2025). That guide predates Vega, so confirm your exact stick is supported on PureVPN's site.

Common mistakes when buying a Fire TV Stick

  • Buying a 4K stick for a 1080p TV.
  • Paying for Wi-Fi 6E without a 6E router. The benefit needs both.
  • Mixing up models. Max, Plus, Select and HD listings look alike. Read the title.
  • Trusting an old sale. Promo codes expire, so check the final price at checkout.
  • Ignoring bundles. A bundle price may beat the stick alone, so compare the total.

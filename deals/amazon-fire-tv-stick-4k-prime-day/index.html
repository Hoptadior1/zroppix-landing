Fire TV Stick 4K Max vs Fire TV Stick 4K Plus — Which to Buy

The 4K Max adds Wi-Fi 6E for faster wireless speeds and doubles storage to 16GB compared to base models — worth it if you have a Wi-Fi 6E-capable router and stream 4K content frequently. During the same current sale window, the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus has also been confirmed as low as $19.99. If you have an older router or primarily watch 1080p content, the 4K Plus at that price is the better value; the small price gap to the Max is worth paying if you have the faster router to actually take advantage of Wi-Fi 6E.

Both the 4K Max and 4K Plus support Dolby Vision, HDR10+, and Dolby Atmos audio — the picture and sound quality ceiling is the same across both models. The real differentiators are Wi-Fi speed (6E vs 6) and storage (16GB vs less), not video quality.

📊 A Genuine Best-Seller, Not a One-Off Discount

MATEIN's own site cites over 80,000 five-star reviews for its backpack line across all sales platforms — this isn't a newly launched product riding a temporary promotion, it's an established, heavily-reviewed best-seller sitting at a stable, everyday price.

The USB Port Is the Actual Differentiator

The built-in internal USB charging cable routes to an external port on the outside of the bag, so a power bank stored inside can charge a phone without digging through the main compartment. Multiple owner reviews specifically call out not realizing this feature existed until using the bag — a small, genuinely convenient touch rather than a marketing checkbox.

💡 The Anti-Theft Pocket Actually WorksThe hidden back pocket sits directly against the wearer's back, making it genuinely difficult to access without the bag being removed first — useful for a passport, phone, or wallet in crowded classrooms, transit, or airports. This is a real functional feature, not just marketing language.

TSA-Friendly Matters More Than It Sounds

The 17-inch version's laptop compartment opens 90-180 degrees flat, letting it lay open for airport security screening without needing to fully unpack the bag — a real convenience for students flying home for breaks who don't want a laptop-specific bin delay every time.

Built for Four Years, Not One Semester

Water-resistant polyester fabric with metal zippers, padded ventilated back support, and a luggage strap for stacking on a rolling suitcase handle round out a design clearly built around durability across repeated daily use — not a bag meant to be replaced after a single school year.

✅ Who Should Buy ThisThis is a strong, low-risk pick for daily college carry, travel, or commuting — the review volume and lifetime defect warranty both point to a genuinely reliable product rather than a disposable budget bag. If you need a more rugged, hiking-oriented pack for outdoor use specifically, a dedicated outdoor brand backpack is the better fit.

What to Do Right Now

At $39.99, this is stable, everyday pricing for a well-reviewed, feature-rich backpack — there's no discount pattern to wait on, so ordering now ahead of move-in avoids the risk of popular colors selling out closer to August.

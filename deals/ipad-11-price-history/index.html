iPad 11th Gen Price History — Apple's Best Value Tablet in 2026

The Apple iPad 11th generation launched on March 12, 2025 at $349 for the 128GB Wi-Fi model. Within 47 days it had already hit $277.78 at Amazon — setting the all-time low. By October 2025 Prime Big Deal Days it was at $279, and Black Friday 2025 matched that at $279. Right now at $299 it is $20 above those record prices, and with Prime Day June 23-26 just 11 days away, the pattern is clear: waiting delivers the best iPad price of the year at Prime Day.

The iPad 11 represents a genuine generational upgrade over the iPad 10. It adds the A16 chip — the same processor found in the iPhone 14 Pro — which delivers meaningfully faster performance for demanding apps, games, and AI features in iPadOS 26. Base storage doubles to 128GB, and a new 512GB option appears for power users. The design is identical to the iPad 10, which means all existing cases, keyboards, and accessories remain compatible.

iPad 11 Full Specifications — What You Get for $299

The iPad 11 packs an 11-inch Liquid Retina IPS display at 2360x1640 resolution — sharp enough for reading, streaming, and drawing. The A16 chip delivers six CPU cores and five GPU cores, handling everything from 4K video editing to demanding games without frame drops. The 12MP ultrawide front camera with Center Stage automatically frames you during video calls. A 12MP rear camera handles document scanning and casual photography. USB-C supports fast charging and direct file transfer. All-day battery life consistently delivers 10 hours in Apple's own testing and 8-9 hours in real-world use.

The iPad 11 works with iPadOS 26, Apple Intelligence, and the full suite of Apple ecosystem features including Handoff, Sidecar for use as a secondary Mac display, and AirDrop. At $279-299 at Prime Day it is not just the best tablet under $300 — it is the best tablet under $400 for anyone who doesn't specifically need the M-series chip found in the iPad Air or iPad Pro.

iPad 11 vs iPad Air — Which Should You Buy at Prime Day?

The iPad Air adds the M2 chip (entry Air) or M3 chip (higher configuration), a landscape front camera, thinner design, and support for the Apple Pencil Pro. At Prime Day pricing the Air starts around $499-549 — roughly $200 more than the iPad 11. For most users the iPad 11 at $279-299 does everything they need. The Air's advantage is raw CPU performance for video editors, musicians using complex synth apps, or anyone running demanding iPad-specific productivity software. For streaming, browsing, note-taking, light gaming, and video calls, the iPad 11 is the right choice.

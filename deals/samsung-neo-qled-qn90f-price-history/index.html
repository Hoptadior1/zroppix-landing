What Is the Samsung Neo QLED QN90F

The QN90F is Samsung's 2025-generation flagship Neo QLED — a Mini LED-backlit 4K TV positioned just below Samsung's true OLED lineup. It runs on the NQ4 AI Gen3 processor with AI-driven 4K upscaling that sharpens lower-resolution content across streaming, live sports, and gaming. The standout feature is its glare-free display technology, which meaningfully cuts reflections in bright rooms — a genuine advantage over standard glossy panels for anyone with a sunlit living room.

For gaming, the QN90F supports 165Hz Motion Xcelerator with 4K VRR, delivering tear-free performance for both PC and console gaming. Audio comes via Object Tracking Sound+ with Dolby Atmos support. It's currently rated 4.7 stars across 717+ reviews at major retailers, with reviewers specifically praising contrast performance that reviewers describe as "incredibly close to OLED levels" for a Mini LED panel.

💡 Neo QLED vs True OLED — Know the Trade-OffThe QN90F uses Mini LED backlighting (branded Neo QLED), not true OLED. Reviewers consistently note its contrast comes remarkably close to OLED-level black levels, while typically delivering higher peak brightness than true OLED panels — genuinely useful in bright rooms where OLED can struggle. If deepest possible blacks and viewing-angle performance matter more than brightness, Samsung's true OLED lineup (like the S95F) is the alternative to compare against directly.

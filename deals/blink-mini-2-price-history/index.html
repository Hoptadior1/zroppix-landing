What You Get for Under $20

The Mini 2 is Blink's second-generation plug-in indoor camera, delivering 1080p HD live view with a wider field of view than the original Mini, plus color night vision powered by a built-in spotlight that activates automatically in low light. Motion detection sends real-time alerts to your phone, and two-way audio lets you speak to whoever's in frame directly from the Blink app.

💡 Amazon Is Selling This Near Cost, and That's the PointAt $19.99, Amazon is leaving very little hardware margin on a device it manufactures under its own Blink brand. The strategic logic mirrors Ring's aggressive pricing: every Blink camera in a home deepens Alexa integration and ecosystem lock-in, and Amazon is willing to subsidize the hardware to get there. For buyers, that translates into genuinely capable current-generation hardware at a price where multi-camera setups become an easy decision rather than a big spend.

Indoor by Default, Outdoor With One Add-On

The Mini 2 is designed as a plug-in indoor camera out of the box, but Blink sells a Weather Resistant Power Adapter separately that extends it to outdoor use — front porch, garage, or garden monitoring — without buying a dedicated outdoor camera model. The included mounting kit handles wall, shelf, or flat-surface placement either way, and setup takes just a few minutes through the Blink app.

Do You Need the Subscription?

No subscription is required for basic live view and motion alerts. A Blink Subscription Plan, which includes a free 30-day trial, unlocks person detection with on-device computer vision, cloud clip storage, and continuous live view beyond a few seconds at a time. Alternatively, a Sync Module 2 with a USB drive keeps footage stored locally at no ongoing cost — a real option for anyone who wants to avoid subscriptions entirely.

✅ Who Should Buy ThisAt this price, buying two or three units to cover multiple rooms, floors, or entry points is a decision that barely requires deliberation — a three-camera setup runs about $60, less than a single premium security camera at standard retail pricing. If you need serious outdoor perimeter coverage with advanced AI features, look at Ring's or Blink's higher-end outdoor models instead.

What to Do Right Now

At $19.99, you're already close to the realistic floor outside of Prime Day's one-off $12 flash pricing — there's little reason to wait for a marginal improvement that isn't guaranteed to reappear before the next major sale event. If you want to build out multiple cameras, check for 2-pack and 3-pack bundle pricing, which sometimes brings the per-camera cost down further.

Setup Tips

Position the camera to avoid pointing directly at reflective surfaces or windows, since the built-in spotlight's color night vision can wash out footage with strong reflections. If using multiple units, name each one by room in the Blink app immediately during setup to avoid confusion later.

Why Ring Devices Are Some of the Most Predictably Discounted Products at Prime Day

Amazon acquired Ring in 2018 for $1.2 billion and has since used Ring doorbells and cameras as loss-leader products at Prime Day — selling the hardware at or below cost to drive Ring Protect subscription sign-ups. A Ring doorbell with no subscription still provides live view, motion alerts, and two-way audio. Adding the Ring Protect plan at $3.99-10/month unlocks video history, person detection, and package alerts. Amazon's long-term revenue comes from subscriptions, which is why the hardware gets dramatic discounts at Prime Day.

The Ring Video Doorbell (2nd Gen and newer) has dropped to $54.99 at its lowest point — a genuinely remarkable price for a device that replaces a traditional wired doorbell with a camera, two-way audio, and real-time motion alerts. At Prime Day pricing of $59-79 it is one of the most obvious home security purchases available.

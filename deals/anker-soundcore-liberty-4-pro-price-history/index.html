What Makes the Liberty 4 Pro Stand Out

The Liberty 4 Pro's headline feature is its 7-sensor adaptive noise cancellation — 6 sound sensors plus 1 barometric pressure sensor working together to capture ambient noise from every direction, recalibrating every 0.3 seconds to the changing environment. Anker claims roughly 3x stronger noise reduction compared to the previous Liberty 4 NC generation.

For sound quality, the earbuds use an enhanced ACAA acoustic architecture with a 10.5mm bass driver and titanium-coated tweeter, supporting LDAC Hi-Res audio for compatible Android devices — a genuinely premium audio codec typically found on earbuds costing significantly more. The distinctive charging case features a small built-in touch-bar display visible through the case lid, letting users adjust ANC levels without opening the case or reaching for their phone.

💡 Real-World Battery PerformanceIndependent testing by SoundGuys measured 5 hours and 42 minutes of real-world battery life with ANC enabled — slightly below Anker's stated 7.5-hour rating but still solid for daily use, especially combined with the case's total 30-hour capacity with ANC active. The 5-minute quick-charge feature delivering roughly 4 hours of playtime is a genuinely useful feature for anyone who forgets to charge overnight.

Liberty 4 Pro vs AirPods Pro — Is It a Real Alternative

At $109.99, the Liberty 4 Pro costs less than half of AirPods Pro's typical price while offering comparable adaptive ANC and genuinely premium audio hardware. For Android users specifically, it has a real edge — LDAC Hi-Res audio support that AirPods Pro simply doesn't offer, since that's an Apple-exclusive ecosystem product. iPhone users lose access to LDAC but retain full ANC, calling, and app functionality via standard Bluetooth. The trade-off is ecosystem integration — AirPods Pro's seamless auto-switching between Apple devices and deeper Siri integration isn't matched here, but for anyone not fully invested in the Apple ecosystem, the Liberty 4 Pro is a legitimate, far cheaper alternative rather than a budget compromise.

Fit and Comfort Notes

The Liberty 4 Pro ships with multiple ear tip sizes, and Anker's Soundcore app includes a built-in Fit Test that checks seal quality per ear — worth running during setup since a poor seal directly undermines both noise cancellation performance and bass response. Reviewers have noted the case's reflective glossy finish (available in blue and black) is a fingerprint magnet, while the matte white variant avoids this but doesn't allow the display to show through the closed lid.

Software and App Experience

The Soundcore companion app extends functionality well beyond basic pairing — adjustable EQ presets, ANC level customization via the case's touch bar or in-app slider, and firmware updates all run through it. Multipoint Bluetooth connectivity lets the earbuds stay paired to two devices simultaneously, such as a phone and laptop, switching seamlessly between audio sources without needing to manually disconnect and reconnect — a genuinely convenient feature for anyone alternating between work and personal device audio throughout the day.

Call Quality and Daily Use

With 6 microphones and an AI-enhanced noise reduction algorithm, the Liberty 4 Pro is specifically engineered for clear calls in noisy environments — wind, traffic, or crowded spaces — using a wind-noise reduction algorithm to keep voice audio intelligible without the muffling that affects many budget earbuds' call quality. The earbuds also support IPX5 water resistance, covering sweat and light rain exposure, making them a reasonable choice for gym use or outdoor exercise alongside general daily listening.

Warranty and Long-Term Value

Anker backs the Liberty 4 Pro with an 18-month warranty, longer than the standard 12-month coverage many competing earbud brands offer, reflecting the company's broader reputation for reliability across its charging and audio product lines.

How to check a home price in 5 steps

  1. Check the exact model name. Robot vacuums and purifiers have near-identical names.
  2. Check the condition. New, renewed and reconditioned units are priced very differently.
  3. Check the size or capacity for fans, purifiers and cooling units.
  4. Open the price history for that model before you pay.
  5. Set an alert if the price isn't right yet.

How to read the prices on this page

Cards with a price show a report from a named source on a specific date. That is different from a live price, and it's why the source and date sit under each one. Cards without a price link to Amazon, where you can see today's price, and some also to a price page.

  • Reported means a deal site or publication posted that price on that date. Many such deals have expired.
  • List or regular price is the seller's reference price. A badge on Amazon compares against a reference price too, so check what it's compared with.
  • We are rebuilding the remaining price pages in batches to the same standard, so more cards will gain a dated price over time.

Questions about home deals

Are these home prices live?

No. Every price on this page is a dated report from a named source. Open the Amazon listing for today's price.

Is a reconditioned vacuum a good deal?

It can be, but compare it with new prices and check the warranty and the return window.

Will home appliances be cheaper on Black Friday?

We can't predict that. Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Check the price history and set an alert.

What should I check on an air purifier?

Check the room size it covers and the cost of replacement filters, as well as the sticker price.

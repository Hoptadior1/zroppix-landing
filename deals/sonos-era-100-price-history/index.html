Sonos Era 100 Price History — What's Confirmed

The Sonos Era 100 launched in 2023, replacing the Sonos One as Sonos's entry-level premium speaker, with a $219 regular price (not $249 — that figure doesn't match current sourcing). During Amazon Prime Day 2026 (June 23-26), the Era 100 dropped to $179 — a confirmed $40 discount, reported the same day by Gizmodo and separately confirmed by 9to5Toys.

Sonos also introduced a newer, lower-cost variant in 2026: the Era 100 SL, listing at $189 and confirmed at $159 during the same Prime Day event — $30 cheaper than the standard Era 100 at every price point. If you're comparing models, check what "SL" changes (Sonos markets it as a distinct SKU) before assuming it's identical hardware at a lower price.

What Makes the Sonos Era 100 Different from a Bluetooth Speaker

The Sonos Era 100 is a networked smart speaker built for permanent room placement and multi-room systems, not a portable Bluetooth speaker. It delivers stereo sound from a single unit — two tweeters and one mid-woofer driven by separate amplifiers create a left-right stereo image wider than the speaker's physical footprint. Gizmodo's coverage notes the Era 100 has a midwoofer roughly 25% larger than its predecessor, the Sonos One.

💡 Sonos Stereo PairTwo Sonos Era 100 speakers can be paired as a true stereo pair in the Sonos app, giving genuine left-right channel separation from two physically separate units — useful for living rooms or larger spaces where a single speaker's built-in stereo effect isn't enough.

The Era 100 added two features missing from the original Sonos One: Bluetooth 5.0 for direct device playback without Wi-Fi, and a USB-C line-in port for connecting turntables, CD players, or other analog sources. Trueplay uses your phone's microphone to calibrate the speaker's EQ automatically for the acoustics of the room it's placed in.

Setting Up Sonos for the First Time

Sonos app setup takes about 10 minutes: download the app, connect to Wi-Fi, name the room, add your streaming services, and run Trueplay calibration. Sonos supports Spotify Connect, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Tidal, and many other services connecting directly through the Sonos app rather than casting from your phone — playback continues even if your phone leaves the room.

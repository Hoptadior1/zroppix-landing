DJI Neo — The Palm-Launch Drone Built for Quick Content

The DJI Neo launched at $199 and has been discounted at Amazon's major sale events since. The confirmed all-time low is $139, reached during Prime Day 2026 and independently confirmed by multiple outlets — a genuine 30% cut, not an inflated comparison. At roughly 135 grams, it's well under the FAA's 250-gram recreational registration threshold.

Worth knowing before you buy: DJI has released a newer DJI Neo 2. This original Neo remains a legitimate budget pick, especially at or near its confirmed low, but check current Neo 2 pricing too — the gap between the two may make the newer model the smarter buy depending on the moment.

What Does the DJI Neo Actually Do?

The Neo's core trick is palm takeoff — no remote required, it launches from your hand and can fly autonomous shot patterns (orbit, dronie, rocket, and similar) using AI subject tracking. It shoots up to 4K 30fps via a 1/2-inch 12-megapixel sensor with software-based Rocksteady stabilization, and can be controlled via smartphone, the RC-N3 controller, or DJI Goggles for FPV-style flying. Propeller guards make it genuinely safe to fly indoors and near people, which most drones in this category can't claim.

The two most commonly cited drawbacks across reviews: no obstacle avoidance, and a short ~17-minute battery life per charge. The Fly More Combo (extra batteries + charging hub) addresses the battery limitation directly and is the more sensible purchase for anyone planning to fly more than the occasional quick clip.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the lowest price the DJI Neo has been?+
The DJI Neo's confirmed all-time low is $139, reached during Prime Day 2026 (June 23-26), independently confirmed by multiple outlets. The drone also sold for $159 at another point during the same event.
Do I need to register the DJI Neo with the FAA?+
No. The DJI Neo weighs about 135 grams, well under the FAA's 250-gram threshold requiring registration for recreational use. Flying over people, near airports, or in controlled airspace still requires following FAA guidelines regardless of weight.
Is there a newer DJI Neo 2?+
Yes. DJI has released a DJI Neo 2, a newer generation of this drone. If you're comparing options, it's worth checking current pricing and specs on the Neo 2 as well before deciding — it may be a better value depending on the price gap at the time you're buying.
How long does the DJI Neo battery last?+
The DJI Neo has roughly a 17-minute battery life per charge — a commonly cited limitation in reviews. The Fly More Combo with additional batteries and a charging hub extends total flight time significantly for regular use.

Which Shark IQ are you looking at?

This is the single most useful thing to know before you shop: "Shark IQ" is a family of models, not one robot. Shark's own product page lists the RV1000S as the "Shark IQ Robot Vacuum with Self-Empty Base, Self-Cleaning Brushroll, Advanced Navigation", with IQ Navigation total home mapping and a one-year limited warranty. Other listings use different model numbers, such as the RV1001AE and RV1002AE, and retailers sell them in different bundles. When we checked Shark's page on October 16, 2026 it showed a list price of $429.99 and the item as out of stock, so the retailer listing you see may differ.

The model number decides what you get and what a fair price is. Always read it off the listing before comparing any two prices.

How the Shark lineup differs

  • Shark IQ. The self-emptying bagless-base line. Tom's Guide tested the self-emptying IQ and noted a back-and-forth cleaning pattern and room mapping through the SharkClean app, with Alexa and Google Assistant support.
  • Shark Matrix IQ. Hip2Save describes Precision Home Mapping with 360-degree LiDAR, object avoidance, no-go zones and scheduling. Its write-up does not mention a self-empty base.
  • Shark PowerDetect. Android Authority describes a 2-in-1 vacuum and mop whose dock empties the bin, washes the mop and refills water, and holds up to 60 days of debris.

Shark's navigation and bin details vary by model, so check the listing for the points you care about: LiDAR or not, mopping or not, and how big the base is.

An honest word on cleaning performance

Tom's Guide's review (last updated October 2022) called the self-emptying Shark IQ "not the best cleaner": in its tests it picked up 77.5 percent of kitty litter, 67.5 percent of Cheerios and 66.5 percent of dog hair, and it took three test units to complete a floor map. That review is several years old and covers one model, so treat it as one data point rather than a verdict on today's lineup. It does tell you what to weigh: the self-emptying base is the main reason to buy this family, and cleaning power on heavy messes is where you should read recent reviews for the exact model.

Which one to look at first

  • You want it to empty itself and don't need mopping: start with the Shark IQ family, and match the exact model number.
  • You want LiDAR mapping and no-go zones: compare the Shark Matrix IQ, but confirm whether the listing includes a self-empty base.
  • You want vacuum and mop in one: look at the PowerDetect, and check the dock details and the model before comparing prices.
  • You have pets or cables on the floor: compare the iRobot Roomba j7+ as well, because obstacle avoidance matters more in that case.

Common mistakes when buying a Shark robot vacuum

  • Comparing prices across different model numbers. A $249 deal on one model says nothing about another.
  • Assuming every Shark has a self-empty base or mopping. They don't.
  • Trusting a "was" price. Compare against recorded price history, not the crossed-out number.
  • Buying from an unknown third-party seller. Check the warranty and the return window.
  • Forgetting running costs. Filters and brushrolls wear out, and the right part depends on the model.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the Shark IQ, Matrix IQ and PowerDetect?

Per the sources we cite: the IQ line has a self-empty base and mapping, the Matrix IQ is described with LiDAR mapping, and the PowerDetect is a vacuum and mop with a self-emptying, self-washing dock. Specs vary by model number, so check the listing.

How much does the Shark IQ cost?

Shark's own page listed the RV1000S at $429.99 when we checked on October 16, 2026. Deals reported by Slickdeals, Hip2Save and Android Authority are for other models and dates, so check the live listing.

Does the Shark IQ empty itself?

The IQ models with a self-empty base do. Shark's page for the RV1000S lists one, and Tom's Guide tested a self-emptying IQ. Check that your listing says so.

Is the Shark IQ good at cleaning?

Tom's Guide's 2022 review found lackluster performance on some tests (for example 66.5 percent of dog hair). It is one older review of one model, so read recent reviews for the exact model you'll buy.

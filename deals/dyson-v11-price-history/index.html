What the Dyson V11 is

The Dyson V11 is a cordless stick vacuum from Dyson's mid-range. Its main features are an LCD screen that shows the cleaning mode, the remaining runtime and maintenance alerts, a cleaner head that adjusts for carpet and hard floors, a removable battery and several cleaning modes. Dyson quotes up to 60 minutes of runtime, but that figure applies to the lowest-power mode. Stronger modes use the battery far faster, so check the runtime for the mode you will actually use.

The V11 is an earlier-generation model, and Dyson has since released newer lines. That makes two checks worth doing before you compare prices: confirm that the listing really is a V11 (names and variants are easy to mix up), and compare it with Dyson's current models, because a newer model at a similar price can be the better buy.

V11, V15 Detect or a Shark: which fits

  • The V11 suits most homes that want strong cordless cleaning and an on-screen runtime readout.
  • The V15 Detect adds the laser dust-reveal and a sensor that counts particles, and has stronger suction. It matters most if you have hard floors and like seeing the dust, or if you have allergies and want the feedback. Check the current price gap before paying for it.
  • A Shark cordless usually costs less and is a reasonable alternative, particularly on hard floors. Compare the exact model's runtime, bin size and whether the battery is removable.
  • Carpet: if much of your home is thick carpet, compare the cleaner heads and read independent tests, not just the spec sheet.

We haven't tested these vacuums, so use this as a way to narrow the choice and check independent reviews for the model you pick.

New or refurbished

Dyson sells certified refurbished units, and refurbished listings also appear on Amazon and other retailers. They can cost less than new, but the terms differ. Check who refurbished it, how long the warranty is compared with a new unit, what is in the box and what the return window is. If the refurbished saving is small, a new unit with the full warranty can be the better choice.

Common mistakes when buying a cordless vacuum

  • Judging by the headline runtime. The longest figure is for the lowest mode.
  • Buying the wrong variant. The tools, head and battery can differ between listings.
  • Trusting a "was" price. Compare against recorded price history instead.
  • Ignoring replacement costs. Check the price of a spare battery and filters.
  • Not checking the seller. Warranty handling can depend on who sells the unit.

Dyson quotes up to 60 minutes in its lowest-power mode. Stronger modes use the battery much faster, so check the runtime for the mode you'll use. The battery is removable on this model.

Is a refurbished V11 a good idea?

Bluetti AC200L — UPS Function for Sensitive Electronics

The AC200L includes a 20ms UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) switchover function. When grid power fails, the unit switches to battery output within 20 milliseconds — fast enough that most computers, NAS drives, and networking equipment do not detect the interruption. This makes the AC200L genuinely useful as a whole-home UPS for a home office setup: plug your router, NAS, desktop, and monitors into the AC200L and they continue running seamlessly through brief outages without the data loss or equipment restart that a typical power cut causes. The 20ms switchover is slower than dedicated UPS units (which switch in 2-4ms) but adequate for most consumer electronics. Gaming consoles and streaming devices tolerate the 20ms gap without issue.

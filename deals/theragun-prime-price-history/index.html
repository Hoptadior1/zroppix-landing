What the Theragun Prime is

The Theragun Prime is a handheld percussive massage device from Therabody. It sits in the middle of the Theragun range, between the smaller Mini and the higher-end Pro. Therabody publishes the current specifications, including amplitude, battery life, speeds and attachments, on its own site. Check those, because models and details change between generations.

Percussive massage devices are used by people looking for muscle relief after exercise. They are a comfort and recovery product, not a medical treatment.

Is the Prime the right Theragun?

  • Check the generation. The listing title should name the generation you want.
  • Prime vs Mini. The Mini is smaller and easier to travel with. The Prime is a larger device. Compare the current power, battery and attachments on Therabody's pages.
  • Prime vs Pro. The Pro is a higher-end model, so decide whether you need the extra features.
  • Compare brands. Hypervolt and other brands compete, so compare noise, battery, attachments and warranty.
  • Health note. If you have an injury, a medical condition, or nerve-related pain, ask a doctor or physiotherapist before using one.

Where to compare

Therabody sells directly, and retailers such as Amazon stock Theragun models too. Bundles, accessories and promotions can differ. Compare the full price including delivery, the seller, the warranty and the return policy. Check that the item is new and from Amazon, Therabody or an authorised seller, since popular devices attract third-party listings.

Common mistakes when buying a massage gun

  • Mixing up models and generations. Read the exact name on the listing.
  • Trusting a "was" price. Compare against recorded price history.
  • Buying an unknown seller's unit. Check the seller and warranty.
  • Overpaying for features you won't use. Compare Mini, Prime and Pro against how you'll actually use it.
  • Treating it as medical treatment. See a professional for injuries or ongoing pain.

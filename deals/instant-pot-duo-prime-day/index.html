Which Size to Buy — 6 Quart vs. 8 Quart

The 6-quart Instant Pot Duo suits most households of up to about 6 servings and is the version with the most price-tracking history and deal coverage — it's the configuration most independent deal sites reference. The 8-quart version is aimed at larger households or batch cooking, and typically carries a higher price point. Unless you're regularly cooking in bulk for a large group, the 6-quart is the more practical default.

Instant Pot Duo vs. Duo Plus

It's worth distinguishing the base Duo (covered here) from the separate Duo Plus, a 9-in-1 model that adds sterilizer and cake-maker functions the base Duo doesn't have, with its own listing and typically higher price. If you're comparing prices between reviews or deal posts, confirm which exact model — Duo, Duo Plus, or the newer Duo Evo Plus — is actually being referenced, since these are genuinely different products.

If your kitchen already has a slow cooker, rice cooker, and steamer taking up separate space, the Duo genuinely replaces all three in daily use — worth factoring into the value calculation beyond just the sticker price, since it consolidates counter space and cleanup into one appliance.

UPPAbaby Vista V3 — Why It Is Worth $999 and Why to Wait Anyway

Buying a double stroller when you have one child means you never need to buy another stroller when the second child arrives. For families planning more than one child, the Vista V3 at $749-849 plus RumbleSeat V3 at Prime Day pricing is cheaper than buying a single stroller now and a double stroller later.

For parents who walk significant distances or live in urban environments, this matters day-to-day. The All-Weather Comfort Seat with removable seasonal liner means the same stroller works comfortably in both summer and winter without purchasing additional accessories.

Galaxy S25 Battery Life and Charging at Prime Day

One of the most common questions about the Galaxy S25 is whether its 4000mAh battery is sufficient given the smaller capacity compared to the S25+ (4900mAh) and S25 Ultra (5000mAh). In real-world testing across multiple reviews, the S25 consistently delivers a full day of use for moderate users — around 6–7 hours of screen-on time. The Snapdragon 8 Elite's efficiency improvements over the Exynos chips used in previous S-series models are substantial, making the S25 noticeably better on battery than the Galaxy S23 or S24 despite a similar battery size.

Fast charging at 25W is included, though Samsung does not include a charger in the box. A compatible 25W charger brings the phone from zero to full in approximately 65 minutes. Wireless charging at 15W is also supported, as is reverse wireless charging at 4.5W — useful for topping up Galaxy Buds or a Galaxy Watch on the go.

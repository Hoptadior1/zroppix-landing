Nest Thermostat vs Nest Learning Thermostat at Prime Day

For most homeowners with a straightforward single-stage heating and cooling system, the standard Nest Thermostat at $79 is the correct buy. Does the Nest Thermostat Work Without Wi-Fi?

Yes — the Nest Thermostat functions as a basic programmable thermostat even without a Wi-Fi connection. You can adjust temperature manually on the device, and any schedules you have set continue to run. Remote control via the Google Home app and smart features like Home/Away Assist require Wi-Fi to function. If your internet goes down temporarily, your home still heats and cools on its last known schedule.

This is an important distinction versus some cheaper smart thermostats that become non-functional during internet outages. The Nest Thermostat's local operation capability makes it a reliable choice for homeowners who want smart features but cannot tolerate a thermostat that stops working when the internet is down.

Nest Thermostat and Google Home Integration

The Nest Thermostat integrates deeply with Google Home, enabling automations that go well beyond basic scheduling. For example: automatically lower the temperature when everyone leaves home based on phone location, raise the heat 30 minutes before your morning alarm, or adjust temperature based on outdoor weather conditions pulled from Google's weather data. These automations run entirely in the background without any manual intervention after initial setup.

For users with Google Home speakers or Nest Hub displays, the thermostat also appears as a controllable device in the Google Home interface — you can see and adjust the current temperature from any Google Home device in the house. Combined with voice control, this makes temperature management genuinely effortless in a Google ecosystem home.

LG C5 vs LG C4 — should I buy the newer model?+
The LG C5 adds brighter highlights (up to 1,500 nits vs 1,300 nits on C4), improved AI picture processing, and webOS 25. For new buyers the C5 at Prime Day pricing is the right choice. If you find C4 stock at $700-800 at Prime Day it remains excellent value.
Is OLED better than QLED for TV picture quality?+
OLED delivers perfect blacks and infinite contrast ratio because each pixel produces its own light and can switch off completely. QLED uses LED backlighting behind an LCD panel, which cannot achieve true black. For dark room viewing, movies, and gaming, OLED picture quality is superior. QLED can be brighter in very bright rooms.
Does the LG C5 support all major HDR formats?+
Yes — the LG C5 supports Dolby Vision, HDR10, HDR10+, and HLG. It is also NVIDIA G-SYNC Compatible and AMD FreeSync Premium certified for gaming. The 4-port HDMI 2.1 setup supports 4K 144Hz from PS5, Xbox Series X, and gaming PCs.

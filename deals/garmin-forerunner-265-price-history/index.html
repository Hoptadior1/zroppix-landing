Why the Garmin Forerunner 265 Is the Best Running Watch at Prime Day Price

The Garmin Forerunner 265 sits at the intersection of serious training analytics and everyday wearability. Unlike Apple Watch which optimizes for lifestyle features first, the Forerunner 265 is built around running — it tracks VO2 max trends, training load, recovery time, race predictor, and provides real-time coaching cues that adapt to your current fitness level. For any runner doing more than 3 runs per week, these features measurably improve training outcomes in a way a general-purpose smartwatch cannot replicate.

The AMOLED display is the Forerunner 265's most visible upgrade over previous Garmin models — maps, data, and notifications are genuinely readable in daylight. At Prime Day pricing of $299-349 it undercuts the Apple Watch Ultra significantly while delivering superior running-specific analytics and 13-day battery life versus Apple Watch's 18-36 hours.

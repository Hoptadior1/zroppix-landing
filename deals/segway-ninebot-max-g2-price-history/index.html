What the Max G2 is, according to reviewers

Tom's Guide (last updated February 11, 2024) gave it its Editor's Choice. It reported a 1,000W rear motor, a 36V/15.3Ah battery (about 551 Wh by our arithmetic), a front hydraulic shock with rear springs, turn signals, built-in Apple Find My support and a weight of 53.5 lbs. Segway's range claim is up to 43 miles, and the company's own estimate is 28 to 43 miles depending on rider size, style and weather. Tom's Guide's test came in at the lower end. Its main complaint was weight and size.

The listing titles on Slickdeals describe it as 43 miles, 22 mph with dual suspension. Treat those as maker figures, and check the current listing and Segway's page.

Before you ride

  • Check local rules. Laws on e-scooters, speed limits, bike lanes and helmets vary by city and state.
  • Look for safety certification. Check that the listing states the battery and electrical system are certified (UL 2272 is the common standard in the US).
  • Plan for weight. At 53.5 lbs it is heavy to carry upstairs.
  • Charge safely. Use the supplied charger, and follow Segway's manual and the warranty terms.

To price it against your commute, take your round-trip cost today (fare, parking or fuel), multiply by the days you would ride each year, and compare it with the scooter's price. We don't give a payback figure because it depends on your numbers.

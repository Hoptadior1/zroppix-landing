Why the M5 Launch Is the Best Thing That Could Have Happened to M4 Buyers

When Apple launches a new MacBook Air generation, the previous model becomes what retailers call a clearance item — inventory that needs to move to make room for the new product. Amazon is highly motivated to sell through M4 stock before it becomes irrelevant. This creates a dynamic where Prime Day M4 discounts will almost certainly be deeper than any previous MacBook Air Prime Day deal.

The precedent: the MacBook Air M3 hit $849 at Prime Day 2025 — its all-time low. That was when the M3 was still the current-gen model. The M4, now a generation behind, is expected to match or beat that price. Some Amazon watchers predict a sub-$849 price is possible for the first time in MacBook Air history.

The M4 chip is exceptional — Apple's Neural Engine performance is class-leading, the 18-hour battery life still outpaces every Windows competitor, and the fanless design means no thermal throttling during sustained workloads. None of the M5's improvements make the M4 obsolete for the average user. If you need a MacBook for university, remote work, or creative tasks, the M4 at Prime Day pricing is one of the best laptop purchases available at any price point.

Who Should Buy the MacBook Air M4 at Prime Day

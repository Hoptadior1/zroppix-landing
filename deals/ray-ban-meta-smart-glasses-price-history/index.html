Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses Price History — The AI Wearable That Actually Discounts

The Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses are the most mainstream AI wearable available in 2026 — designed to look exactly like regular Ray-Ban Wayfarers while packing a 12MP ultra-wide camera, open-ear speakers, a five-microphone array, and direct access to Meta AI. The Gen 1 launched at $299 and the Gen 2 at $379. Both generations confirmed genuine all-time lows during Prime Day 2026 (June 23-26) — Gen 1 at $224.25, beating even the prior Black Friday low, and Gen 2 at $322.15. That event has now passed, and prices have recovered to their standard levels.

Unlike most premium tech, Ray-Ban Meta glasses discount aggressively and frequently — Meta clearly prioritizes adoption over margin to build its AI wearable user base. Prime Day proved to be the single largest discount event of the year for this product, exactly as expected. With both generations having now confirmed real, deep discounts at a major sale event, Big Deal Days in October is the next realistic opportunity for similar pricing.

A New Alternative Just Launched: Meta Glasses (Non-Ray-Ban)

On June 23, 2026 — during Prime Day itself — Meta announced a separate product line called simply Meta Glasses, starting at $299. Unlike the Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta lines, these carry no Ray-Ban or Oakley branding, though they're still built in partnership with Ray-Ban's parent company EssilorLuxottica. This is a genuinely new, distinct product from what's covered on this page — worth knowing about if you're comparing the full range of Meta's wearable lineup, though the Ray-Ban Meta glasses covered here remain the more established, widely-reviewed option with a longer track record.

Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses — What Can They Actually Do?

The Ray-Ban Meta glasses look indistinguishable from regular Ray-Bans — that is the entire point. Inside the classic Wayfarer frame is a 12MP ultra-wide camera for hands-free photos and video, open-ear speakers for music and calls that nobody nearby can hear, and Meta AI accessible by voice saying "Hey Meta." The AI can answer questions about what you are looking at, translate text in real time, identify objects, and provide directions — all without touching your phone.

For content creators and streamers the hands-free camera is the headline feature — capturing first-person perspective video during activities where holding a phone is impractical. For commuters and daily users the open-ear audio and Meta AI integration replace earbuds without the isolation. The battery life is 4 hours of active use on Gen 1, or 8 hours on the Gen 2 — enough for a full day of intermittent use.

Ray-Ban Meta Gen 1 vs Gen 2 — Which Should You Buy

The Gen 2 adds significantly better photo and video quality (12MP with improved low-light vs 5MP on Gen 1), longer battery life (8 hours vs 4 hours), and improved open-ear speaker audio. The Gen 1 at its confirmed $224.25 low is the right choice for buyers who want to try AI glasses without fully committing. The Gen 2 at its confirmed $322.15 low is the right choice for content creators who want the best camera quality.

Both generations receive the same Meta AI software updates — the Gen 1 is not abandoned hardware. For most buyers the Gen 2 is worth the roughly $100 premium over Gen 1 for the battery life improvement alone. An 8-hour battery covers a full day comfortably; a 4-hour battery requires mid-day charging.

Do Ray-Ban Meta glasses work without a phone?+
Ray-Ban Meta glasses can play music, take photos, and access Meta AI without a phone nearby once set up. However the Meta app on your iPhone or Android is required for initial setup, transferring photos and videos, and some AI features that require internet connectivity. For most use cases a phone in your bag or pocket is sufficient — you do not need to hold it.
Ray-Ban Meta Gen 1 vs Gen 2 — is the upgrade worth it?+
The Gen 2 adds a significantly better camera (12MP ultra-wide vs 5MP on Gen 1), double the battery life (8 hours vs 4 hours), and improved speaker audio quality. At their respective all-time lows of $224 (Gen 1) vs $322 (Gen 2), the roughly $100 premium is worth it primarily for the battery life improvement and camera upgrade if you plan to record video.
Are Ray-Ban Meta glasses prescription compatible?+
Yes — Ray-Ban offers prescription lens options for the Meta smart glasses through its website and participating opticians. Standard clear lenses, shaded lenses, transition lenses, and polarised lenses are available in prescription variants. Prescription pricing adds $100-200 to the base price depending on the lens type and prescription strength.

Yes. On June 23, 2026, Meta announced a separate product line called simply "Meta Glasses," starting at $299, built with EssilorLuxottica but without Ray-Ban or Oakley branding. This is a distinct product from the Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta lines covered on this page, aimed at a lower price point within Meta's wearable lineup.

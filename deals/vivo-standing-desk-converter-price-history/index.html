Which VIVO converter is it?

VIVO sells several converters, and model numbers matter. Wood Art Supply's listing for the DESK-V026KB (black) gives a 25.6 x 15.7 inch desktop, a 25.4 x 11.8 inch keyboard tray that rises with the desktop, a lift range of 4.5 to 16.9 inches, a 33 lb desktop capacity and 4.4 lb tray capacity, a steel frame, touch-operated height locking, a 3-year warranty and $119.99. Connection lists a different K Series number, the DESK-V000K, with a 33 lb capacity and a 44 lb shipping weight.

Check the model number against your monitor size and desk before buying.

What we check before showing an offer

  • The page must show a date, and the date must be within the last 30 days. Older reports are listed as rejected, with their age.
  • A coupon needs an expiry date. No expiry, no coupon.
  • Every offer links to the page it came from, and that page must have appeared in our search. Nothing is made up.
  • A second check confirms it's the same model and variant you chose.
  • We never show predictions, countdowns or pressure to buy.

How it works

  1. Type a product. We suggest the closest matches and you pick the right one. This is free.
  2. Use a check. We search and show what passed our rules, and what didn't.
  3. Open the source and check the live price yourself before you buy.

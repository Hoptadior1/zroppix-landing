Amazon-owned devices — Echo, Kindle, Fire TV, Ring, Blink — have historically seen some of the deepest percentage discounts at major sale events, since Amazon uses them to drive Prime membership and ecosystem adoption. That's a general pattern, not a guarantee for any specific model at any specific moment — check the individual product's price history page for sourced numbers.

Apple products — AirPods, iPad, MacBook, Apple Watch — see real but generally smaller percentage discounts than Amazon's own devices. Apple maintains tighter pricing control with retailers. See our dedicated AirPods buying guide for verified, sourced 2026 pricing.

TVs are a category that has historically seen some of the largest dollar-amount discounts around major sale events, particularly OLED models from LG and Samsung. Dollar savings can be substantial on higher-end models — check the specific model's price history rather than assuming a blanket percentage.

Headphones and premium audio — brands like Sony and Bose have historically discounted meaningfully around major sale events. Again, check the specific model's price history page for sourced figures rather than a general claim.

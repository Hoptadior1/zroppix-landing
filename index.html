FAQ

Common questions

Is Zroppix really free?+
Yes. The free plan is free forever with no time limit and no credit card required. You get BUY or WAIT verdicts, price history, overpay score and up to 3 price alerts — all completely free.
How accurate are the predictions?+
Zroppix gives a BUY or WAIT verdict from a product's 90-day price history. These are estimates, not guarantees, and we have not published an accuracy rate. Always check the live price and history before you buy.
Which stores does it work on?+
Zroppix currently works on Amazon. We are actively adding Walmart, Best Buy, Target, Nike, Adidas and more. Pro users get access to all new stores as they launch.
Will it slow down my browser?+
No. Zroppix only activates when you click the icon on a product page. It has zero impact on browsing speed or page load times.
Is my data private?+
Yes. We only collect product prices — never your personal information, browsing history or purchase data. We never sell data to third parties. Ever.
How is Zroppix different from CamelCamelCamel?+
CamelCamelCamel shows you price history graphs and lets you set alerts. Zroppix goes much further — it uses AI to analyze the data and tell you exactly what to do. BUY or WAIT. How much you could save. When the price will drop. What others paid.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?+
Yes. Cancel anytime with one click. No questions asked. No awkward cancellation flows. You keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

How to claim

  • Keep the receipt or order number. You will need it.
  • Check the lower price live at the same store. Match the exact item: model number, colour and seller.
  • Ask inside the window, in store, by chat or by phone, depending on the store.
  • If refused, check your credit card's price protection, which some cards offer, or return and rebuy where the store allows it.
